Each plan sets how many requests a key may make a minute (counted per key and endpoint), how many calls an account may make in a UTC day, and whether the data may be used in a commercial product. The table is generated from the API spec.

Commercial use applies to the endpoints marked Data plans in the [endpoint reference](/api/endpoints).

Start the trial or buy a Data plan at [/profile/api](https://cctools.network/profile/api), where your keys are. Every endpoint, with the plans that cover it and a live Send button, is at [cctools.network/api-docs](https://cctools.network/api-docs).

| Plan | Price | Requests per minute (per key and endpoint) | Calls per day (per account) | Keys | Commercial use | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trial or Evaluation | - | 60 | 1,000 | 1 | No | A 30-day trial, once per account, or an evaluation key from our team. |
| Pro | - | 60 | 1,000 | 10 | No | Included with a CCTools Pro subscription. Self-service. |
| Data Starter | US$49 a month, US$490 a year | 120 | 20,000 | 10 | Yes | Commercial use, with attribution. Checkout in Canton Coin. |
| Data Team | US$199 a month, US$1,990 a year | 600 | No daily cap | 10 | Yes | Commercial use, with attribution. Checkout in Canton Coin. |
| Validator | - | No per-key limit | No daily cap | 10 | Yes | Canton validator operators, verified. Free for 12 months. |
| Enterprise | From US$499 a month | No per-key limit | No daily cap | 10 | Yes | Commercial use inside your product, under an agreed order. Contact us. |

<Note>
No per-key limit and No daily cap mean the plan sets none. Every key still counts against 600 requests per minute per IP address. A price marked From is where a negotiated plan starts.
</Note>
