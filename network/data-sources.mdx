CCTools displays live data from multiple sources. Understanding where data comes from helps you interpret what you see — and helps you troubleshoot if something looks off. Every section of CCTools pulls from a different upstream source, each with its own update cadence.

<Info>
  CCTools reads public blockchain data only. Your private keys and seed phrases are never requested or stored. You connect a wallet address for read access — not signing authority.
</Info>

## Portfolio and wallet data

Your portfolio balances, transaction history, and staking positions are sourced from Canton Network blockchain indexers. The indexer reads on-chain state directly and reflects the actual contents of your wallet on Canton Network. No third-party custody or wallet access is required — CCTools sees only what is publicly visible on-chain.

Portfolio data syncs when you click the **Refresh** button on the Portfolio page. Between refreshes, CCTools displays the most recently indexed snapshot.

## Market data

Token prices and liquidity pool data are sourced from Canton DEXes — Cantex, OneSwap, Send, and Tradecraft — and supplemented by market data providers for tokens that also trade on external exchanges. Prices are updated continuously as new trades occur on-chain.

The Get CC page and token pages aggregate prices across multiple venues in real time, so comparisons reflect current market conditions rather than stale snapshots.

## Network stats

The data behind the [Network Stats page](https://cctools.network/stats) — throughput, transaction volume, burn/mint ratio, validator uptime, and the CC halving countdown — comes directly from Canton Network nodes. These metrics are read from the chain itself, not from a third-party data provider, making them as close to ground truth as possible.

## Governance data

On-chain governance events, including proposals, votes, and price consensus rounds, are read directly from Canton Network. CCTools surfaces these events so you can follow governance activity without querying the chain yourself.

## Ecosystem project data

Project profiles in the CCTools ecosystem directory are maintained by the CCTools team in collaboration with project teams. AI-generated scores and summaries are recalculated periodically based on available on-chain and off-chain signals. If you represent a project and want to update your listing, contact the team via the [feedback form](https://cctools.network/feedback).

## When data looks wrong

Canton Network indexers occasionally run behind during periods of high activity or after a network event. If something looks incorrect:

1. **Click Refresh** on the Portfolio page and wait a few minutes before concluding there's an issue
2. **Check the Stats page** — if throughput or transaction volume looks unusual, the network may be under load
3. **Visit the Discord #status channel** for any known outages or indexer delays the team is tracking
4. **Submit a report** via the [feedback form](https://cctools.network/feedback) if the issue persists after 30 minutes

Most data discrepancies resolve on their own once the indexer catches up. Persistent issues are rare and are typically addressed quickly once reported.
