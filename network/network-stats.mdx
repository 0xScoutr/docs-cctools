## What the daily record is

The **Network overview**, first in the **Network** menu at [cctools.network/network](https://cctools.network/network), opens on the daily record: the public record of Canton network state, one row per UTC day, derived from Canton Scan. The page opens on the latest day, and its date field opens any other day at its own address, `cctools.network/network/YYYY-MM-DD`.

## What a day holds

The **Key figures** of a day are:

- **Super validators** and the **Voting threshold**
- **Featured app rights** and **Validator licenses**
- **Supply**, **Net supply change** and **Issuance**
- **Reference price**, the CC price the super validators vote

A day closes at 00:00 UTC. Its values are provisional until the day settles, then final, and a final value changes only through a logged revision. A value that was not recorded is empty, never zero, and its reason is listed with its day. A day's own page shows its gaps and revisions.

Canton Coin is issued every round on a schedule that steps down over time. The issuance of a day is what its rounds scheduled, and the split between app, validator and super validator rewards is read from each round, never typed by hand.

## The rest of the page

- **The network since genesis** - counts by UTC day since the network's first snapshot
- **Tiles** into every Network page, then **Network activity**, the transactions a day and the featured apps in them
- **CC burned** - Loop Data's burn series, outside the record until the record measures burn
- **Burned and scheduled issuance** - the CC burned as a share of the CC the same days scheduled
- **Next halving** - the latest round, the round the next step starts at, and what a day schedules now and after it
- **Issuance schedule** - each phase with its years, its first round, the CC it schedules a year and its split, with no maximum supply
- **Scheduled issuance** - each day's issuance split into its reward parts
- **The last seven days** and **Every day of the record**, one square per day, each opening its day
- **Fees and rounds** and **Super validators** - from the governance state CCTools reads every hour

Charts with **24H**, **7D**, **30D**, **90D** or **All** above them switch their period. A period shows only where the data covers it.

The schedule and the halving are read from the network's issuance curve and the record. They say scheduled, never minted: an unclaimed reward is not minted.

## Fees and rounds

**Fees and rounds**, at [cctools.network/network/fees](https://cctools.network/network/fees), shows what using the network costs: the traffic price a MB, the free traffic each node gets in each window, and the round length. Since CIP-0078 a transfer and a new holding carry no CC fee. The page also charts the rounds by day.

## Burned and minted

**Burned and minted**, at [cctools.network/network/supply](https://cctools.network/network/supply), sets the CC burned beside the CC minted by UTC day, and the net change in supply between them. Minted is estimated as the net change in supply plus the CC burned, for the days that hold both. **Issuance by category** shows what the rounds scheduled, which is not minted supply.

## Download and cite

The record on the page, the open files and the API are the same numbers. The files are public and need no key, under `https://api.cctools.network/api/open/network-record/v1/`:

- `daily.csv` and `daily.json` - one row per day
- `days/YYYY-MM-DD.json` - one file per day
- `gaps.csv` - the values that were not recorded, with their reasons
- `datapackage.json` - what the files hold
- `values.csv` - each value with its day, its status, the Scan host it was read from and its time
- `revisions.csv` - every change to a value after it became final
- `methodology.json` - how each value is read
- `status.json` - the latest final and provisional days

The **Cite** button beside a day's title gives a citation with the day's permalink, and the page names the license and the attribution at its foot.

Each block's menu offers **Cite**, **Download CSV**, **API** and **Ask Synchro**, where they apply. **Cite** gives a **Plain** citation that ends with the page's link, and **BibTeX** where the block has it. A block with an AI note opens on **Summary**: the note and the page's link.
