## What the daily record is

The **Daily record**, first in the **Network** menu at [cctools.network/network](https://cctools.network/network), is the public record of Canton network state: one row per UTC day, derived from Canton Scan. The page opens on the latest day, and its date field opens any other day at its own address, `cctools.network/network/YYYY-MM-DD`.

## What a day holds

The **Key figures** of a day are:

- **Super validators** and the **Voting threshold**
- **Featured app rights** and **Validator licenses**
- **Supply**, **Net supply change** and **Issuance**
- **Reference price**, the CC price the super validators vote

A day closes at 00:00 UTC. Its values are provisional until the day settles, then final, and a final value changes only through a logged revision. A value that was not recorded is empty, never zero, and its reason is listed with its day. A day's own page shows its gaps and revisions.

Canton Coin is issued every round on a schedule that steps down over time. The issuance of a day is what its rounds scheduled, and the split between app, validator and super validator rewards is read from each round, never typed by hand.

## The rest of the page

- **Issuance and app rewards** - the last 30 UTC days
- **Fees and rounds** - the transfer fee, the create fee, the holding fee and the round length
- **CC burned** - burn is not in the daily record yet, so until the record measures it this block shows Loop Data's burn series over 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days, with daily bars, and its source and time
- **Super validators** - each member's reward weight, its share of the weight and the round it joined, with the votes a proposal needs under the table
- **Featured apps** and **Governance** - the day's featured app rights, and the proposals with their approval rate
- **Data freshness** - how current each dataset on the page is

The fees, the super validators and the proposals come from the governance state CCTools reads every hour, each block with its own source and time.

## Download and cite

The record on the page, the open files and the API are the same numbers. The files are public and need no key, under `https://api.cctools.network/api/open/network-record/v1/`:

- `daily.csv` and `daily.json` - one row per day
- `days/YYYY-MM-DD.json` - one file per day
- `gaps.csv` - the values that were not recorded, with their reasons
- `datapackage.json` - what the files hold
- `values.csv` - each value with its day, its status, the Scan host it was read from and its time
- `revisions.csv` - every change to a value after it became final
- `methodology.json` - how each value is read
- `status.json` - the latest final and provisional days

The **Cite** button beside a day's title gives a citation with the day's permalink, and the page names the license and the attribution at its foot.
