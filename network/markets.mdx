**Markets**, in the top bar, holds seven pages, each with its own sources: [cctools.network/markets](https://cctools.network/markets) opens DeFi. Tables have a search, filters with a count on each option and, where it helps, a **Sort by** control, with a line that says how many rows show. **Open** opens the venue or issuer in a new tab, and a project's name opens its directory page.

Status chips read **Live** or **Planned**; **Curated**, with its date, marks a list CCTools keeps by hand; **Lagging** marks a source that is late. Each page ends with **About this page**, which names its sources and how often they are read, and a link to its Learn path.

## DeFi

**Value locked in Canton DeFi** is DefiLlama's own total for the Canton chain, with its 7-day change and a 90-day chart. Below it:

- **Protocols** - each protocol's TVL, 7-day change, share of the total, category and source, grouped by whether DefiLlama counts it: counted in the total, bridges, left out by DefiLlama, and protocols not on DefiLlama that CCTools reads itself
- **Lending rates on Alpend** - supply and borrow APY, supplied, borrowed and the share lent out, from a feed CCTools reads every 5 minutes
- **Pools** - the pools of Tradecraft, Pool Party (Send) and OneSwap, grouped by venue since each measures fees and volume its own way, with TVL, volume per day, fee and fee APR. Filter by venue and by pool size ($1,000 and up by default). CCTools does not read Cantex's pool list yet, so Cantex's TVL is DefiLlama's, under Protocols

## Tokenized assets

- **Issued on the network** (curated) - each token, how much is deposited on Temple and held in Tradecraft pools, and what it represents
- **Issuers** - each issuer's category, what you can do with it and what public data shows, grouped into price exposure without a token of the asset, live on Canton but private by design, announced with nothing public yet, and listed

## Tokens

- **Tokens on the network** - price, 24-hour change, market cap, supply on Canton and holders, filtered by kind. Supply is read every 15 minutes and CoinGecko prices every 5 minutes
- **Tokens of Canton projects, issued on other chains** - the project, the chain, the contract (with a copy button), price, 24-hour change and market cap, filtered by chain

## Stablecoins

Each stablecoin's supply on Canton, type, peg, Canton venues and status, grouped into bridged to Canton, issued on Canton, and announced but not live. Filter by type and sort by supply.

## Bridges

Each route's amount on Canton now, direction, attestation and status, with routes that are not bridges listed apart. Filter by direction (into Canton, out of Canton or both ways) and by network.

## Wallets and custody

A curated catalog in four tiers: **Wallets**, **Hardware wallets**, **Custody for institutions** and **OTC desks**. Each row shows its platforms, its custody (self-custody or custodial) and whether it asks for KYC, and you can filter by custody and by KYC.

## Get CC

- **Canton Coin today** - the price, its 24-hour change, the market cap, the 24-hour volume and the reference price, the super validators' median vote
- **Convert CC** - at a live price or your own, in your currency, then on to the [calculator](/canton-network/rewards-calculator)
- **Exchanges that list CC** (curated) - each exchange's share of CC volume, CC volume over 24 hours, price, type (CEX, DEX or Canton order book) and KYC, refreshed every 5 minutes. Filter by venue type and by KYC, and sort by volume or price. KYC is a curated snapshot: check each venue's terms before you deposit
- **On Canton itself** - the CC/USDCx pools on Canton, with Temple and Cantex beside them
- **Already hold assets on another chain?** - links to the bridge routes
