**Markets**, in the top bar, opens the overview at [cctools.network/markets](https://cctools.network/markets). Its tab row holds **Prices** (Tokens, Launchpad, Pairs and Venues) and **Sectors** (DeFi, Tokenized assets, Stablecoins, Bridges, and Wallets and custody). Tables have a search and filters. A project's name opens its directory page.

Status chips read **Live** or **Planned**; **Curated**, with its date, marks a list CCTools keeps by hand; **Lagging** marks a source that is late; **Not reviewed** marks a token CCTools has not reviewed. Each page ends with **About this page**, which names its sources and how often they are read.

## Overview

**Canton markets today** puts the token markets on one page: CC's price and supply change, market cap, volume, liquidity and markets by venue, then fear and greed, the movers over 24 hours, the most liquid tokens, launches, venues, the most traded pairs, the tokens first seen in the last 7 days, and the sectors.

Gainers and losers need $1,000 of liquidity. Launches are permissionless, not reviewed by CCTools, and kept apart from the main board and its totals.

## Fear and greed

The **Fear and greed** reading, from 0 to 100 with its band, describes CC's market mood on Canton against its own past, from market, network and news data. It is not a price signal, a forecast or advice. The overview and the cctools.network home show it with the reading a day, a week and a month before, and the component that moved it most.

**The reading and its method** opens [cctools.network/markets/fear-greed](https://cctools.network/markets/fear-greed): each component with its value, its rank against its past, its weight and its points, the reading over time, and the stories behind the news tone. A day's reading is provisional and computed again each hour until the day's last run stores it.

## Tokens

[Tokens](https://cctools.network/markets/tokens) ranks the tokens on Canton's venues with their price, 24-hour change, market cap or FDV, volume, liquidity and **Liquidity score**, from 0 to 100. Filter by kind and venue, keep **Priced only** or **Known issuer only**, set bounds on a figure, and pick the columns. A star adds a token to your favorites.

A token's page shows its price chart, where it trades (each venue with its gap from the reference price, its depth within 2% and its 24-hour volume), its supply over time and its **Ledger identity**: admin party, instrument id, decimals and registry. When another token carries the same symbol, the page says so: check the admin party before you rely on it. **Favorite**, **Alert** and, when the swap trades the token, **Swap** sit at the top, and a converter turns an amount into US dollars or another currency.

The weekly vote on a token's page takes one vote, **Up** or **Down**, from Verified members, once a week, Monday to Monday in UTC. A vote is final once cast. The card shows this week's split and last week's once they have votes.

A launch token's page adds **Token info**, its links and summary as a moderator checked them, and **Suggest info** to send its site, X, Telegram or team. Tokens of Canton projects issued on other chains have pages of their own, with the chain and the contract.

## Launchpad, pairs and venues

- **[Launchpad](https://cctools.network/markets/launchpad)** - tokens launched on a curve, still on it or graduated to their pool, priced in CC and in US dollars at CCTools' CC price, with their age, volume, trades, net flow and graduation
- **[Pairs](https://cctools.network/markets/pairs)** - every market on Canton's venues with its price, spread, depth within 2%, volume, fee and the last day's fee APR (not a forecast). A pair's page shows what a buy or a sell of a given size pays, the book, the same pair on every venue and its daily history
- **[Venues](https://cctools.network/markets/venues)** - the venues ranked by 24-hour volume. A venue's page says what it publishes (markets, prices, volume, order books or pool reserves) and lists its markets

## DeFi

**Value locked in Canton DeFi** is DefiLlama's own total for the Canton chain, with its 7-day change and a 90-day chart. Below it:

- **Protocols** - each protocol's TVL, 7-day change, share of the total, category and source, grouped by whether DefiLlama counts it: counted in the total, bridges, left out by DefiLlama, and protocols not on DefiLlama that CCTools reads itself
- **Lending rates on Alpend** - supply and borrow APY, supplied, borrowed and the share lent out, from a feed CCTools reads every 5 minutes
- **Pools** - the pools of Tradecraft, Pool Party (Send) and OneSwap, grouped by venue since each measures fees and volume its own way, with TVL, volume per day, fee and fee APR. Filter by venue and by pool size ($1,000 and up by default). CCTools does not read Cantex's pool list yet, so Cantex's TVL is DefiLlama's, under Protocols

## Tokenized assets

- **Issued on the network** (curated) - each token, how much is deposited on Temple and held in Tradecraft pools, and what it represents
- **Issuers** - each issuer's category, what you can do with it and what public data shows, grouped into price exposure without a token of the asset, live on Canton but private by design, announced with nothing public yet, and listed

## Stablecoins

Each stablecoin's supply on Canton, type, peg, Canton venues and status, grouped into bridged to Canton, issued on Canton, and announced but not live. Filter by type and sort by supply.

## Bridges

Each route's amount on Canton now, direction, attestation and status, with routes that are not bridges listed apart. Filter by direction (into Canton, out of Canton or both ways) and by network. To move USDC yourself, see [Bridge](/network/trade#bridge).

## Wallets and custody

A curated catalog in four tiers: **Wallets**, **Hardware wallets**, **Custody for institutions** and **OTC desks**. Each row shows its platforms, its custody (self-custody or custodial) and whether it asks for KYC, and you can filter by custody and by KYC.

Get CC, the swap and the bridge are in [Trade](/network/trade).
