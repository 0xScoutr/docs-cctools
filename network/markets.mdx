## DeFi pools in depth

The DeFi Pools page ([/markets/defi](https://cctools.network/markets/defi)) aggregates liquidity pool data across every major Canton DEX. For each pool you can see total value locked (TVL), 14-day trading volume, and the current annualized percentage rate (APR) — the yield you'd earn by providing liquidity.

As a reference point, here are examples of top pools and approximate metrics (data updates in real time; values below are illustrative):

| Pool | DEX | TVL | 14d Volume | APR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CC / USDCx | Cantex | ~$457K | ~$4M | ~10.42% |
| CC / USDCx | OneSwap | — | — | ~5.08% |
| CC / USDCx | Send | — | — | ~3.4–4.2% |

<Warning>
  APR figures fluctuate with pool activity and are not guaranteed. Always check the live figures on the [DeFi pools page](https://cctools.network/markets/defi) before depositing liquidity.
</Warning>

## Real-world assets (RWA)

The RWA page ([/markets/rwa](https://cctools.network/markets/rwa)) lists regulated and permissioned issuers bringing off-chain assets onto Canton Network. Current issuers include:

- **Canborsa** — perpetuals on gold and stocks
- **Fairmint** — private equity, enabling fractional ownership of private companies on-chain
- **Hecto** — a pre-IPO index product giving exposure to a basket of pre-public companies
- **Solv Protocol** — BTC-backed yield products

Each issuer page includes a project overview, asset types, and links to their Canton Network presence.

## Stablecoins

Canton stablecoins listed on CCTools include:

- **CUSD** — issued by Send in partnership with Brale, backed by frxUSD
- **USDCx** — Circle's xReserve version of USDC, bridged to Canton Network
