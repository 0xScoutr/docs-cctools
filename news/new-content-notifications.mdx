Checking every section of CCTools manually to stay current isn't practical. Notifications handle that for you — CCTools scans for new content every 30 minutes and alerts you about the categories you care about, both inside the app and optionally by email.

## What you can be notified about

You choose exactly which types of content trigger a notification. The available categories are:

| Category | What triggers it |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Ecosystem updates** | A new project is added to the Canton ecosystem directory |
| **Governance alerts** | A new governance proposal is published, or a vote result is posted |
| **Learn paths** | A new Learn Path is released on CCTools |
| **Earn & Campaigns** *(Pro)* | A new Earn opportunity or campaign goes live |
| **Canton News** *(Pro)* | New curated Canton Network articles are ingested into the News feed |
| **Community** *(Pro)* | New forum announcements and governance discussion posts are published |

Categories marked **Pro** require an active CCTools Pro subscription. All other categories are available on the free tier.

## How to enable notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your profile icon and navigate to **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle your categories">
    Each notification category has an individual toggle. Turn on the ones you want and leave the rest off. You can adjust these at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure email preferences">
    For each active category, you can also choose whether to receive an email in addition to the in-app notification. Email notifications are **on by default** — toggle them off in Settings if you prefer in-app only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How email notifications work

CCTools batches outgoing emails every 10 minutes. If multiple notification triggers fire in quick succession — for example, several new ecosystem projects added at once — you receive a single digest email covering all of them rather than a separate email for each. This prevents notification overload while ensuring you don't miss anything.

## Pro subscription and notifications

Some notification categories are only available with a Pro subscription. If you see a category greyed out in Settings, it requires Pro. You can upgrade from your account settings or the CCTools pricing page.

<Info>
  In-app notifications appear in the **notification bell** in the top navigation bar. Email notifications are sent separately on a scheduled batching cycle and may arrive a few minutes after the in-app alert.
</Info>
