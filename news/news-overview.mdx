## What News shows

**News**, in the **Ecosystem** menu at [cctools.network/news](https://cctools.network/news), lists every story about Canton that the CCTools news index holds, and the stories about projects in its ecosystem. Each headline opens the story at its source, in a new tab.

Three tabs split the list: **All stories**, **About Canton** and **Ecosystem**. A story is under Ecosystem when its headline names a directory project by its full name.

## Where the stories come from

The index reads CantonNews and the Canton Network Blog every 10 minutes, and Google News searches about once an hour.

Headlines that say the same thing within three days are one row, which reads for example "Same story from 3 sources". **Most covered in the last 7 days** shows the story the most outlets told in those days, when two or more outlets told it, and a coverage chart counts the stories of each of the last 12 weeks.

## Projects in a story

A solid chip means the headline names a project in the CCTools directory; an outlined chip, that the story's text names it, counted only for a name that is no everyday word. A match is by name, so a project whose name is also an everyday phrase can be matched by mistake: **Report a wrong match** on the page tells us. It opens Feedback, which needs level 8; below it, write to us on the [Contact](https://cctools.network/contact) page.

Forum threads are not in News: they are on the [Global Synchronizer Foundation lists](https://lists.sync.global/).
