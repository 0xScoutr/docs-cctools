The CCTools News section is your centralised source for Canton Network information. Rather than monitoring a dozen different outlets and community channels, you get everything in one place — curated news articles pulled from across the web, alongside posts submitted directly by community members. The feed refreshes every 10 minutes, so you're always looking at current information.

## News feed

The **News** tab at [cctools.network/news](https://cctools.network/news) surfaces curated Canton Network articles from multiple web sources. The pipeline pulls from Google News and RSS feeds, deduplicates stories that appear across multiple publishers, and generates a short plain-language summary for each item so you can get the key takeaway without clicking through.

Each item in the feed shows:
- **Title** and **source publication**
- **Publication date**
- **AI-generated summary** — a sentence or two covering the essentials
- **Related Canton projects** mentioned in the article (visible on the detail page)

Click any item to open its detail page with the full summary and a link to the original source.

## Forum

The **Forum** tab at [cctools.network/news/forum](https://cctools.network/news/forum) hosts posts submitted directly by CCTools community members — announcements, threads, takes, write-ups, and updates from Canton Network participants. Unlike the curated news feed, the forum is driven entirely by the community.

Forum posts go through the same moderation process as Academy submissions. Approved posts earn the submitter XP. Each post shows the author's level and links to their public profile, the same way Academy posts do.

## Filtering and searching

Both the News and Forum feeds support time filters, tag filters, and source filters to help you focus on what's relevant to you. You can also search by keyword across titles, summaries, and source names.

For full details on how to use every filter option, see [Search & Filter](/news/news-search-filter).

## Notifications

You don't have to check the News page manually to stay current. CCTools can notify you when new Canton content is published — including curated news articles and new Academy posts.

To learn how to set up and manage your notification preferences, see [Notifications](/news/new-content-notifications).
