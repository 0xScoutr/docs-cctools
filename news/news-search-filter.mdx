The CCTools news feed aggregates Canton Network coverage from many sources at once. Filters let you cut through the volume and focus on exactly what's relevant to you — whether that's the latest 24 hours of headlines or a specific topic like governance or RWA. All filters work across both the **News** tab and the **Forum** tab at [cctools.network/news](https://cctools.network/news).

## Time filters

Use time filters to limit results to articles published within a specific window:

| Filter | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Last 24 hours** | Articles published in the past day |
| **Last 7 days** | Articles from the past week |
| **Last 30 days** | Articles from the past month |
| **Last 90 days** | Articles from the past three months |

By default the feed shows recent items without a time restriction. Apply a time filter when you want to focus on breaking news or catch up after a period away.

<Tip>
  Set the **Last 24 hours** filter and check in each morning to keep a daily pulse on Canton Network developments without getting buried in older content.
</Tip>

## Tag filters

Tags reflect the topics covered in each article. Use tag filters to narrow the feed to a specific area of the Canton ecosystem, such as:

- **DeFi** — liquidity pools, yield, trading activity
- **RWA** — real-world asset tokenisation projects and news
- **Governance** — proposals, voting, protocol changes
- **Ecosystem** — new projects, partnerships, funding announcements
- **Developer** — tooling, SDKs, protocol upgrades

The available tags reflect what's actually in the feed, so the tag list updates as new content is ingested.

## Source filters

Filter by publication or community channel to see content from specific outlets you trust or follow. Source filters are especially useful if you want to focus on primary sources (such as official Canton Network announcements) or a particular media outlet covering the ecosystem.

Available sources depend on what's currently in the feed and update automatically as new sources are added to the ingestion pipeline.

## Searching

The search bar at the top of the feed matches against article titles, summaries, and source names. Typing a search term triggers a deeper fetch — instead of showing only the most recent cached items, CCTools loads the full recent archive for the active tab, so you can find older stories that would otherwise require scrolling through many pages.

## Combining filters

All filters stack. You can apply a time window, one or more tag filters, and a source filter at the same time to get a highly specific slice of the feed. For example: **Last 7 days** + **Governance** tag + a specific source gives you only governance coverage from that outlet in the past week.

To reset, clear all active filters. The feed returns to its default unfiltered view.

## Load more

The feed initially loads the most recent items. Click **Load More** at the bottom of the list to fetch the next page of results without reloading the page. The tab badge always shows the true total number of available items — not just what's currently loaded — so you always know how much more is available.
