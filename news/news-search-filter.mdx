## Search

**Search every headline** at the top of the page searches every listed story, not only the ones on screen.

## Filters

- **All projects** - pick a project to see the stories that name it
- **All outlets** - pick a source
- **Period** - the last 7, 30 or 90 days, the last 12 months, or one week of the coverage chart

Filters you pick show as chips you can remove one by one. The filters, the counts and the pages cover every listed story.

## Submitting news

News takes no submissions: the index reads its sources by itself. If a story is matched to the wrong project, use **Report a wrong match** on the page.
