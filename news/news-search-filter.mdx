## Search

The search bar at the top of the page matches against titles, summaries, and source names. Searching triggers a deeper fetch - instead of the initial cached top items, CCTools loads the full recent archive for the channel you're in so you can find older stories.

## Load More

Each feed starts with the most recent items and a **Load More** button at the bottom. Clicking it fetches the next page without reloading the page. The tab badges always show the true total in the database, not just what's currently loaded.

## Community Submissions

To submit a community post, open the Community tab and click **Submit**. Community submissions go through the same moderation queue as Academy posts and earn XP when approved.
