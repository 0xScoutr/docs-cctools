Connecting social accounts to CCTools enables social login, strengthens your Human Score, and links your identity across the Canton community. The more accounts you connect, the more complete your verified presence becomes — both for your own convenience and as a signal of authenticity to others.

## Connecting accounts

You can link any combination of Google, X (Twitter), Discord, and Telegram to your CCTools account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connected Accounts">
    Navigate to **Settings → Profile → Connected Accounts** (or go directly to `/profile/connections`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a platform to connect">
    You'll see a row for each supported platform: **Google**, **X (Twitter)**, **Discord**, and **Telegram**. Click **Connect** next to the one you want to link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the connection">
    A pop-up will open asking you to authorize CCTools to read your basic profile information from that platform. Follow the prompts to approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Once authorized, the platform will appear as **Connected** in your list. You can link multiple accounts in the same session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnecting accounts

If you want to remove a linked account, return to **Settings → Profile → Connected Accounts** (`/profile/connections`) and click **Disconnect** next to the platform you want to unlink. The connection is removed immediately. You can reconnect that account at any time by following the steps above.

<Note>
  CCTools only reads basic profile information — such as your name and username — from connected accounts. It does not access your private messages, read your feeds, or post anything on your behalf.
</Note>
