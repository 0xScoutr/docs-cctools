---

**Profile > Activity** keeps what you did on CCTools in five tabs. The figure on each tab counts what it holds, and every list puts what needs you first. Activity is only for you, and only when you are signed in.

## Contributions

The edits and listings you send to the directory. A moderator decides each one, and each row shows its state: **With a moderator**, **Changes asked**, **Accepted**, **Listed** (a new project that went live) or **Rejected**, with the moderator's note when there is one.

## Campaigns

Each campaign you joined: where it stands, how many of its tasks are done and the one thing left to do. Its tasks, prize and rule stay on the campaign's own page. States include **Tasks left**, **Every task done**, **Waiting for the draw**, **Not drawn** and **Code claimed**.

## Learn

Your Learn paths, done and in progress. A lesson counts once you pass its quiz, and a path once every lesson does. See [Learn](/earn/learn-paths).

## Check-in and XP

Your check-ins over the last 12 weeks, your longest streak, your level, your latest XP events (each run of daily check-ins on one line) and your badges. Pro members also see how many streak recoveries are left this month. See [XP, levels and badges](/earn/xp-system).

## Votes

The projects you voted for in the directory, newest first. Only you see this list; while your page is public, its public data still names your three most-voted projects as favorites.

## Connected accounts and wallets

Connected accounts are in **Settings > Connected accounts**, and wallets in **Manage wallets** on your Portfolio. See [Your Profile and Settings](/account/profile-settings#settings).
