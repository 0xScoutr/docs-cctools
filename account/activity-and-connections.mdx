The Activity and Connections sections let you review your engagement history and link external accounts for community participation and portfolio management.

## Activity

Visit **Activity** to see your complete engagement history across three tabs.

### XP tab

Every XP event you earned appears here, sorted newest first. Each entry shows:

- The action that earned XP (for example, "Daily check-in", "Upvote project", "Project approved").
- When you earned it (relative time like "2h ago" or exact date for older events).
- The XP amount in green.

The summary strip at the top displays your total XP events earned.

### Submissions tab

Track every project, opportunity, and edit suggestion you submitted. Each entry shows:

- The submission title.
- Its type label (New project, Opportunity, or Edit suggestion).
- Submission date.
- Current status as a colored badge:
  - Green "Approved"
  - Amber "Pending" or "Needs revision"
  - Red "Rejected"

The summary strip shows your total submissions, broken down by approved and pending counts. For approved project submissions, you can click the arrow icon to jump to the project page.

### Votes tab

View all the projects you upvoted, sorted newest first. Each row displays:

- The project name and logo.
- Its category (if set).
- When you upvoted it.
- A green badge confirming "Upvoted".
- A link to the project page.

The summary strip shows your total upvoted projects.

## Connections

The **Connections** page lets you link social accounts and wallets to enable task verification and cross-chain portfolio management.

### Connected Accounts

Link your social accounts to unlock participation in community-driven features and task verification. Five providers are supported:

- **Google**: Click Connect to authenticate with your Google account. Once linked, your email appears below the label. Disconnect anytime with the disconnect icon.
- **X (Twitter)**: Link your X account by clicking Connect. Your handle (@username) displays when linked.
- **Discord**: Connect your Discord account to enable community verification. Your Discord username appears when linked.
- **Telegram**: On desktop, a login widget appears when you click Connect. On mobile, you are redirected to Telegram for authentication. Once connected, your Telegram username displays.
- **GitHub**: Link your GitHub account using OAuth. Your GitHub username appears when linked.

For each connected account, you see a green "Connected" badge. Click the disconnect icon to remove the account. The backend will then clear the link on your next session.

### Wallets

Link Canton wallets and cross-chain EVM or Solana (SVM) wallets to aggregate your portfolio. You can:

- Connect multiple wallets: 1 per chain on the Free plan, up to 5 per chain on Pro, with one designated as primary per chain.
- Rename wallet labels inline for easy identification.
- Set a primary wallet.
- Disconnect wallets at any time.

For the full wallet setup guide, including step-by-step instructions for adding wallets and managing labels, see [Adding Wallets](/portfolio/adding-wallets).
