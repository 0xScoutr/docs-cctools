## What is CCTools Pro?

CCTools Pro is a premium subscription that unlocks advanced features:

- **10 wallets** (vs 3 on free plan)
- **30-day balance history** (vs 7-day on free)
- **Tax report export** - CSV format compatible with Koinly
- **Validator performance dashboard**
- **Network historical charts**
- **Compare up to 4 projects** (vs 2 on free)
- **6 badge showcase slots** (vs 3 on free)
- **Priority support**

Visit the **Pro** page on CCTools to see current pricing. Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime.

## How to Subscribe

Go to **Settings > Subscription** and click **Upgrade to Pro**. Payment is processed securely via Stripe.

You can cancel anytime from the same page. Your Pro features remain active until the end of your billing period.
