CCTools Pro is an optional subscription that unlocks an enhanced set of features for power users of the Canton Network. If you want real-time alerts on earn opportunities, curated Canton Network news delivered directly to you, and deeper community integration, Pro is built for that.

## What Pro includes

Upgrading to Pro gives you access to three additional notification categories and the content streams that power them:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Earn & Campaigns" icon="coins">
    Get alerted the moment new earn opportunities and campaigns go live — so you can act before they fill up or expire.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Canton News" icon="newspaper">
    Receive a curated feed of articles, announcements, and updates from across the Canton Network, surfaced directly in CCTools.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community" icon="users">
    Stay in the loop on forum announcements, governance discussion posts, and key community moments as they happen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="More coming soon" icon="sparkles">
    The Pro feature set continues to grow. Subscribers get first access to new capabilities as they roll out.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to upgrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Account settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Account** and look for the **Pro Subscription** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or click any locked feature">
    If you try to enable a Pro-only notification toggle or access Pro content, you'll see a **Pro** badge next to it. Click it to be taken directly to the upgrade flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the upgrade">
    Follow the on-screen prompts to activate your Pro subscription. Once confirmed, all Pro features and notification categories unlock immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The vast majority of CCTools features are completely free. Pro is designed for users who want real-time alerts on earning opportunities and curated Canton Network content — not a paywall on core functionality.
</Note>
