## What is CCTools Pro?

CCTools Pro is a premium subscription that unlocks advanced features:

- **15 wallets in one portfolio** - 5 each on Canton, EVM and Solana (free is 1 per chain)
- **90 days of portfolio history** - balances, rewards and transfers, against 30 days on free
- **Portfolio alerts (coming soon)** - CC in or out and balance levels on your wallets
- **Streak recovery** - repair a broken login streak, up to 3x a month
- **Exclusive Pro badges** - the Pro Member badge, plus XP toward your level
- **Email delivery for notifications** - portfolio alerts and referral notifications also arrive by email

## How to Subscribe

Go to the **Pro** page at [cctools.network/pro](https://cctools.network/pro) and pick a plan. There is no Subscription tab in Settings.

You can pay by card through Stripe, monthly or annually, or make a one-time payment in Canton Coin. A CC payment buys a fixed 30-day or 1-year window and does not auto-renew.

Card subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from the same page, and your Pro features stay active until the end of the billing period.
