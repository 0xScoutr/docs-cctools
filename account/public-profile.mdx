## Your public page

Every CCTools member has a public page at `cctools.network/u/{handle}`. Anyone with the link can open it, signed in or not.

It shows your display name, your handle, your photo and the month you joined, plus a **Verified** tag when you are Verified and have left it on, and your level and streak when you turn them on. The tag never shows which wallet. Visitors can pass the page on with **Share**. Under **Campaign badges** it lists the campaign badges you signed, each with the campaign and the day. It does not show your earlier badges or your activity lists, but its public data carries your earlier badges while the page is public.

To change your name or photo, or copy your page's address, use **Edit profile** on your Profile. See [Your Profile and Settings](/account/profile-settings).

## Privacy switches

**Profile > Privacy** decides what others see. You always see your own full profile.

- **Public page** - anyone with the link sees your page. Off, visitors see only "This profile is private", you are not listed on the leaderboard, and the switches below wait.
- **Verified on your public page** - the Verified tag, never which wallet
- **Level and streak on your public page** - "Level N, M-day streak" beside your name. Off by default
- **Show activity stats** - your XP, level, current and longest streaks, check-in days, rank and counts in your public profile's data, and your level and XP in its link preview and search data

The Privacy tab also previews your page as a visitor sees it, and the preview changes as each switch saves.

## What is never shown

- Your email address, your account settings and your ways to sign in
- Which wallet made you Verified, or signed a campaign badge

See [Getting Verified](/account/verified) for what the Verified tag means.
