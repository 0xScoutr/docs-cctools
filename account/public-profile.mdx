Your public profile is what other CCTools users see when they look up your account. You control exactly what's visible — from your Human Score and earned badges down to your XP level and activity streak. These settings let you share as much or as little as you like.

## Privacy controls

To manage your profile visibility, go to **Settings → Profile → Privacy**. You'll find three toggles that let you fine-tune what's exposed to the rest of the community.

| Toggle | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Public profile** | When turned off, your entire profile becomes private and no one else can view it. |
| **Show Reputation Score** | Show or hide your Human Score on your public profile page. |
| **Show activity stats** | Show or hide your level, XP, streak, badges, and other activity data. |

<Note>
  Changing your privacy settings only affects what others see. Regardless of your configuration, you always have full visibility into your own account data, stats, and history.
</Note>

## What others see on your public profile

When your profile is set to public, other users can see the following information:

- **Username** — your CCTools display name
- **Badges** — achievements you've earned through quests and platform activity
- **XP level** — your current experience level on the platform
- **Streak** — your current daily activity streak
- **Referral count** — the number of users you've referred to CCTools
- **Reputation Score** — your Human Score, if you've chosen to display it

You can hide your Reputation Score and activity stats independently using the toggles in Privacy settings, so you have full control over which of these fields are visible.

## Leaderboard visibility

Your leaderboard rankings are visible to all users by default when your profile is public. This allows the community to see how you rank on XP, streaks, and other competitive metrics. If you set your profile to private, you will be removed from public leaderboard views.
