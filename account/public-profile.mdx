## Overview

Every CCTools user has a public profile at `cctools.network/u/{slug}`. Your profile is publicly viewable and shareable, even without login. By default, your profile is set to public, but you control what appears on it through privacy toggles on the **Privacy** page.

Your public profile acts as your Canton Network identity card, displaying your level, achievements, activity streak, badges, and favorite projects.

## What Appears on Your Profile

### Header Section

Your profile header shows:

- **Display name** - The name you set on **Profile > Identity**
- **Avatar** - Your profile picture
- **Level bubble** - Your current level (1 to 50, the cap) displayed as a number in the top right of your avatar ring
- **Level name and color** - Your tier (e.g. "Validator", "Architect", "Canton OG") rendered in your level's color
- **Member since** - The month and year you joined CCTools
- **Era badge** - Genesis or Discovery era badge, displayed in the top right of the banner
- **Human Score badge** - A trust-signal badge (Verified or Elite) appears below your name if you have a verified Score and have not hidden it (see Privacy controls below)

### Activity Stats Grid

When **Show activity stats** is enabled, your profile displays 8 cards:

- **Total XP** - Cumulative XP earned (formatted as 1.2k for 1200+)
- **Streak** - Current consecutive daily active days, or "-" if no active streak
- **Badges** - Count of earned badges (excluding era badges)
- **XP Rank** - Your global rank by total XP, or "-" if unranked
- **Actions** - Total submissions, upvotes, interests, and referral completions
- **Referrals** - Confirmed referral count
- **Upvotes given** - Total project upvotes you have cast
- **Interests** - Total opportunity interests you have marked

### Level Card

Below the header stats, a card displays your progression:

- **Level Crest** - Shows your current XP, progress bar toward the next level, and milestone indicator

### Badge Gallery

Your earned badges appear in two sections:

- **Top Badges** - Your 3 rarest badges (sorted by rarity: Legendary, Epic, Rare, Uncommon, Common), displayed large with name and rarity level
- **All Badges** - All remaining badges in a compact grid, up to 12 visible. A "more badges" button shows if you have earned more than 15
- **Era badge** - A standalone section for your Genesis or Discovery era tier

Click any badge to see its full name, description, rarity, and the exact date you earned it.

### Activity Calendar

A 90-day check-in calendar shows your daily activity across 3 months. This calendar:

- Displays the last 90 days of your check-ins
- Shows your current streak and best streak (lifetime)
- Uses color-coded cells: level color for active days, amber for today, and light gray for inactive days
- Lists the count of active days per month

### Favorite Projects

Your top 3 projects by upvote frequency appear as linked cards, each showing:

- **Rank** (1st, 2nd, 3rd) in a colored pill
- **Project logo and name**
- **Category** tag
- **Total votes** this project has received from all users
- **Your votes** (×N voted) if you have upvoted it multiple times
- A colored bar showing relative vote weight

Clicking a favorite project takes you to its ecosystem profile.

### Human Score Badge

For verified-and-above users, a Human Score badge appears under your display name in the header (when enabled via privacy controls). The badge shows:

- **Shield icon and tier name** (Verified or Elite) in green
- A clickable button that opens an explainer showing what the score measures and how it is calculated

The badge is a public trust signal. If your score is below the verification threshold (26 points), or if you have not connected a Canton wallet, the badge does not appear. If your account is restricted (banned), a red "Restricted" badge appears instead.

For more detail on how the Human Score works, see the Human Score page.

## What Is Hidden

The following never appear on your public profile:

- **Email address** - Only you can see it
- **User ID** - Internal UUID not exposed
- **Account settings** - Password, 2FA, session data
- **Disabled sections** - Any section you toggle off in Privacy remains hidden
- **Admin and operator accounts** - The profile returns "User not found"
- **Private profiles** - Returns "This profile does not exist or is private"

## Getting and Sharing Your URL

### Copy Your URL

On the public profile page, click the **Share** button at the top right to open the Share Modal. Use the three action buttons:

1. **Copy URL** - Copies your full profile URL to the clipboard (e.g., `https://cctools.network/u/yourusername`)
2. **Post on X** - Opens a pre-filled tweet with your display name, level, tier, rank, and XP, with a link to your profile
3. **Save Image** - Downloads a profile card PNG with your avatar, name, level, tier, and top stats

### Your URL Slug

Your URL slug is derived automatically from your display name when your account is created: lowercased, accents folded, anything else turned into a hyphen, capped at 32 characters, with a numeric suffix if it collides with an existing slug. Once assigned it is stable. There is no slug field to edit on **Profile > Identity**, and renaming your account later does not change your URL.

## Privacy Controls

Visit **Profile > Privacy** to manage what others see on your public profile. All toggles default to **ON**. Toggling a setting off immediately removes that section from your public profile view:

- **Public profile** (master toggle) - When OFF, your profile returns "This profile is private" to all visitors. You always see your full profile. This is the primary privacy gate.
- **Show activity stats** - Controls visibility of level, XP, streak, badges, calendar, and stats (Rank, Actions, Referrals, Upvotes, Interests)
- **Show Score** - Controls whether the Human Score badge appears in your header (note: requires Verified+ tier and an active score; Newcomer users see no badge regardless)

**Note:** You always see your own full profile. Privacy toggles only affect what other visitors see.
