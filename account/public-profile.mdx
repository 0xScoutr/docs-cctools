## Your public page

Every CCTools member has a public page at `cctools.network/u/{address}`. Anyone with the link can open it, signed in or not.

It shows your display name, your address, your photo and the month you joined, plus a **Verified** tag when you are Verified and have left it on, and your level and streak unless you hide them. The tag never shows which wallet. Visitors can pass the page on with **Share**. Under **Campaign badges** it lists the campaign badges you signed, each with the campaign and the day. It does not show your earlier badges or your activity lists, but its public data carries your earlier badges while the page is public.

To change your name, photo or page address, use **Edit profile** on your Profile. See [Your Profile and Settings](/account/profile-settings).

## Privacy switches

**Profile > Privacy** decides what others see. You always see your own full profile.

- **Public page** - anyone with the link sees your page. Off, visitors see only "This profile is private", you are not listed on the leaderboard, and the switches below wait.
- **Verified on your public page** - the Verified tag, never which wallet
- **Level and streak on your public page** - "Level N, M-day streak" beside your name, and in your page's link preview and search data. On by default
- **Show activity stats** - your XP and longest streak on your page, and your XP in its link preview and search data
- **Contributions and Learn paths on your public page** - the edits and listings a moderator accepted, and the Learn paths you finished. Off by default

The Privacy tab also previews your page as a visitor sees it, and the preview changes as each switch saves.

## What is never shown

- Your email address, your account settings and your ways to sign in
- Which wallet made you Verified, or signed a campaign badge

See [Getting Verified](/account/verified) for what the Verified tag means.
