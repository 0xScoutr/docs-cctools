## Password Security

Use a strong, unique password for your CCTools account. We recommend:

- At least 12 characters
- Mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
- Don't reuse passwords from other sites
- Use a password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, etc.)

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

CCTools supports 2FA for extra account security:

- **Email 2FA** - Receive a verification code via email on login
- **TOTP** - Use an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.)

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA in **Settings > Account**.

## General Tips

- Never share your account credentials with anyone
- CCTools will never ask for your password via email or DM
- Be wary of phishing sites - always check the URL is **cctools.network**
- Report suspicious activity via the contact form at **cctools.network/contact**
