Securing your CCTools account prevents unauthorized access to your portfolio data, earned XP, and activity history. Taking a few minutes to set up a strong password and two-factor authentication (2FA) significantly reduces the risk of your account being compromised.

## Change your password

To update your password, go to **Settings → Account → Password**. Enter your current password, then choose a new one.

A strong password should be:

- At least 12 characters long
- A mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols
- Unique to CCTools — not reused from any other site or service

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a second layer of verification when you sign in. Even if someone gets hold of your password, they won't be able to access your account without passing the second factor. CCTools supports two 2FA methods.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Option 1 — Email verification">
    Go to **Settings → Security → Email Verification** and click **Enable**. When you sign in, CCTools will send a 6-digit code to your registered email address. Enter that code to complete the login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Option 2 — Authenticator app">
    Go to **Settings → Security → Authenticator App** and click **Enable**. A QR code will appear on screen. Open your authenticator app — such as **Google Authenticator**, **Authy**, or any TOTP-compatible app — and scan the QR code to link it to your CCTools account. From that point on, you'll enter a time-based code from the app each time you sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use an authenticator app rather than email 2FA wherever possible. Authenticator apps are not vulnerable to SIM-swapping attacks or email account compromises, making them the stronger option.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  If you lose access to your 2FA method — for example, a lost phone or inaccessible email account — account recovery options may be limited. When setting up an authenticator app, save your backup codes in a secure location such as a password manager.
</Warning>

## Active sessions

CCTools tracks your current active session. If you need to sign out — for example, after using a shared device — go to **Settings → Session → Sign Out**. This immediately ends your current session and requires you to sign in again.

## Deleting your account

If you want to permanently remove your CCTools account, go to **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account**.

Before proceeding, be aware that:

- All your account data will be permanently deleted, including your XP, badges, streak, and referral history
- This action **cannot be undone**
- You will not be able to recover the same username or account after deletion

You will be prompted to confirm your intent before the deletion is carried out.
