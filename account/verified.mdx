## What Verified means

Verified tells campaigns and visitors that a real person is behind an account. It is a yes or no, never a score.

Verified means your account signed for a Canton wallet it links, and that wallet has a footprint: at least 1 CC, a first transfer 30 or more days old, or the CCTools Discord check. Until CCTools finishes its move to signed wallets, its current check counts too (campaign pages call it the earlier check). That check needs a connected Canton wallet, together with enough other signals on your account, such as a confirmed email, two-factor sign-in, a photo, linked social accounts, the age of your account and steady activity.

## Get Verified

Open **Get Verified** at [cctools.network/profile/verify](https://cctools.network/profile/verify), from the **Verified** card on your Profile, or from **Get Verified** on a campaign page. It shows the one next step that is open to you:

- **Get Verified with CCTools' current check** - while wallet signatures are not switched on, Verified comes from CCTools' current check. **Open the current check** shows what you have and what is still missing.
- **Connect your wallet** (step 1 of 2) - once wallet signatures are on, connect a Canton wallet, or pick one you already linked, and sign one message in it. The signature moves nothing and costs no fee.
- **One more step** (step 2 of 2) - the signed wallet needs a footprint: hold at least 1 CC in it, or pass the CCTools Discord check. A wallet whose first CC transfer is 30 or more days old counts too. Press **Check again** once it has one.
- **You're Verified** - the page says how it was proven.

A wallet counts for one account only.

If your **Verified** card reads **Verified until** a date, your Verified comes from the earlier check: sign a wallet before that day to keep it. An account whose wallets are all hosted by an app that cannot sign keeps the earlier check instead.

## Where it counts

Some campaigns ask for Verified to claim a code or win a draw, and each campaign page says its rule. A draw marked "earlier check" counts CCTools' earlier check, not a wallet signature. See [How winners are chosen](/campaigns/how-winners-are-chosen).

## Who sees it

Your public page shows a **Verified** tag while **Verified on your public page** is on in **Profile > Privacy**. The tag never shows which wallet.
