## What Verified means

Verified tells campaigns and visitors that a real person is behind an account. It is a yes or no, never a score.

There are two ways to be Verified:

- **A signed wallet** - your account signed for a Canton wallet it links, and that wallet shows use: at least 1 CC, a first CC transfer 30 or more days old, or the CCTools Discord check
- **CCTools' account check** - a connected Canton wallet, together with enough other signals on your account, such as a confirmed email, two-factor sign-in, a photo, linked social accounts, the age of your account and steady activity. It runs every 4 hours

## Get Verified

Open **Get Verified** at [cctools.network/profile/verify](https://cctools.network/profile/verify), from the **Verified** card on your Profile, or from **Get Verified** on a campaign page. It shows the one next step that is open to you:

- **Connect your wallet** (step 1 of 2) - connect a Canton wallet, or pick one you already linked, and sign one message in it. The signature moves nothing and costs no fee
- **Wallet signed** (step 2 of 2) - the signed wallet needs to show use: hold at least 1 CC in it, or pass the CCTools Discord check. A wallet whose first CC transfer is 30 or more days old counts too. Press **Check again** once it does
- **You're Verified** - the page says why you are Verified, how you keep it, and where it counts right now

One wallet's keys count for one CCTools account. A wallet linked before 25 October 2026 counts for the account check without a signature, so keep it linked. A site that asks you to send CC to get Verified is a scam.

## Where it counts

- **Draws** - a draw marked **Verified to win** picks its winners only among Verified accounts, and each campaign page says its rule. A draw marked **account check** counts only CCTools' account check: a signature alone does not pass it. **Open the account check** shows what you have and what is still missing
- **The rankings** - they list Verified accounts only, and they read the account check. See [The ranking](/campaigns/how-campaigns-work#the-ranking)

See [How winners are chosen](/campaigns/how-winners-are-chosen).

## Who sees it

Your public page shows a **Verified** tag while **Verified on your public page** is on in **Profile > Privacy**. The tag never shows which wallet.
