## Where to find Alerts

**Alerts**, at [cctools.network/profile/alerts](https://cctools.network/profile/alerts), sets every alert you get and where it arrives. Open it from the **Alerts** tab of your Profile, **Alerts** in your account menu, **Open alerts** in **Settings > Plan and preferences**, or **Choose your alerts** at the top of the bell. Each choice saves as you change it.

**Your latest alerts** lists your five latest, as the bell holds them.

## What reaches you, and where

Alerts arrive on four channels:

- **Bell** - in CCTools, on any device. Always on: every alert you keep reaches it, except the weekly digest
- **Push** - on this device, once you press **Turn on** and your browser allows it. On an iPhone, add CCTools to the Home Screen first. **Turn off** ends it on that device
- **Email** - to your account's address, once you switch it on. With no address CCTools can write to, set one in **Settings**
- **Telegram** - **Connect** gives you a one-time link, good for 15 minutes, to the CCTools bot on Telegram, the same bot you can sign in with. **Open the bot** and press **Start** there to finish. **Disconnect** ends it, and so does sending `/stop` in the chat

Below the channels, a table lists each kind of alert (**Your activity**, **Projects you follow**, **Topics** and **Portfolio**) with a box for **Push**, **Email** and **Telegram**. Tick the channels each kind should use; the bell always gets it.

Each channel has a daily limit:

- **Push** - at most 3 a day, 1 of them from follows and topics. News never pushes
- **Email** - at most 3 alert emails a day, the rest in one email the next morning. Every alert email has a link that turns its kind of email off in one click, without signing in
- **Telegram** - at most 10 a day

## Your activity

On in the bell from the start, each with its own switch:

- **Your campaigns** - you won, the claim reminder, and a campaign ending with tasks left. A code that became yours shows in the bell only
- **Your edits and listings** - a moderator accepts or declines one. The decisions you are told of already reach the bell and push, with the moderators' email as before; this alert adds Telegram. A decline you are not told of sends nothing

## Projects you follow

**Follow**, on a project's page beside **Vote**, adds the project to your alerts. Each project you follow has its own choices:

- **News** - headline news about the project
- **Featured app rights** - the project gains or loses a featured app right
- **Votes** - a governance vote that names the project opens, and its result
- **Campaigns** - the project starts a campaign on CCTools

A new follow starts with News, Featured app rights and Votes ticked, and Campaigns not. A project with no Canton party linked to it has no rights or votes to follow. On the Alerts page, **For every project you follow** switches each kind for all your projects at once; a project's own choice must be on as well. You can follow up to 50 projects, and **Unfollow** stops a project's alerts.

What you follow is private: nobody sees or counts it.

## Topics

Follow a topic on the Alerts page to get it in the bell:

- **Daily record** - when a day's record is final, with its figures
- **Governance votes** - every vote that opens, and its result
- **New projects** - Mondays: the projects added to the directory that week
- **DeFi** - Mondays: new DeFi and DEX projects
- **Tokenized assets** - Mondays: new tokenized asset and RWA projects
- **Validators** - Mondays: the week's change in validator licenses, from the record
- **CC price** - CoinGecko's market price passes a level you set (above or below, in USD), or moves more than you set in 24 hours. With no level set, a move of 10% in 24 hours
- **New campaigns** - at most once a day: the campaigns that started on CCTools

If you follow both **Governance votes** and a project that a vote names, you get the project's alert only.

## Portfolio alerts

Portfolio alerts are for Pro members. They watch up to 5 Canton wallets you signed for in [Get Verified](/account/verified), checked every hour. For each wallet you can set:

- **CC in or out over** an amount of CC
- **Balance** below or above a level of CC

A wallet you have not signed for reads **Not signed**. **Remove** takes a wallet's alerts away.

## Weekly digest

The weekly digest goes out on Mondays at 09:00 UTC, by email or by Telegram, never in the bell or by push. It holds the record's week, the projects added to the directory, the governance votes and Canton DeFi value locked. Ticking it by email does not turn on alert email.

## What is on from the start

- **Your activity** in the bell: your campaigns, and your edits and listings
- Push, email and Telegram off for every kind until you tick them, with email switched off. **Turn on** in the push popup ticks Push for Your activity
- No project or topic followed, and the weekly digest off

## Push on this device

On your first visit signed in, on a browser that can show notifications, a popup asks **Turn on alerts on this device?** **Turn on** asks your browser for permission and ticks **Push** for **Your activity**. **Later** brings the question back the next day. **No** stops it for good: push is then turned on only from the Alerts page. Until you answer No, the bell also offers to turn on alerts on this device; turning them on there before you have answered Turn on does the same.

## Turning alerts off

Every choice on the Alerts page can be changed at any time: untick a channel or a kind, unfollow a project or a topic, remove a Portfolio rule, turn push off on a device, or disconnect Telegram. The bell always shows the alerts you keep.

## Other notices from CCTools

Some notices are not alerts: the Alerts page does not set them, and the alert limits do not count them. Each reaches the bell, and push on the devices where push is on:

- **Always**: announcements from the CCTools team, and answers to your requests (your API access application, and the moderators' decisions on your edits and listings)
- **Unless you turned them off in the earlier notification settings**: badges you earn, referrals completed with your invite link, new content (new projects, campaigns, Earn opportunities and Learn paths) and new governance proposals

An update to a campaign you marked interest in reaches the bell only. Replies to an API access application also come by email, and a completed referral can be emailed to Pro members. No page in CCTools turns these notices off; turning push off on a device stops every push CCTools sends there.

Security emails (password resets, two-factor sign-in codes and account lock notices) are sent apart from alerts and cannot be turned off.

## Privacy

- CCTools keeps your alert choices; the projects and topics you follow, which are private and never shown or counted to anyone; your Portfolio rules; your answer to the push popup; and, for each alert, its title, text and link, and whether it reached you in the bell, by push, by email or on Telegram. The record of each alert stays for as long as your account exists, so the bell can show it and the daily limits can be counted
- Push goes through your browser's push service (Google, Apple, Microsoft or Mozilla, depending on your browser). CCTools keeps each device's push address until you turn push off there, the browser ends it, or deliveries to it keep failing
- Alert emails are sent through Resend to your account's address
- Telegram messages go only to a chat you started with a link from your account. CCTools keeps that chat's id and your Telegram username
- CCTools counts, in total and never per person, how often each kind of alert is read in the bell, to send fewer of the kinds people do not read
- No partner can buy an alert or a push

The record of each alert and your answer to the push popup cannot be edited; deleting your account deletes all of it. See the [Privacy Policy](https://cctools.network/privacy).
