Your CCTools profile is your identity on the platform. From Settings, you can update your account credentials, control what others see on your public profile, and connect social accounts.

Settings are grouped into focused areas so you can make one change without having to scroll through unrelated options. Whether you want to tighten your account security, adjust what your profile shows publicly, or configure which notifications you receive, each section below takes you directly where you need to go.
