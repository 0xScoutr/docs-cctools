## Update Your Profile

Go to **Settings** from the sidebar. Here you can:

- Change your display name
- Upload an avatar
- View your account email
- See your account creation date and stats

## Connected Accounts

You can connect social accounts to your CCTools profile:

- **Twitter/X** - Required for the X Follower badge and campaign tasks like follow/retweet
- **Discord** - Used for campaign integrations
- **Google** - Alternative login method

Manage your connected accounts in **Settings > Profile**. You must always have at least one login method active (you can't disconnect your only identity).

## Public Profile

Every CCTools user has a public profile at **cctools.network/u/your-username**. Your public profile shows:

- Display name and avatar
- Level and level title
- Badge showcase (3 free, 6 Pro)
- Current streak

You can share your profile link on social media to showcase your Canton ecosystem engagement.
