## Your Profile

Open **Profile** from your account menu. It has five tabs: **Overview**, **Activity**, **Alerts**, **API keys** and **Privacy**. See [Alerts](/account/alerts) for the Alerts tab.

**Overview** shows:

- Your name, your handle, the month you joined and your **Verified** state, with **Edit profile** and **Share**
- Four figures: **Contributions accepted**, **Campaigns completed**, **Learn paths done** and **Level**, each opening its part of [Activity](/account/activity-and-connections)
- **Today** and **This week** - your daily quests and weekly challenges, what each one pays, and the one thing to do next (see [XP, levels and badges](/earn/xp-system))
- **Check-in** - your daily check-in, your streak and this month's check-ins (see [XP, levels and badges](/earn/xp-system))
- **Verified** - where you stand and your next step (see [Getting Verified](/account/verified))
- **Invite** - your invite link
- **Your record** - what needs you first (a prize to claim, a campaign with tasks left, a Learn path in progress, a contribution sent back for changes), then what you did, newest first

## Edit your name and photo

Click **Edit profile**:

- **Display name** - 2 to 50 characters: letters, numbers, spaces, dots, hyphens and underscores, with no links. Two members cannot share a name.
- **Photo** - a PNG, JPG or WebP image of up to 2 MB
- **Your page's address** - the link to your public page, with **Copy**
- **Show my level and streak** - beside your name on your public page. Off by default

The panel also previews your public page. See [Public Profile](/account/public-profile).

## Your invite link

The **Invite** card holds your link, `https://cctools.network/ref/` followed by your code (`CC-` and six letters or digits), with **Copy link**, and counts the people who joined with it. A friend who opens the link lands on sign-up with your code already filled in. The same block is at [cctools.network/referral](https://cctools.network/referral).

A friend who joined with your link finishes the invite with the steps under **Finish your invite** on their Profile. Its 3 project votes count before level 8 once they follow CCTools on X and are Verified with a signed Canton wallet. Each person can be referred only once.

## Settings

Open **Settings** from your account menu. It has four sections:

- **Sign-in and security** - your ways to sign in, your email, your password, two-factor sign-in, and this browser's session
- **Connected accounts** - Google, X, Discord, Telegram and GitHub, each with what CCTools uses it for: Google, X and Discord can sign you in; X, Discord, Telegram and GitHub check campaign tasks; Discord also carries the Pro role on the CCTools server
- **Plan and preferences** - your plan, with a link to [Pro](/account/pro-subscription); your wallets, with **Manage wallets**; **Alerts**, with **Open alerts** (see [Alerts](/account/alerts)); and the theme, kept in this browser
- **Delete account** - what a deletion ends, said before you confirm it

You cannot disconnect the account that is your only way to sign in.

## Wallets

Wallets are managed from **Manage wallets**, on your Portfolio or in **Settings > Plan and preferences**. Free accounts connect 1 wallet per chain, and Pro accounts up to 5 per chain, on Canton, EVM and Solana. See [Adding Wallets](/portfolio/adding-wallets).
