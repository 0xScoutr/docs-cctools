## Update Your Profile

Go to **Settings** from the sidebar, then select **Profile**. Here you can:

- Change your display name
- Upload an avatar
- View your account email
- See your account creation date and stats

## Connected Accounts

You can connect social accounts to your CCTools profile for campaign tasks and verifications.

### Available connections

- **X (Twitter)** - Required for X Follower badge and campaign tasks like follow/retweet
- **Discord** - Used for campaign integrations and server join verification
- **Telegram** - Used for campaign integrations and group verification
- **Google** - Alternative login method

### Managing connections

Go to **Settings > Connections** to add or remove social accounts. 

- You can connect multiple social accounts to a single CCTools profile
- You must always have at least one active login method (you cannot disconnect your only identity)
- Connected accounts appear with their display name or username so you can confirm which account is linked

## Wallet Connections

You can link blockchain wallets across multiple chains to your profile.

### Supported chains

- **Canton** - Link your primary Canton wallet for verification. Free tier supports 1 wallet, Pro supports up to 5
- **EVM** - Ethereum and EVM-compatible chains (Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, etc.)
- **Solana** - Solana (SVM) wallets, linked via signature verification

### Managing wallets

Go to **Settings > Connections** to link new wallets or manage existing ones.

- Set any linked wallet as primary with a single click
- Label wallets to keep track of multiple addresses
- Disconnect wallets that are no longer in use
- Each chain has its own wallet limit based on your plan (free tier: 1 per chain, Pro tier: up to 5 per chain)
- Cross-chain wallet connections add bonus score toward your Canton ecosystem verification

## Public Profile

Every CCTools user has a public profile at **cctools.network/u/your-username**. Your public profile displays:

- Display name and avatar
- Level and level title
- Your three rarest badges, featured automatically
- Current streak

Share your profile link on social media to showcase your Canton ecosystem engagement.
