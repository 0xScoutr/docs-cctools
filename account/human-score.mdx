Your Human Score is a credibility signal that helps the Canton Network community identify verified humans and filter out bots and sybils. Unlike a raw number, you see it as a descriptive tier that matches how other identity platforms (Worldcoin, BrightID, Galxe) communicate trust.

## What it measures

The Human Score combines multiple corroboration signals to answer a single question: "Is this a unique human?" It pulls from three main categories.

- **Identity and security**: Email verification, two-factor authentication, and an uploaded avatar
- **On-chain and social proof**: Canton wallet connection, linked social accounts (X, Discord, Telegram), account age, and activity patterns
- **Community signals**: Engagement consistency (daily streaks of 7+ days or 10+ actions in 30 days), being referred by a trusted user

The score is computed by a materialized view that refreshes every four hours and on-demand when you add or remove a stamp. Banned accounts are hard-capped at a score of 0 and never Verified, regardless of other signals.

## Score tiers

Your score maps to one of four tiers. Every stamp below is priced by how expensive it is to forge, which puts the highest score anyone can reach at **73**, not 100.

| Tier | Range | Verified? | Meaning |
|------|-------|-----------|---------|
| Restricted | 0 | No | Account banned (contact support if you think this is a mistake) |
| Newcomer | Any | No | No Canton wallet connected, or score below the floor |
| Verified | 26-54 | Yes | Connected Canton wallet AND score at or above 26 |
| Elite | 55-73 | Yes | Score 55 or higher, which is out of reach without on-chain history |

The **Verified floor is 26**: you need both a connected Canton wallet (the anchor) AND a score of at least 26 to be Verified. This two-part gate (anchor plus corroboration) ensures that a wallet alone is not sufficient, and soft factors alone are never sufficient.

Only Verified and Elite earn a visible shield badge on your public profile. Newcomer accounts show no badge.

## What "Verified" means

Verified is a formal gate: your account is only Verified if you have BOTH of these conditions met.

1. A connected Canton wallet (Send, 5N Loop, or another Canton client)
2. A Human Score of 26 or higher

This mirrors the "anchor plus corroboration" pattern used by Gitcoin Passport and other credibility platforms. On Canton, this design is stronger because party-IDs are not free: a single human cannot easily create many verified accounts.

Once you reach Verified, you unlock the ability to participate in on-chain campaigns and other community features that require proof of humanity.

## How to improve your score

The concrete levers are the stamps you earn. Connect a Canton wallet, add social and identity proofs, stay active, and keep your account fresh.

**Wallet and on-chain (up to 40 points)**
- Connect a Canton wallet: +8 points. This is the anchor for the Verified badge.
- Your linked Canton wallet holds CC: +12 points
- Active on Canton for 90+ days (first on-chain transfer): +15 points
- Link more than one Canton wallet: +2 points (+1 for two, +2 for three or more)
- On-chain history on a linked EVM or Solana wallet: +3 points

**Social proof (up to 21 points)**
- Pass verification in the CCTools Discord server: +10 points
- Linked GitHub that is 1+ year old and has 5+ public repos: +5 points
- Link your X account: +2 points
- Link your GitHub account: +2 points
- Link your Discord account: +1 point
- Link your Telegram account: +1 point

**Security and identity (up to 8 points)**
- Enable two-factor authentication: +6 points
- Verify your email: +1 point
- Upload an avatar: +1 point

**Activity and longevity (up to 4 points)**
- Daily engagement streak (7+ days) or 10+ actions in 30 days: +2 points
- Account age: +2 points, scaled by time and capped at 6 months

Your score increases as you earn stamps, and you can always see your progress on the Score tab.

## Where you see it

**Your Score tab**
Visit **/profile/score** to see your detailed breakdown. The page shows your current tier, tier description, a progress ring, and every stamp you can earn: organized by Identity, Socials, Wallet, and Activity. Earned stamps show your actual contribution to the score; unearthable stamps show the target points.

**Your public profile**
If you have a Verified-and-above tier and the **show_human_score** privacy toggle is on, your score badge appears as a shield pill in your public profile header (/u/[your-slug]). The badge shows your tier name (Verified or Elite) but never the raw number.

**Where badges render**
The Verified badge also appears on your profile in the CCTools hub and on leaderboards.

## Privacy control

The **show_human_score** toggle on the Privacy tab (/profile/privacy) controls whether your score badge is visible to others on your public profile. When the toggle is off, your score remains private to you: only you see it on your own /profile/score page.

All other privacy rules (is_public, show_activity_stats) continue to work as before. Remember: changing privacy settings only affects what others see. You always see everything on your own account.

## FAQ

**Can I see my raw score?**
No. The 0-100 score is internal and only surfaces as a tier label and the points breakdown. This matches how other identity platforms present trust: tier names are clearer and less gamifiable than raw numbers.

**Do I need to visit /profile/score to earn stamps?**
No. Stamps are earned automatically when you complete the action (link a social, enable 2FA, stay active). The /profile/score page just shows you the breakdown.

**Is my score ever deleted?**
No. Your score persists until you either ban your account or your account is banned. Removing individual stamps (e.g. unlinking a social) will lower your score, but it will not reset to zero.

**How often does my score update?**
The score recomputes every four hours on a cron schedule, and instantly when you add or remove a stamp via the UI (the breakdown API reflects the change immediately).
