The Human Score is a reputation metric CCTools uses to verify that activity on the platform comes from real users, not bots or automated accounts. It gives the community a way to gauge how genuine and engaged a participant you are — and it gives you a way to demonstrate your credibility within the Canton ecosystem.

## What affects your Human Score

Your Human Score is calculated from a combination of signals that reflect real, sustained engagement with CCTools:

- **Quest completion** — finishing Learn & Earn quests shows consistent, purposeful use of the platform
- **Consistent platform activity** — regular logins and interactions contribute positively over time
- **Connected social accounts** — linking Google, X (Twitter), Discord, or Telegram adds verified identity signals
- **Profile completeness** — a fully filled-out profile is a stronger trust signal than an empty one
- **Referral quality** — referring users who go on to engage actively with the platform reflects well on your score

## Where it appears

Your Human Score can appear in two places:

- **Your public profile** — visible to other CCTools users when you enable it in **Settings → Profile → Privacy → Show Reputation Score**
- **Community contexts** — the score acts as a trust signal that the broader Canton community can reference when evaluating participants

If you've disabled the Reputation Score toggle in your Privacy settings, your Human Score will not be shown to other users. See [Public Profile](/account/public-profile) for details on managing that toggle.

## Improving your score

The most effective way to grow your Human Score is to use CCTools as an active, genuine participant. Specifically:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use CCTools regularly">
    Log in and interact with the platform consistently. Regular engagement over time is weighted more heavily than sporadic bursts of activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Learn & Earn quests">
    Working through quests in the Academy and Earn sections demonstrates purposeful engagement and contributes directly to your score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your social accounts">
    Link Google, X (Twitter), Discord, and Telegram in **Settings → Profile → Connected Accounts**. Each verified connection strengthens your identity signal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintain a daily streak">
    Keeping an active streak shows that you're a consistent user — one of the clearest indicators of genuine human activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The Human Score is a trust signal — it does not directly affect your XP total or leaderboard rankings. Think of it as a reputation layer that sits alongside your activity stats, not a replacement for them.
</Info>
