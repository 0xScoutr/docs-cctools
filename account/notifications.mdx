CCTools sends notifications to keep you informed about new Canton ecosystem events, governance proposals, learning content, and earning opportunities. You can fine-tune exactly which categories you hear about — in-app, by email, or both.

## In-app notifications

In-app notifications appear in the **notification bell** at the top of the CCTools interface. To manage which categories trigger a notification, go to **Settings → Notifications** and toggle each one on or off.

| Category | What you'll be notified about | Plan required |
|---|---|---|
| **Ecosystem updates** | New projects and participants added to the Canton ecosystem | Free |
| **Governance alerts** | New proposals submitted and vote results published | Free |
| **Learn paths** | New learning content and courses published in the Academy | Free |
| **Earn & Campaigns** | New earn opportunities and active campaigns | Pro |
| **Canton News** | Curated articles and updates about the Canton Network | Pro |
| **Community** | Forum announcements and governance discussion posts | Pro |

## Email notifications

Email notifications are enabled by default and cover the same categories as in-app notifications. They are managed separately from in-app toggles and run on a scheduled cadence — you won't receive an email for every individual event, but rather a digest of activity in each category.

If you want to stop receiving email notifications for a category, you can manage your email preferences from the link included in any CCTools notification email.

## Pro-only notifications

The **Earn & Campaigns**, **Canton News**, and **Community** notification categories require an active CCTools Pro subscription. If you're on the free plan, these toggles will appear locked in your notification settings.

Upgrading to Pro gives you access to all six notification categories and ensures you never miss a time-sensitive earning opportunity or important Canton Network update. See [Pro Subscription](/account/pro-subscription) for details on upgrading.

<Info>
  In-app notifications appear in the notification bell icon and are delivered in real time as events occur. Email notifications arrive on a scheduled cadence and may not reflect the very latest activity at the moment they land in your inbox.
</Info>
