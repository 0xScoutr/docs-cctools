## Notification Categories

CCTools sends notifications for several distinct events:

- **Portfolio alert** - Balance moves and stalled rewards on the wallets you track
- **New content** - New projects, Earn opportunities, and Learn paths published on CCTools
- **Campaign update** - When an Earn campaign you marked interest in is updated. Sent to everyone who marked interest, whatever the Earn preference says
- **New governance proposal** - When a new Super Validator proposal opens, and when one is approved or rejected
- **Badge earned** - When you unlock a new badge
- **Referral completed** - When a referred user confirms their referral
- **Announcement** - Platform-wide messages from the CCTools team, always delivered in-app
- **Canton News** - Curated articles about Canton Network. Pro only, and off by default
- **Community** - Forum announcements and governance posts. Pro only, and off by default
- **API access** - The status of your public API application, always delivered in-app

Decisions on your submissions (project, opportunity, or post approved / rejected / needs changes) and account security events (password reset, 2FA login code, account lock) are **not** part of this system. They are sent as separate direct emails, never appear in the bell dropdown, and have no toggle.

## In-App vs Email

Every notification appears in the **bell dropdown** in the top bar. **Settings > Notifications** controls that bell.

Most categories are in-app only. The only ones that can produce an email are **portfolio alerts** and **referral completed**, and that email is a **Pro** feature: free accounts never receive it, whatever the stored email preference says. **API access** replies are the exception. They answer something you submitted, so they go to every applicant, free accounts included. The Settings page currently exposes only the in-app column.

When emails do go out, the delivery job runs every 10 minutes and sends **one email per notification**, not a combined digest.

Security-sensitive emails (password reset, 2FA login code, account lock) are always sent and cannot be disabled.

## Turning Things Off

**Settings > Notifications** has six toggles: Ecosystem updates, Governance alerts, Learn paths, Earn & Campaigns, Canton News and Community. Canton News, Community and Earn & Campaigns are marked Pro, and their toggles are disabled on a free account. Portfolio alerts, badge earned, referral completed, announcements and API access replies have no toggle at all, so they cannot be muted.

The **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom of a notification email opens the **Account** tab of your settings. The per-category toggles are one tab over, on **Settings > Notifications**. There is no one-click per-category unsubscribe.
