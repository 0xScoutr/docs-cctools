## General

**Is CCTools free?**
Yes! CCTools is free to use. We offer a Pro tier for advanced features, but all core features are free.

**Is CCTools official?**
CCTools is not an official Canton Network or Digital Asset product. We are an independent platform, built for the Canton ecosystem and part of it.

**How do I report a bug?**
Use the contact form at **cctools.network/contact** with a description of the bug, screenshots if possible, and your browser/device info.

## Account & XP

**Why did my XP drop?**
Taking back today's vote on a project removes the XP that vote gave.

**How do I reach level 8?**
Check in every day, pass Learn quizzes and complete Learn paths, and finish campaign tasks. Level 8 opens voting for projects, feedback and tool suggestions. See [XP, levels and badges](/earn/xp-system).

**Can I reset my account?**
Use the contact form at **cctools.network/contact** if you need an account reset. Note that this will erase all XP and badges.

## Portfolio

**Is my wallet safe?**
You connect a wallet through the wallet app itself (the wallet picker for Canton, a signed message for EVM and Solana), never by pasting an address, and CCTools only reads public data for read-only tracking. We never ask for private keys or seed phrases. Your wallet is never at risk.

**Why are my balances wrong?**
Prices are updated in near real-time but may have slight delays. If balances seem significantly wrong, try refreshing or removing and re-adding your wallet.

**Can I track wallets on other chains?**
Yes! You can connect Canton wallets along with cross-chain wallets from EVM and SVM networks to track a unified portfolio. Open **Manage wallets** on your **Portfolio** or in **Settings** to link all your wallets in one place.
