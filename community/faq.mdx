Below you'll find answers to the questions that come up most often about CCTools. If you can't find what you're looking for here, ask in the [CCTools Discord](https://discord.gg/Pm2YpafyxP) or send a message through the [feedback form](https://cctools.network/feedback).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is CCTools?">
    CCTools is an all-in-one toolkit for the Canton Network ecosystem. It brings together portfolio tracking, an ecosystem directory, DeFi tools, governance information, news, a developer resources hub, a community Academy, and more — all in one place. CCTools is an independent platform built for the Canton community; it is not an official Digital Asset or Canton Network product. CCTools has received an approved Canton Dev Fund grant.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is CCTools free to use?">
    Yes. CCTools is free for all core features including portfolio tracking, the ecosystem directory, the Academy, the news feed, and the XP and rewards system. A **Pro subscription** unlocks advanced features such as additional notification categories, expanded analytics, and Earn & Campaign alerts. You can use CCTools fully without ever upgrading.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my private key or wallet safe?">
    CCTools never asks for your private key or seed phrase — not during sign-up, not anywhere in the platform. Portfolio tracking only requires your **public wallet address** for read-only access to on-chain data. Your wallet funds are never at risk when using CCTools. If you ever receive a message claiming to be from CCTools and asking for private keys, it is a scam.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does portfolio tracking work?">
    You add your Canton (and optionally EVM or SVM) wallet addresses in **Portfolio** or **Profile → Connections**. CCTools reads your on-chain balances and transaction history using the public address — no signing required. Token prices are updated in near real-time. If balances appear incorrect, try the **Refresh** button on the portfolio page; if the issue persists, try removing and re-adding the wallet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the XP system?">
    XP (experience points) measure your engagement with the CCTools platform. You earn XP by completing daily actions: logging in, upvoting projects, following CCTools on X, adding wallets, publishing Academy posts, contributing to the ecosystem directory, and more. XP accumulates into your **Level**, which unlocks additional platform features — for example, Academy upvoting becomes available at Level 4. Your level and XP total are visible on your public profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Canton Coin (CC)?">
    Canton Coin (CC) is the native utility token of the Canton Network. CCTools surfaces Canton Coin data — price, supply, staking information, and governance relevance — across the portfolio, network, and governance sections of the platform. CCTools does not issue or manage Canton Coin; it displays publicly available on-chain and market data about it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the Pro subscription?">
    Pro is CCTools' paid tier. It unlocks advanced features including additional notification categories (Canton News alerts, Earn & Campaign alerts, Community forum notifications), expanded analytics, and future Pro-exclusive tools. You can upgrade to Pro from your account settings. Core CCTools features remain free regardless of subscription status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the algorithm score on project listings?">
    Each project in the CCTools ecosystem directory has an algorithm score that reflects a combination of signals: community upvotes, engagement activity, on-chain data where available, and editorial weighting. It's not a financial recommendation — it's a relative indicator of community interest and project activity to help you quickly compare listings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often is data updated?">
    Different data types update on different schedules:
    - **News feed** — refreshed every 10 minutes
    - **Portfolio balances** — updated in near real-time; use the Refresh button to force a sync
    - **Notifications** — CCTools scans for new content every 30 minutes
    - **Status page** — each endpoint is checked approximately every 2 minutes

    Canton indexers occasionally experience delays. If your portfolio data looks stale, wait a few minutes and refresh.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    The fastest way to get help is the [CCTools Discord](https://discord.gg/Pm2YpafyxP) — post in **#support** and the team or community members will respond. For bugs or account issues that need a direct response, use the [feedback form](https://cctools.network/feedback). Include a description of the issue, any relevant screenshots, and your browser and device details for technical problems.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
