The CCTools Discord server is the real-time hub of the Canton Network community. It's where you'll find the fastest answers to platform questions, hear about new features before they're announced elsewhere, and connect directly with other users, builders, and ecosystem participants.

Join using this invite link: **[discord.gg/Pm2YpafyxP](https://discord.gg/Pm2YpafyxP)**

You can also reach the same link at [cctools.network/discord](https://cctools.network/discord).

## What's in the server

Once you're in and verified, you'll have access to the full community:

- **#announcements** — Official CCTools updates, new feature launches, and important ecosystem news. Follow this channel to never miss a release.
- **#support** — Ask questions about the platform and get help from the CCTools team and experienced community members. This is the fastest way to resolve account or feature issues.
- **#ecosystem** — Discuss Canton Network projects, share discoveries, and talk through what's being built across the ecosystem.
- **#governance** — Follow Canton governance proposals, share your views, and stay informed on protocol decisions that affect the network.

## Getting verified

When you first join, complete the emoji captcha to unlock full server access:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the verify command">
    Type `/verify` in any channel, or click the persistent **Verify** button in the welcome channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the challenge">
    A short challenge appears with four emoji buttons. Click the one that matches the prompt (for example, "Click the Apple"). The challenge expires after 5 minutes — if it times out, just run `/verify` again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the Verified role">
    Once you click the correct emoji, you receive the **Verified** role within seconds and gain full access to all channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Link your CCTools account

Linking your Discord account to your CCTools profile unlocks additional benefits: your Discord roles automatically mirror your CCTools platform level, and you earn engagement XP for chat activity (messages, reactions received, and replies received — up to 300 XP per day).

To link your account, run `/link` in Discord and follow the OAuth prompt. Make sure you're logged into [cctools.network](https://cctools.network) in your browser first. The connection syncs within about a minute.

You can manage your linked accounts at any time from **Profile → Connections** on CCTools.
