CCTools is built around community participation — the Academy, the News forum, the ecosystem directory, and the Discord server all rely on members contributing honestly and treating each other well. These guidelines apply everywhere you interact with the CCTools community. By using the platform you agree to follow them.

## General conduct

The baseline expectation across all CCTools channels is straightforward:

- **Be respectful** — Treat other users the way you'd want to be treated. Disagreements are fine; harassment, personal attacks, and hate speech are not.
- **No spam** — Don't post repetitive messages, unsolicited advertisements, or links to scams or phishing sites.
- **No impersonation** — Don't present yourself as a CCTools team member or impersonate another user.
- **Keep it on-topic** — Main channels and feeds are focused on Canton Network. Price speculation and "wen pump" posts don't belong here.
- **No exploit sharing** — Don't share, discuss, or promote methods for gaming XP, upvotes, or any other platform mechanic.

## Content standards

Anything you publish — Academy posts, forum submissions, project listings, or ecosystem contributions — is subject to review. Content must be:

- **Accurate** — Verify Canton-specific claims before publishing. Misinformation about protocol mechanics, project details, or financial data undermines the community.
- **Original** — Don't copy content from other sources without attribution. Properly credit any sources you quote or paraphrase.
- **Relevant** — Content should relate to Canton Network, the DeFi ecosystem built on it, ecosystem projects, or CCTools platform features.

## Upvoting

Upvotes exist to surface genuinely useful content. Use them that way:

- Upvote posts, projects, and contributions you personally found valuable
- Don't coordinate mass upvoting campaigns with other users
- Don't use multiple accounts to upvote your own content

The daily upvote limits built into the platform are there for the same reason — to keep the quality signal meaningful.

## Project submissions

The ecosystem directory and devtools listings are community-maintained resources. When submitting:

- Only submit **real, active** Canton Network projects
- Provide accurate descriptions, links, and team information
- Don't submit projects you're not associated with without confirming the details are correct

Misrepresentation in project submissions — including fake projects, inflated claims, or spam listings — may result in removal and account action.

## Enforcement

CCTools moderators review content and community behaviour across all channels. Violations are generally handled progressively:

1. **Warning** — A first offense typically results in a warning
2. **Mute or content removal** — Repeated or more serious violations may result in a temporary mute (on Discord) or removal of the offending content
3. **Suspension or ban** — Continued or severe violations result in account suspension or a permanent ban

Moderators have discretion to skip steps for severe violations. Sharing scam links, posting explicit content, or attempting to defraud community members will result in an immediate ban without warning.

<Note>
  These rules apply across all CCTools surfaces: the main platform, the Academy, the News forum, and the Discord server. The same standards apply everywhere.
</Note>
