<div className="cc-brand-rules">
<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Do">

- Keep clear space of half the icon's height around the logo.
- Keep the icon at 16 px or larger. At 16 px, use the <a href="/brand/cctools-icon-lime-16.svg" download>16 px cut</a>.
- Use the avatar where the icon is cropped to a circle: it is drawn to fit.
- Keep the editorial logo for large, calm layouts, such as covers and print.

</Card>
<Card title="Don't">

- Recolor, rotate or flip the head.
- Add effects or outlines.
- Put the icon on busy photos.
