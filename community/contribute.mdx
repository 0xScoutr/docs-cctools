CCTools is shaped by the community that uses it. The ecosystem directory, Academy, and news forum all grow through contributions from people like you. Whether you want to share knowledge, add a project you've discovered, report a bug, or just connect with other Canton Network participants, there's a place for you to help.

Every approved contribution earns you XP toward your platform level and badges on your public profile.

## Ways to contribute

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Write an Academy post" icon="pen-to-square" href="https://cctools.network/academy/submit-post">
    Share your Canton Network knowledge as an article, guide, or tutorial. Approved posts are published to the Academy feed and credited to your profile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Submit a project" icon="rocket" href="https://cctools.network/ecosystem/submit">
    Know a Canton Network project that isn't in the directory? Submit it through the multi-step form. Approved listings help the whole community discover what's being built.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Submit a DevTool" icon="code" href="https://cctools.network/devtools/submit">
    Add developer tools, libraries, SDKs, and GitHub repositories to the CCTools DevTools directory. Help other builders find the resources they need.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Join Discord" icon="discord" href="https://discord.gg/Pm2YpafyxP">
    Connect with the CCTools community in real time. Share updates, ask questions, discuss Canton projects, and earn engagement XP through participation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send feedback" icon="comment" href="https://cctools.network/feedback">
    Suggest new features, report bugs, or tell us what's not working. Feedback from active users is the primary driver of product improvements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Suggest an edit" icon="pencil" href="https://cctools.network/ecosystem">
    Spot outdated or incorrect information on a project listing? Use the Suggest Edit option on any ecosystem project page to submit a correction for review.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Discord

The CCTools Discord server is the fastest way to connect with other Canton Network participants, get real-time help, and stay on top of platform announcements.

Join at **[discord.gg/Pm2YpafyxP](https://discord.gg/Pm2YpafyxP)** or via [cctools.network/discord](https://cctools.network/discord). Key channels include:

- **#announcements** — Official CCTools updates and new feature launches
- **#support** — Get help with the platform from the team and community members
- **#ecosystem** — Discuss Canton Network projects and ecosystem developments
- **#governance** — Follow and discuss Canton governance proposals

Once you've joined, link your Discord account to your CCTools profile (via `/link` in Discord) to sync your platform level and earn XP for Discord engagement activity.

For a full guide to the Discord server — including verification, account linking, and how to earn engagement XP — see [Discord](/community/discord).

## Feedback

The fastest way to suggest a feature or report a bug is through the feedback form at [cctools.network/feedback](https://cctools.network/feedback). Include as much detail as you can: what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and your browser or device if it's a technical issue. Screenshots help.

For urgent issues or anything that needs a direct response, use the contact form at [cctools.network/contact](https://cctools.network/contact).
