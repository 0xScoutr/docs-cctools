## Submit a project

Know a Canton project the directory misses? Sign in, then open **Submit a project** from the **Ecosystem** menu, at [cctools.network/ecosystem/submit](https://cctools.network/ecosystem/submit). The form has four steps:

1. **Basics** (required) - the name, a one-sentence tagline, the category, a description of at least 100 characters, a line on what it does on Canton, the website (unless it is a validator) and its status
2. **Links and Canton** (optional) - docs, app, GitHub, X, Telegram, Discord and its parties
3. **More detail** (optional) - this can also be added after approval, with **Suggest an update**
4. **Review** (required) - with **About you**: a contact email, how you know the project (**I work on it**, **I use it**, **I found it online** or **Other**), a note and a public link

If a project with the same name is already listed, the form asks whether yours is a different one and how it differs. An editor reviews every submission within 24-72 hours, checking the links, the category and any party IDs, then approves or declines it with a note. You can submit up to 10 projects a day. Your submissions and their state are in **Profile > Activity > Contributions**.

## Suggest an update

If a listing is outdated or wrong, open the project's page and click **Suggest an update**. Sign in, change the fields that need it, section by section, and send the change. A CCTools moderator checks each change before it shows on the profile, and its state shows in **Profile > Activity > Contributions**.

## Suggest a tool

**Build on Canton**, under **Developers** in the **Library** menu, lists SDKs, tools and repositories. From level 8 you can suggest one there, up to 3 in 24 hours.

## Report what is wrong

- **Report a wrong match** on News, when a story is linked to the wrong project (it opens Feedback, from level 8)
- **Suggest a correction** on an article (it opens Feedback, from level 8)
- **Send feedback**, in your account menu, from level 8
- The [Contact](https://cctools.network/contact) page, for anything else, at any level
