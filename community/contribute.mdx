## Suggest Opportunities

Found an interesting earn opportunity in the Canton ecosystem? You can suggest it directly on CCTools:

1. Go to **Earn** and click **Submit Opportunity**
2. Fill in the details: title, URL, type, description, provider, estimated value
3. Submit for review

Each suggestion earns you XP, and approved suggestions earn you the **Opp Scout** badge. Get multiple suggestions approved for the **Verified Scout** badge.

There is a daily limit on suggestions to maintain quality.

## Submit a Project

Know a project building on Canton that's not in our directory? Submit it:

1. Go to **Ecosystem** and click **Submit Project**
2. Fill in the multi-step form (project info, team, features, FAQ)
3. Submit for review

Approved submissions help grow the ecosystem directory for everyone.

## Suggest Edits

If you notice outdated or incorrect information on a project listing, you can suggest edits:

1. Go to the project's page in the Ecosystem
2. Click **Suggest Edit**
3. Update the fields that need correction
4. Submit for review

This helps keep the directory accurate and up to date.
