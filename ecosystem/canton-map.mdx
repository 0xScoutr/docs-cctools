## Two views

**Map**, in the **Ecosystem** menu at [cctools.network/canton-map](https://cctools.network/canton-map), has two views, picked with **Sectors** and **Connections** at the top. Both show every live and building project in the CCTools directory, with no paid placements. Inactive projects are not drawn: they are in the directory.

## Sectors

The default view groups the projects into seven sectors: **Infrastructure and nodes**, **Payments and wallets**, **Trading and exchanges**, **DeFi and assets**, **Institutions and capital**, **Builders and data**, and **Community and culture**. An index of the sectors, with their counts, leads to one card per sector, sub-grouped by category. Each name opens the project's profile.

Find a project with the search, filter by status (Live or Building), and highlight the projects with a featured app or a validator. **Next match** steps through the results. In the key, a dashed mark is a project still building and a small square marks a featured app. On a phone, the sector index becomes one row you scroll sideways.

## Connections

The **Connections** view draws the projects and their parties as a graph. Search among the projects and super validators, show all sectors or one, filter by status, and highlight the projects with a featured app, with a validator, running a super validator or with a campaign. Layer switches show or hide **Validators**, **Super validators**, **Featured apps** and **Campaigns**.

In the key, a project is larger the more parties it links, a validator sits beside its project, and a super validator carries its project's logo. Zoom in and out, **Fit the whole graph** or go **Full screen**. On a phone, one finger scrolls the page, two fingers move and pinch the graph, and a tap opens a mark.

Click a mark to open its panel: its roles, links to its validator or featured app page, the other categories it is in, and **Suggest an update**. Below the graph, tables list the super validators, the projects with the most linked parties and the validator licenses.
