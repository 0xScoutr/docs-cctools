The Canton Map gives you a bird's-eye view of the Canton Network as a living, interactive graph. Instead of reading through lists, you can see validators, ecosystem projects, and the relationships between them rendered visually — making it easy to understand how different participants connect and where any given project sits within the broader network.

## Accessing the map

Go to [cctools.network/canton-map](https://cctools.network/canton-map) to open the map. From the landing page, select a category (Finance, Infrastructure, Social, and others) from the category grid. The map loads the subgraph for that category, keeping performance snappy regardless of how large the overall network grows.

## What you'll see

The map renders Canton Network participants as nodes in a force-directed graph, with edges representing the relationships between them.

**Node types** you'll encounter:

- **Projects** — solid colored circles grouped by category color (for example, blue for DeFi, amber for Trading)
- **Validators** — hollow ring nodes that secure one or more projects on-chain
- **Super Validators** — amber ring nodes with elevated governance rights
- **Featured Apps** — solid purple circles representing apps built on top of ecosystem projects
- **Earn Listings** — green circles marking active earning opportunities within the category
- **Campaigns** — purple circles showing limited-time promotions tied to projects

**Visual overlays** on project nodes give you at-a-glance status:

- A **purple sparkle badge** means the project has featured apps built on top of it
- An **amber bolt badge** indicates a sponsored project
- A **green hexagon badge** means at least one validator secures this project
- An **amber hexagon badge** means at least one Super Validator secures this project

**Connections** tell the story of relationships: projects connect upward to their category hub, validators connect inward to the projects they secure, and featured apps branch outward from the projects that power them.

## Navigating the map

**Pan** by clicking and dragging anywhere on the canvas.

**Zoom** by scrolling up to zoom in and scrolling down to zoom out. The zoom buttons in the bottom-right corner give you finer step-by-step control. Use **Reset View** to return to the default position at any time.

**Search** using the search bar at the top to find any project, validator, or app by name. Results appear in a dropdown with keyboard navigation — use the arrow keys to move through suggestions and press Enter to jump to a node. If the node you're jumping to lives in a different category, the map automatically switches to that category and focuses on the node.

**Click any node** to open its detail panel on the right side of the screen (or as a bottom sheet on mobile). The panel shows the node's name, logo, category, connected nodes, scores, and links to external resources. From the detail panel you can navigate directly to the project's full CCTools profile page.

**Filter node types** using the filters button to toggle validators, Super Validators, featured apps, earn listings, and campaigns on or off. A badge on the button shows how many filter types are currently active.

If nodes start to pile up or the layout feels cluttered after a lot of interaction, click **Reheat Simulation** to restart the force physics and let the graph settle back into a clean arrangement.

<Info>
  The Canton Map is updated continuously as new projects and validators join the network. Category subgraphs typically contain 30–80 nodes and load in under 500ms, with the simulation running entirely in your browser for smooth 60fps interaction.
</Info>

## Desktop vs. mobile

On **desktop**, the legend sidebar appears on the left with the node-type key, visual overlays guide, and category picker. You can hide the sidebar to expand the canvas. The node detail panel docks to the right side.

On **mobile**, tap the filters button to open the legend and category picker in a bottom sheet. The detail panel slides up from the bottom when you select a node.

## Tips for exploring the map

- **Start with a smaller category** like Services or DAO if you're new to the map — Finance (DeFi) is the largest subgraph and can feel overwhelming at first.
- **Hover over a node** to see its name in a tooltip before clicking.
- **Search for a project you already know** to jump straight to it, then explore its connected edges to discover related validators and apps you might not have found otherwise.
- **Use the legend** on the left to keep track of what each color and shape means — the visual grammar is consistent across all categories.
