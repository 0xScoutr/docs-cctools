The CCTools Ecosystem directory is the most comprehensive index of Canton Network projects available — covering 470+ projects across every sector of the network, from decentralized exchanges and wallets to tokenized real-world assets and core infrastructure. Whether you're researching where to swap CC, looking for a custody solution, or simply exploring what's being built on Canton, the Ecosystem page is your starting point.

**Sort options** let you reorder results by what matters most to you:

- **Highest Score** — surfaces the top-rated projects by CCTools AI algorithm score
- **Most Upvoted** — shows projects the community endorses most
- **Most Viewed** — highlights projects attracting the most attention right now
- **Recently Updated** — brings freshly updated listings to the top

You can combine a category filter with any sort option. For example, filter to **Wallet** and sort by **Highest Score** to find the best-rated wallet apps on Canton.

## Project cards

Each project in the directory is represented by a card showing the key details at a glance:

- **Name and logo** — the project's identity
- **Category** — which sector of the ecosystem it belongs to
- **Status** — either **Live** (actively running on Canton) or **Building** (in development)
- **AI Score** — an out-of-100 score generated by the CCTools algorithm, covering activity, maturity, community, transparency, and documentation
- **Upvote count** — the total number of community upvotes the project has received
- **View count** — how many times the project page has been opened

These at-a-glance metrics let you quickly compare projects without clicking into each one individually.

## Project detail pages

Click any project card to open its full detail page. Here you'll find everything CCTools knows about that project:

- **Full description** — what the project does and the problem it solves
- **Role on Canton** — how the project participates in the Canton Network (validator, app, protocol, etc.)
- **Features** — key capabilities and product highlights
- **Team** — the people and organization behind the project
- **FAQ** — answers to common questions about the project
- **Connected projects** — other Canton projects this one integrates with or is related to

### Algorithm Analysis breakdown

Every project detail page includes an **Algorithm Analysis** section that breaks down the AI score into its five component dimensions:

| Dimension | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Activity** | On-chain and off-chain activity signals — transactions, deployments, updates |
| **Maturity** | How established the project is — age, stability, track record |
| **Community** | Social following, engagement, and community growth |
| **Transparency** | Openness of team, code, roadmap, and governance |
| **Documentation** | Quality and completeness of technical and user-facing docs |

Each dimension is scored independently, so you can see exactly where a project excels and where it has room to grow.

## Upvoting

If you find a project valuable, click the **upvote button** on its card or detail page to show your support. Upvotes are public and contribute to the project's **Community** dimension score in the AI algorithm.

Projects with strong upvote momentum may also surface in trending sections across CCTools, giving them additional visibility with the broader community.

## Submitting a project

Don't see a Canton project in the directory? Anyone can submit a project for review.

Head to [cctools.network/ecosystem/submit](https://cctools.network/ecosystem/submit) and fill out the submission form. The CCTools team reviews every submission for relevance and quality before it appears in the directory. Submissions are free and open to all Canton Network participants.
