## The Ecosystem directory

**Ecosystem** lists the Canton projects CCTools maps, at [cctools.network/ecosystem](https://cctools.network/ecosystem). Its menu opens each view:

- **Directory** - the mapped projects, with search and filters
- **Map** - the projects grouped by sector
- **New this month** - the projects added to the directory this month
- **News** - what the press says about the projects, linked to each one
- **Categories** - the projects by what they do; a category with a page of its own opens it
- **A to Z** - every mapped project in one list
- **Compare** - projects side by side

A project's page opens from any of them. **Submit a project** adds one the directory misses. Each project page's **Analytics** shows how people find the project on CCTools: see [Project analytics](/ecosystem/project-analytics).

## Search, filters and order

Search by name, then narrow the list:

- **Status** - Live, Building or Inactive
- **Role** - Featured app, Validator or No linked party
- **Category** and **Utility** - from what the listed projects declare

Order the list by **Name A to Z** or **Recently added**. A sponsored project inside its paid window stays first under every filter and order that keeps it: see [Sponsored listings](/ecosystem/sponsored-listings).

## Category pages

Nineteen categories have a page of their own, at `cctools.network/ecosystem/category/` followed by the category, such as [cctools.network/ecosystem/category/defi](https://cctools.network/ecosystem/category/defi): Node Operations, Infrastructure, Financial Institution, DeFi, Tokenized Assets, Exchange, Analytics, Services, Payments, Trading, Wallet, Investor, Developer Tools, Custody, DEX, Stablecoin, Security, Interoperability and Tools. A category page opens while the directory lists at least 5 projects in it. In the **Categories** view, such a category opens its page, and any other category opens the directory filtered to it.

A category page shows:

- how many projects the directory lists in the category, inactive ones included, by status, and how many of them hold a featured app right, run a validator or have no linked party
- search, the **Status** and **Role** filters, the sectors the category's projects name, and the order, **A to Z** or **Newest**
- the projects as cards, 24 at first: what each one does, its status when it is not live, its roles and how many parties are linked to it, its voters over the last 30 days, and **New** when it was listed this month. The rest follow by name, and **Show them as cards** shows them all as cards
- **New this month**, or **Recently added** when fewer than six were listed this month, and **Trending this week**, the voters since Monday 00:00 UTC
- **Other categories**, each with its number of projects

A sponsored project comes first, labelled. Votes never order the list. **Pick to compare** lets you pick projects from the cards to open in [Compare](/ecosystem/compare).

## Voting for projects

The **Vote** button on a project's page shows your support for it, and the count beside it reads the project's voters over 30 days.

- Voting opens at **level 8**. Below level 8, a live campaign you joined lets you vote for its project
- You have **3 votes a day**, one per project, and the day starts at 00:00 UTC
- You can take back today's vote on the project's page, which frees the slot for another project

Your votes are listed, only for you, in **Profile > Activity > Votes**.
