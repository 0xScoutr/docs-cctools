## The Ecosystem directory

**Ecosystem** lists the Canton projects CCTools maps, at [cctools.network/ecosystem](https://cctools.network/ecosystem). Its menu opens each view:

- **Directory** - the mapped projects, with search and filters
- **Map** - the projects grouped by sector
- **New this month** - the projects added to the directory this month
- **News** - what the press says about the projects, linked to each one
- **Categories** - the projects by what they do
- **A to Z** - every mapped project in one list
- **Compare** - projects side by side

A project's page opens from any of them. **Submit a project** adds one the directory misses.

## Search, filters and order

Search by name, then narrow the list:

- **Status** - Live, Building or Inactive
- **Role** - Featured app, Validator or No linked party
- **Category** and **Utility** - from what the listed projects declare

Order the list by **Name A to Z** or **Recently added**. A sponsored project inside its paid window stays first under every filter and order that keeps it: see [Sponsored listings](/ecosystem/sponsored-listings).

## Voting for projects

The **Vote** button on a project's page shows your support for it, and the count beside it reads the project's voters over 30 days.

- Voting opens at **level 8**. Below level 8, a live campaign you joined lets you vote for its project
- You have **3 votes a day**, one per project, and the day starts at 00:00 UTC
- You can take back today's vote on the project's page, which frees the slot for another project

Your votes are listed, only for you, in **Profile > Activity > Votes**.
