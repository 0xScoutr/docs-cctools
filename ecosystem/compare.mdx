## Comparing Projects

The Compare tool lets you put projects side by side to evaluate them:

1. Go to **Ecosystem > Compare** or click **Compare** in the sidebar
2. Select up to 3 projects. The limit is the same on free and Pro.
3. View a detailed comparison including category, role, status, social metrics, token data, and community scores

This is especially useful when researching which projects to engage with or invest in.

There is no separate validator mode. When a selected project runs a validator, the table adds a **Canton Network** section with its network role and its CC rewards.

Canton has no delegation, so this is for reading a project's footprint, not for picking a validator to back.
