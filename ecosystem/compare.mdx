## Comparing projects

**Compare**, in the **Ecosystem** menu at [cctools.network/ecosystem/compare](https://cctools.network/ecosystem/compare), puts up to three projects of the CCTools directory side by side. The limit is the same on free and Pro.

1. Find a project in the picker, then add one or two more. **Or start from one of these** suggests sets computed from the directory, never picked by hand
2. Read the comparison, section by section
3. Switch between **All rows** and **Only differences**, which keeps only the rows where the projects differ

On a [category page](/ecosystem/browsing-projects#category-pages), **Pick to compare** puts a checkbox on each card. Pick up to three projects, then **Compare** opens them here in the order you picked them. **Stop picking** clears the picks.

The address keeps the projects you chose, so you can share a comparison.

## The sections

- **On the network** (always first) - the featured app right, validators, super validator, linked parties, lifetime app, validator and super validator rewards where a project has that role, and the featured app votes
- **Value locked** - from the project's own feed where CCTools reads one, else DefiLlama
- **On CCTools** - voters over the last 30 days and votes of all time, X followers, campaigns, stories over the last 90 days, the latest story and when the project joined the directory
- **Profile** - tagline, utilities, tags, details and team, as each profile lists them
- **Links** - the links each profile lists

Nothing on Compare ranks the projects: there is no score and no best or worst, and a numeric row draws a neutral bar beside each value. A value a read could not give says **Not recorded**, and a field a profile does not fill in says **Not listed**. Each section names its source and when it was read.

Canton has no delegation, so this is for reading a project's footprint, not for picking a validator to back.
