## How sponsored placements show

A project can pay for a sponsored placement for a set window. While the window runs:

- in the Ecosystem directory, the project is listed first under every filter and order that keeps it, with a **Sponsored** label
- in **Trending this week** on the directory, the sponsored project comes first, labelled and unnumbered, before the five projects most people voted for since Monday
- on some pages, a sponsored banner shows, with a **Sponsored** label above it

The label shows only during the paid window. The Ecosystem map has no paid placements.

## How to ask for one

Write to us on the [Contact](https://cctools.network/contact) page under **Partnership**, with the project, what you have in mind and when.
