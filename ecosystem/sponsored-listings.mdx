CCTools offers sponsored and featured listing placements for Canton Network projects that want elevated visibility in the Ecosystem directory. With 470+ projects indexed on the platform and a growing community of 20,000+ active users, sponsored placement puts your project in front of the people most engaged with the Canton Network on a daily basis.

## Featured listings

Featured projects receive prime placement throughout the CCTools platform:

- **Top of the Ecosystem page** — your project appears at the top of the directory, above organic results, with a clear **Featured** badge
- **Trending sections** — featured projects are eligible to appear in the weekly Trending highlights surfaced on the Ecosystem page and the Overview dashboard
- **Overview dashboard** — featured projects can appear in the dashboard spotlight visible to all logged-in CCTools users when they first open the platform

This placement ensures that users who are actively researching Canton projects — and are therefore most likely to engage with what you're building — see your project first.

## How to apply

Applying for a sponsored listing takes just a few minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the submit form">
    Head to [cctools.network/ecosystem/submit](https://cctools.network/ecosystem/submit).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out your project details">
    Provide your project name, description, category, website, and any relevant social or documentation links. The more complete your submission, the faster the review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request featured placement">
    In the submission form, indicate that you're interested in sponsored or featured placement. The CCTools team will follow up to discuss options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for review">
    The CCTools team reviews every submission for quality and relevance to the Canton Network. You'll typically hear back within 48 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organic scoring

It's worth understanding how sponsored placement relates to the CCTools AI algorithm score. The two systems are entirely separate.

Your project's AI score — which measures Activity, Maturity, Community, Transparency, and Documentation — is calculated objectively based on publicly verifiable signals. Sponsored placement has no effect on any of these dimensions, and the algorithm does not know or consider whether a project is sponsored.

This separation is intentional: it means users can trust that the scores they see in the directory are a fair reflection of each project's actual standing, not its marketing budget.

<Note>
  Sponsored placement does not affect a project's AI algorithm score. Scores are always calculated independently, based solely on objective metrics across Activity, Maturity, Community, Transparency, and Documentation.
</Note>
