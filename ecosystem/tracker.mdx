## What the Tracker Is

**Tracker** is a public Canton party inspector (Canton's answer to Etherscan). Search for any party by name from the party-labels directory, or paste a party-id directly to inspect balances, activity, validators, featured apps, and ANS names. No login required - everything is public and instant.

Think of it as a read-only explorer: see what any wallet holds, who they trade with, and how long they have been active on Canton.

## Searching for a Party

The Tracker search works two ways:

**By name** - Start typing a project, validator, app, or ANS name. The search surfaces results from the party-labels directory, sorted by match relevance:
- Projects and featured apps show their logo and category label
- Validators display a "Validator" tag
- ANS names appear with an "ANS" label
- Each result shows the short address for quick verification

**By party-id** - Paste or type a full party-id (format: hint::fingerprint) directly into the search box. Press Enter or click Inspect to jump straight to the detail page. No suggestions dropdown appears for party-ids.

Use arrow keys to navigate suggestions, Enter to select, or Escape to close the dropdown.

## What You See

When you inspect a party, the Tracker displays:

### Identity & Balance
- Party name (if registered) and full party-id
- Current balance in CC and USD equivalent
- **Badges**:
  - Stale indicator (shows cache age if data is older than live)
  - Persona badges (e.g., Validator, Featured App)
  - Active since date and days on network

### 30-Day Balance Chart
A line chart showing balance history over the last 30 days, with CC and USD values on hover. If less than 2 days of history exist, the chart does not display.

### Balance Breakdown
Four stats at a glance:
- Unlocked CC
- Locked CC
- Estimated rewards (30-day projection)
- Hosted on (validator or infrastructure provider)

### Activity Stats (Last 30 Days)
A summary of on-chain movement:
- Total number of transfers
- Volume received (in CC)
- Volume sent (in CC)
- Number of unique counterparties

### Recent Activity
The 10 most recent transfers to or from this party. Each row shows:
- Direction (sent/received, indicated by a red up-arrow or green down-arrow)
- Counterparty address (clickable link to inspect them)
- Amount transferred
- Timestamp

Click any counterparty address to inspect them in the Tracker.

### Top Counterparties
The parties this wallet exchanged with most in the last 30 days, ranked by frequency and volume:
- Number of transfers with each party
- Total CC exchanged
- Click to jump to that party's detail page

### Linked Project
If the inspected party is a Canton project (validator, featured app, or ecosystem member), a card displays:
- Project logo and name
- Link to view the full project details in the Ecosystem

## Recent Lookups

All successful inspections are saved locally in your browser under a "Recent lookups" card on the main Tracker page:
- Stored only in **browser localStorage** - not on your CCTools account
- Shows up to 8 most recent parties
- Click to jump back to a previous inspection
- Clear all history with the "Clear" button

This is browser-side convenience history only, similar to an explorer's bookmark list. Each browser and device keeps its own list.

## Tips for Effective Browsing

- **Search tips**: The search requires at least 2 characters. Full party-ids (containing ::) skip the name lookup and go straight to the detail page.
- **Navigation**: Use the back button to return to the main Tracker page. Recent lookups let you jump between multiple inspections without re-searching.
- **Activity context**: All stats reset to zeros if no activity occurred in the 30-day window. Recent transfers may show a counterparty of "network" if it was a direct mint or burn.
- **Stale data**: Balance data refreshes on a cache schedule. The Stale badge on the identity card shows you how old the cached balance is.
