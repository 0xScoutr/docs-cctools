## What Party lookup is

**Party lookup**, at [cctools.network/tracker](https://cctools.network/tracker) and in the footer, opens any Canton party read-only. No sign-in is needed, and nothing is saved to an account.

It shows what the network makes public: CC balances, CC transfers and how long a party has been active. Other activity on Canton is private to the parties involved, so it does not appear here.

## Find a party

Search a project or ANS name, or paste a party id: a name, two colons, then 68 hexadecimal characters. Each suggestion says what the match is: **Validator**, **Featured app**, **Validator and featured app**, **ANS name** or **Project**. Press **Look up** to open the party. You can also paste a whole party id into the site's search.

**Recent lookups** keeps the last 8 parties you opened, in this browser only, and **Clear** empties it.

## What you see

- **The header** - the party's name or short id, what it is (for example Validator or Featured app), its party id with a copy button, and **Look up another party**
- **Balance** - the CC total and its USD value, **Unlocked**, **Locked**, **Rewards, last 30 days** and **Active since**, the node it is hosted on, and the directory project it is listed as, when it is one
- **Balance, last 30 days** - a chart, drawn when every reading is whole and the balance is live; otherwise the page says why
- **Activity, last 30 days** - transfers, received, sent and counterparties, marked "at least" when a reading was partial
- **Latest transfers** - time (UTC), amount, direction and counterparty, with a CSV
- **Top counterparties** - the five parties it exchanged with most in 30 days, each opening its own lookup

Balances come from the Loop Data API, or from a backup provider whose snapshot can be more than 3 hours old, and the page says which source answered and when. Lookups are limited per visitor, and an answer is kept for up to two minutes.
