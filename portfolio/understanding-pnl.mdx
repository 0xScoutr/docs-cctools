## How P&L Works

CCTools never sees what you paid for your CC, so there is no cost basis and no acquisition price in the product. What the Portfolio shows instead:

- **Current value** - your CC balance priced at the live CC market price
- **Change** - how your balance moved over the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, measured against your own balance history
- **Rewards and burns** - each wallet page lists the CC it received and the CC it burned, which is where a balance change comes from

The value is shown in USD and the change as a percentage.

## Export Options

You can export your portfolio data in CSV format for:

- Tax reporting (compatible with Koinly and other tax tools)
- Personal record keeping
- Spreadsheet analysis

Go to **Portfolio** and click the **Export** button in the top right. It does not export on the spot: it opens the dedicated [Tax & Data Export page](https://cctools.network/exports), where you choose the wallet, the date range and the format.

**Note:** Exporting is a Pro feature, whichever purpose or format you pick. The one export route behind that page refuses a non-Pro account with a 403 before it even reads the requested format. Pro also loads a longer history: a free account loads 30 days of balances, rewards and transfers, Pro loads 90 days.
