CCTools calculates your portfolio P&L by comparing your current wallet values against historical snapshots, factoring in all inflows, outflows, and staking rewards earned over the selected period. The result gives you a clear picture of how your Canton holdings have changed — not just in raw CC terms, but in real value.

## Time range views

At the top of the Portfolio dashboard you'll find three period tabs: **24h**, **7d**, and **30d**. Switching between them updates every P&L figure on the page to reflect that window.

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **24h** | Balance change over the last 24 hours — useful for spotting same-day reward payouts or sudden price moves |
| **7d** | Seven-day view — good for tracking weekly reward accumulation and short-term trends |
| **30d** | Thirty-day view — the broadest default view, best for understanding your overall monthly position |

Each tab displays the total balance change as both an **absolute CC/USD amount** and a **percentage**, so you can see the magnitude and scale of the movement at a glance.

## What's included in P&L

Your P&L figure rolls up everything that affects your wallet's value over the selected period:

- **CC balance changes** — movement in your CC holdings due to price fluctuations in USD or actual transfers in and out
- **Staking rewards earned** — all CC rewards received from delegating to Canton Network validators during the period
- **Incoming transfers** — CC received from other addresses, which increases your portfolio value
- **Outgoing transfers** — CC sent to other addresses, which reduces your portfolio value

## Balance series chart

Each wallet's detail view includes a **balance series chart** — a time-series graph of that wallet's CC balance plotted over time. Use it to:

- **Identify reward payouts** — regular step-ups in the line correspond to staking rewards landing in your wallet
- **Spot large transfers** — sharp jumps or drops indicate significant CC moving in or out
- **Track price impact** — when the chart is denominated in USD, wider movements reflect CC price changes against the dollar

To open a wallet's chart, click that wallet's card from the main Portfolio page.

## Why your P&L may look unexpected

<Info>
  A few common reasons your P&L figure might seem off:

  - **New transfers look like gains or losses** — any CC you moved into a tracked wallet during the period appears as an inflow, which increases the period-over-period change. This is expected behavior, not an error.
  - **Data not yet updated** — Canton Network data can occasionally take a few minutes to fully reflect recent activity. If a recent transaction isn't showing yet, wait a moment and click **Refresh**.
  - **CC price denominated in USD** — P&L in USD terms moves with the CC/USD exchange rate, not just your on-chain balance. A flat CC balance can still show a positive or negative USD P&L if the price has moved.
</Info>

## Exporting for tax purposes

P&L figures in CCTools give you a real-time overview, but for formal accounting or tax reporting you'll want a complete, itemized transaction record. The **Export** tool generates a full CSV or JSON history of your rewards and transfers for any date range you choose.

See [Track Staking Rewards and Export Portfolio Data](/portfolio/rewards-tracking) to learn how to generate an export.
