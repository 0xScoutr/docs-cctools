## Rewards on your Portfolio

The **Net worth** card counts your Canton wallets' rewards over the last 30 days. It counts each wallet's first page of reward events, so a busy wallet earned at least that much, and a wallet whose page could not be read counts in no sum.

## A wallet's rewards

Open **Portfolio** and click a Canton wallet. Its page shows what moved over the period (**Received**, **Rewards**, **Sent**, **Burned in fees** and **Net change**) and splits the rewards into the three kinds a party can receive: **Validator**, **Featured app** and **Super validator**.

**Rewards by UTC day** charts them day by day, and its menu downloads them as CSV. The same page draws the wallet's closing CC balance by day and lists its transfers: 50 on Free, more pages on Pro.

Rewards, transfers and burns come from the Loop Data API, for the last 30 days on Free and 90 on Pro. A read that stops at its page limit says so and counts "at least", and a read that fails says so instead of showing zero.
