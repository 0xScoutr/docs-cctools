## Rewards Dashboard

The Rewards section in your Portfolio shows all rewards earned from:

- **Validator rewards** - CC earned by the validator node that hosts your party, per round
- **App rewards** - CC earned when your party is credited as a Featured App
- **Super Validator rewards** - CC earned when your party belongs to a Super Validator

Rewards are tracked automatically once your wallet is connected.

## Historical Rewards

The **Rewards** tab sits on a wallet's own page: open **Portfolio** and click a Canton wallet to reach it. It charts that wallet's daily rewards and lists each day under the chart. There are no filters for period, reward type or wallet, and the chart covers only the wallet you opened.

How far back the chart goes depends on your plan. Free accounts load the last 30 days of a wallet's rewards, Pro accounts load the last 90 days.
