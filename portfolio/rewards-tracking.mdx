## Rewards on your Portfolio

The **Net worth** card counts your Canton wallets' rewards over the last 30 days. It counts each wallet's first page of reward events, so a busy wallet earned at least that much, and a wallet whose page could not be read counts in no sum.

## A wallet's rewards

Open **Portfolio** and click a Canton wallet. **What moved, 30 days** shows **Received**, **Rewards**, **Sent**, **Burned in fees** and **Net change**, with the rewards split into the three kinds a party can receive: **Validator**, **Featured app** and **Super validator**.

**Rewards by UTC day** charts the same 30 days day by day, and its menu downloads them as CSV. The same page draws the wallet's closing CC balance by day, back up to 90 days on Pro, and lists its transfers: 50 on Free, more pages on Pro.

Rewards, transfers and burns come from the Loop Data API. **What moved** and **Rewards by UTC day** always cover 30 UTC days; Pro's 90 days are the balance line's. A read that stops at its page limit says so and counts "at least", and a read that fails says so instead of showing zero.
