The CCTools Portfolio lets you track any number of Canton wallet addresses in one place. You don't need to "connect" a wallet in the traditional sense — there's no signing, no permissions dialog, and no browser extension required. Just paste a public address and CCTools does the rest.

<Note>
  CCTools only reads publicly available Canton Network data. You never need to provide a private key or seed phrase. Only ever add public wallet addresses — your funds remain entirely in your control at all times.
</Note>

## Adding a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Portfolio page">
    Sign in to CCTools and navigate to [Portfolio](/portfolio). This is your central dashboard for all Canton wallet activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open My Wallets">
    Click the **My Wallets** button to open the wallet management panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new wallet">
    Click **Add Wallet**, then paste your Canton Network public wallet address into the input field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and load">
    Click **Save**. CCTools immediately begins loading your wallet data — your balance, rewards, and transaction history typically appear within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you see per wallet

Once a wallet is added, click on it to open its detail view. For each wallet you can see:

- **Current CC balance** — the live on-chain balance for that address
- **Staking rewards (30d summary)** — a snapshot of rewards earned over the last 30 days from delegating CC to validators
- **Recent transfers** — incoming and outgoing CC transactions with timestamps and counterparties
- **Balance chart** — a historical view of your CC balance over time
- **P&L** — profit and loss calculated against historical snapshots

## Multiple wallets

You can add as many wallets as you like — there's no cap on the number of Canton addresses you track. The top-level Portfolio view aggregates everything, showing your **combined total balance**, overall P&L, and period-over-period changes across all wallets at once. Click any individual wallet card to drill into its own dedicated detail view.

<Tip>
  Add all your wallets — including validator hot wallets — to get a complete picture of your Canton holdings. Seeing everything in one dashboard makes it far easier to spot reward patterns and track your total position.
</Tip>

## Refreshing data

CCTools keeps your Portfolio data up to date automatically, but you can request a manual refresh at any time. Click the **Refresh** button at the top of the Portfolio page to pull the very latest balances, rewards, and transfers from the Canton Network.

<Note>
  During periods of high Canton Network activity, a refresh may take a little longer than usual. If it doesn't complete right away, wait a moment and try again. The data shown in the meantime reflects your most recent successful sync.
</Note>

## Removing a wallet

To stop tracking a wallet, open **My Wallets**, locate the wallet you want to remove, and click the remove option. This only unlinks that address from your CCTools account — it has no effect whatsoever on the wallet itself or any funds held on-chain. You can always re-add the same address later.
