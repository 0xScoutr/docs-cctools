## Add a Canton Wallet

Navigate to **Portfolio** and click the **My Wallets** button. Select **Connect Canton wallet** and follow the wallet picker to link your wallet (Send or 5N Loop).

Once linked, CCTools automatically scans your wallet and displays:

- Balance in CC and USD
- Unlocked and locked amounts
- 7-day price change
- 30-day reward estimate

## Connect Cross-Chain Wallets

You can also connect EVM (Ethereum, Base, Polygon, etc.) and Solana wallets to your portfolio. In the **My Wallets** modal, add an EVM or Solana wallet by clicking **+ Connect** under the corresponding chain section.

Each wallet you connect:

- Is linked via signature verification (no seed phrases shared)
- Shows holdings and real-time balance
- Contributes to your unified portfolio net worth
- Adds a cross-chain score bonus to your Canton profile

## Wallet Limits by Plan

Free and Pro users have different wallet caps per chain:

- **Free plan**: 1 wallet per chain (Canton, EVM, Solana)
- **Pro plan**: Up to 5 wallets per chain

If you hit the free limit, upgrade to Pro to add more wallets.

## Manage Your Wallets

In the **My Wallets** modal, you can:

- **Rename** a wallet (up to 40 characters) - click the pencil icon
- **Set as primary** - mark a wallet as your main one with the star icon
- **Disconnect** - remove tracking from any wallet with the link-off icon

Disconnecting only removes tracking in CCTools and does not affect your actual wallet.

## What Your Portfolio Shows

Your unified portfolio displays:

**Total net worth**
- All Canton, EVM, and Solana wallets summed in USD
- Per-chain allocation bar showing balance distribution
- 30-day CC rewards estimate (Canton wallets only)
- Last updated timestamp

**Per-wallet detail pages**
Click any wallet card to see:
- Locked and unlocked CC breakdown
- Holding fees paid
- Daily rewards history chart (if you're running a validator or Featured App)
- Recent transfers and counterparties
- Wallet stats (reward/burn ratio, top trading partners)

**Note:** Detailed profit and loss charts are available to Pro users only. Free users see balances, rewards, and activity metrics for all connected wallets.

## Just Exploring?

If you don't want to connect a wallet yet, use the **Tracker** to inspect any Canton party ID or wallet address read-only. Click the **Track any Party ID** link in My Wallets or go to **Tracker** to get started.
