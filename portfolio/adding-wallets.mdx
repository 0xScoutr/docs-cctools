## Add a Canton wallet

Your Portfolio starts with a Canton wallet you connect. Until one is connected, **Portfolio** shows **Connect a Canton wallet**: click **Connect Canton wallet**, then pick your wallet in the **Connect a wallet** picker. For now it offers 5N Loop and Console, and on a phone 5N Loop connects there. A wallet that cannot sign on CCTools yet is marked **Can't sign yet**.

Send, Cantor8 and other wallets are not offered for a new connection for now. A wallet you already linked stays linked, with its record, and its row in **Manage wallets** reads **Can't sign yet**.

## Add EVM and Solana wallets

Once your Canton wallet is in, open **Manage wallets** on your Portfolio, or in **Settings > Plan and preferences**, and use **Connect an EVM wallet** or **Connect a Solana wallet**. EVM and Solana wallets sign one message to connect: no transaction and no fee. CCTools reads balances and activity, nothing else.

Wallets are always connected through the wallet app itself, never by pasting an address, so no seed phrase is ever shared. A wallet can be linked to one CCTools account only.

## Wallet limits by plan

- **Free**: 1 wallet per chain (Canton, EVM, Solana)
- **Pro**: up to 5 wallets per chain, 15 in all

When you reach your plan's limit, Manage wallets says so. [Pro](/account/pro-subscription) raises it.

## Manage your wallets

In **Manage wallets** you can rename a wallet, set one as the primary wallet of its chain, and disconnect a wallet. Disconnecting only stops CCTools from tracking it and does not touch the wallet itself. Disconnecting the signed wallet that makes your account Verified takes that proof away: you stay Verified only if CCTools' account check still passes without it. Removing your last Canton wallet sends the Portfolio back to its connect step.

## What your Portfolio shows

Your Portfolio puts your Canton, EVM and Solana wallets in one read-only view: your net worth and its value over time, your wallets, your positions and recent activity. Each wallet opens its own page. A Canton wallet's page loads 30 days of history on Free and 90 days on Pro, and the Portfolio's value chart covers up to 30 days for everyone.

## Just exploring?

If you don't want to connect a wallet yet, **Party lookup**, at [cctools.network/tracker](https://cctools.network/tracker) and in the footer, opens any Canton party read-only, without signing in.
