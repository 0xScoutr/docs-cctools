## Sign Up

Go to **cctools.network** and click **Sign Up** in the top right corner. You can create an account using:

- **Email + Password** - Standard email registration with email verification
- **Google** - One-click signup with your Google account
- **Twitter/X** - Connect your X account directly
- **Discord** - Sign up with your Discord account

After signing up, verify your email to activate your account.

## Connect Your Wallet

Once logged in, open **Portfolio**. Until a Canton wallet is linked the page shows a single gate titled **Connect a wallet to unlock your Portfolio**, and wallets can also be linked at any time from **Profile > Connections**. CCTools supports:

- **Canton** - Click **Connect Canton wallet** on that gate, then pick your wallet in the **Connect a wallet** picker (Send, 5N Loop and Cantor8 are among the options)
- **EVM and Solana** - Open **Profile > Connections**, click **+ Connect** under the chain, and sign a message to prove you own the address

Wallets are always connected through the wallet app itself, never by pasting an address, so no seed phrase is ever shared. Free accounts can connect 1 wallet per chain and Pro accounts up to 5. Once a Canton wallet is linked, the **My Wallets** button appears in the Portfolio header and manages every chain from one place. See [Adding Wallets](/portfolio/adding-wallets) for the full guide.

## Complete Your Profile

Head to **Profile** and **Settings** to finish setting up:

- Add a display name and upload an avatar under **Profile > Identity**
- Link X, Discord, Telegram, or Google under **Profile > Connections**, which authorizes the account instead of asking you to type a handle
- Enable notifications for the features you care about under **Settings > Notifications**
