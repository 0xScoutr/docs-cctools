## Sign up

Go to **cctools.network**, click **Sign in** at the top right, then **Create one** under the form. On **Create an account** you can sign up with:

- **Email and password** - then confirm your email from the message we send
- **Google**, **X** or **Discord** - with that account, in one step

If a friend sent you their invite link, the form shows the code it carries, and you can remove it before you create the account.

## Connect your wallet

Once you are signed in, open **Portfolio**. Until a Canton wallet is connected, the page shows **Connect a Canton wallet**: click **Connect Canton wallet**, then pick your wallet in the wallet picker (Send, 5N Loop and Cantor8 are among the options). EVM and Solana wallets can join your Portfolio after that, from **Manage wallets**, where you sign a message to prove you own the address.

Wallets are always connected through the wallet app itself, never by pasting an address, so no seed phrase is ever shared. Free accounts can connect 1 wallet per chain and Pro accounts up to 5. See [Adding Wallets](/portfolio/adding-wallets) for the full guide.

## Finish your profile

- Set your display name and photo with **Edit profile** on your **Profile**
- Connect X, Discord, Telegram, Google or GitHub in **Settings > Connected accounts**, which authorizes the account instead of asking you to type a handle
- Choose your alerts, and where they reach you, on [Alerts](/account/alerts)
- [Get Verified](/account/verified) if you want to take part in campaigns that ask for it
