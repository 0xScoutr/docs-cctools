Getting started with CCTools is fast and completely free. The sign-up process takes less than a minute — you can use your email address or log in directly with a social account you already have. Once you're in, you'll be able to connect your Canton wallets, start earning XP, and explore the full Canton Network ecosystem straight away.

<Note>
  CCTools will never ask for your private keys or seed phrases. When adding wallets to your portfolio, you only ever provide your **public wallet address**. Your funds remain entirely in your control.
</Note>

## Sign-up steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to cctools.network and click Launch App">
    Open [cctools.network](https://cctools.network) in your browser and click the **Launch App** button. This takes you to the main platform where you can sign up or log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your sign-up method">
    Create your account with **Email / Password**, or connect an existing account through one of the supported providers:

    - **Google** — one-click sign-in with your Google account
    - **X (Twitter)** — sign in with your X account
    - **Discord** — connect your Discord account
    - **Telegram** — sign in via Telegram

    All methods are equally supported. Choose whichever is most convenient for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email (email sign-up only)">
    If you signed up with an email address, check your inbox for a verification message from CCTools and click the confirmation link. This activates your account and ensures you can recover access later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first Canton wallet">
    Head to the **Portfolio** section and click **My Wallets**, then select **Connect Canton wallet**. Paste in your public Canton wallet address and CCTools will automatically scan it and display your balance, rewards, and recent activity.

    <Tip>
      You can skip this step and come back to it later — but adding a wallet immediately unlocks your portfolio dashboard and earns you your first XP bonus.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the platform">
    You're all set. The **Overview** dashboard is your starting point — you'll see the live CC token price chart, trending ecosystem projects, top leaderboard users, and your portfolio summary. From here, the entire platform is a click away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connecting social accounts

You don't have to link every social account at sign-up. You can connect or add accounts at any time from **Settings → Profile → Connected Accounts**. Supported providers are Google, X (Twitter), Discord, and Telegram.

Connecting social accounts gives you two benefits:

- **Flexible login** — use any connected account to sign in, so you're never locked out if one provider has an issue.
- **Community identity** — your linked accounts appear on your public CCTools profile, helping other community members recognize and connect with you across the ecosystem.

## Pro subscription

The vast majority of CCTools features are available completely free. A **Pro subscription** unlocks a layer of advanced capabilities for power users, including more wallet slots, detailed P&L history, tax report exports, advanced notification controls, curated Canton news, and a 1.25× XP multiplier on all actions.

<Info>
  You can explore the full platform and get genuine value from CCTools before deciding whether Pro is right for you. Upgrade anytime from Settings.
</Info>
