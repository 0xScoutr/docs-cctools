CCTools is the all-in-one toolkit built for everyone participating in the Canton Network ecosystem. Whether you're tracking your CC holdings, discovering the projects shaping the network, completing quests to earn rewards, or keeping an eye on on-chain governance — CCTools gives you a single, purpose-built dashboard to do it all. It's free to use, community-driven, and trusted by over 20,000 Canton Network users worldwide.

## Who uses CCTools

CCTools is home to a 20,000+ strong community of Canton Network participants — from first-time explorers to long-time validators and project builders. The platform itself is community-built: users contribute Academy articles, submit ecosystem projects, vote on content, run referral campaigns, and shape the leaderboard every day. Whether you're brand new to Canton or a seasoned ecosystem veteran, you'll find tools here that are relevant to where you are in your journey.

## Canton Network

CCTools is purpose-built for [Canton Network](https://www.canton.network/) — the enterprise-grade blockchain developed by Digital Asset that enables synchronized, privacy-preserving transactions across financial institutions and applications. Every piece of data on CCTools — portfolio balances, network stats, governance proposals, ecosystem projects — is Canton-specific. If you're active on Canton Network, CCTools is your home base.
