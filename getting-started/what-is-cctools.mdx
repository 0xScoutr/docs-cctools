## Overview

CCTools is the public record of the Canton Network. It publishes an open daily record of the network that anyone can download and cite, maps the projects building on Canton, follows the network's markets and governance, and gives you tools to track your own wallets.

Figures on CCTools show where they come from, and the [Methodology](https://cctools.network/methodology) page explains how each one is collected.

## What you can do

**Network** - The **Daily record**: network state, issuance and supply by day, read from Scan, with one open file per day to download and cite. Beside it: fees and rounds, super validators, every licensed validator ranked by its rewards, the next network upgrade and how many validators are ready, the featured apps, governance proposals and a rewards calculator.

**Ecosystem** - A directory of the Canton projects CCTools maps, with search and filters, a map grouped by sector, the projects added this month, categories, an A to Z list, side-by-side comparison, and the news about each project. Anyone can submit a project the directory misses.

**Markets** - DeFi TVL per protocol as DefiLlama reports it, tokenized assets, prices, stablecoins, bridges, wallets and custody, and where to get Canton Coin.

**Portfolio** - Your wallets on Canton, EVM and Solana in one view, with their balances and your CC rewards and transfers. **Party lookup** opens any Canton party read-only, without signing in.

**Library** - **Guides** (articles on how the network works), **Learn** (short lessons in order, each with a quiz) and **What's new**, the changelog.

**Developers** - The public API with keys and plans, an MCP server to connect an AI agent, the Methodology, and **Build on Canton**, a list of SDKs, tools and repositories.

**Campaigns** - Partners run campaigns on CCTools. Each campaign page states its tasks, its reward and who can claim a code or win a draw.

**Your profile** - Get Verified, check in each day, follow your XP and level, and share your public page.

**Ask Synchro** - The assistant on this docs site is Synchro, the same one as on cctools.network. It answers from the data CCTools keeps and from this documentation, with its sources listed.

## Who is it for?

- **Researchers, analysts and journalists** who need Canton Network figures they can cite
- **Builders and data teams** who read the network through the API
- **Holders and validators** who track wallets, rewards and the network's rules
- **Projects** that want to be found in the directory
- **Newcomers** learning how the network works
