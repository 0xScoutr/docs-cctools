## Sidebar Navigation

The left sidebar is your main navigation hub. Entries appear in this order, separated by thin dividers:

- **Overview** - Your personalized dashboard
- **Portfolio** - Wallet tracking and P&L
- **Tracker** - Inspect any Canton party ID or project, read-only and without logging in
- **Ecosystem** - Project directory, category filters, and Compare
- **Canton Map** - Visual network topology and relationships
- **Earn** - Quests, campaigns, and opportunities
- **Access** - Invite codes, allowlists, and gated onboarding from Canton projects
- **Markets** - DeFi, RWA, Stablecoins, Tokens, Get CC, Bridges, and Wallets
- **News** - Canton news feed and community posts, filterable by tag
- **Education** - Academy and Learn paths
- **Network** - Network stats, Validators, Featured Apps, Governance, Calculator, and DevTools
- **Leaderboard** - Community rankings across the User, Validators, Featured Apps, and Ecosystem boards

Entries that have sub-items open a flyout panel when you hover them. By default the sidebar sits as a narrow icon rail and expands on hover: click the pin icon in the top row, next to the logo and the theme toggle, to keep it open permanently.

## Global Search

Press Cmd+K (Mac) or Ctrl+K (Windows) to open the global search. Results are grouped into three sections:

- **Pages** - Specific pages and features
- **Projects** - Projects in the ecosystem
- **Earn Listings** - Live earn listings and campaigns

Search is the fastest way to find anything on CCTools. Documentation is not indexed there: use the search on this docs site instead.

## Ask Synchro - Docs Assistant

Click the "Ask Synchro" button (bottom right) to open the CCTools docs assistant. Synchro can help you with:

- Wallet setup and management
- XP and gamification systems
- Campaign mechanics and rewards
- Ecosystem and market questions

Synchro answers are sourced directly from the CCTools documentation. For the most accurate information, refer to the official docs.

## Settings and Profile

Use the footer section of the sidebar to:

- Access **Settings** for your account, notification, and appearance preferences (social integrations live under **Profile > Connections**)
- View your **Profile** with your engagement level and Pro status (if applicable)
- Sign in if you're not yet logged in
