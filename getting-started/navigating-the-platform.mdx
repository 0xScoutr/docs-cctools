## Sidebar Navigation

The left sidebar is your main navigation hub. It contains links to all major sections:

### Core Features
- **Overview** - Your personalized dashboard
- **Portfolio** - Wallet tracking and P&L
- **Tracker** - Monitor your positions and performance metrics

### Explore the Ecosystem
- **Ecosystem** - Project directory with hundreds of projects
- **Canton Map** - Visual network topology and relationships
- **Earn** - Quests, campaigns, and opportunities
- **Markets** - DeFi, RWA, stablecoins, tokens, and exchange options

### Stay Connected
- **News** - Canton news feed and community posts
- **Discover** - Validators and featured apps
- **Education** - Academy and structured learning paths

### Analyze and Govern
- **Network** - On-chain analytics, governance proposals, and calculators
- **Leaderboard** - Community rankings (standard, weekly, bracket)

### Other Features
- **$CC Token** - Token info, burn/mint charts
- **DevTools** - Developer utilities and integrations

You can collapse the sidebar by clicking the pin icon at the bottom to expand it only on hover, or pin it open permanently.

## Global Search

Press Cmd+K (Mac) or Ctrl+K (Windows) to open the global search. You can search for:

- Projects in the ecosystem
- Specific pages and features
- Help articles and documentation

Search is the fastest way to find anything on CCTools.

## Ask Synchro - Docs Assistant

Click the "Ask Synchro" button (bottom right) to open the CCTools docs assistant. Synchro can help you with:

- Wallet setup and management
- XP and gamification systems
- Campaign mechanics and rewards
- Ecosystem and market questions

Synchro answers are sourced directly from the CCTools documentation. For the most accurate information, refer to the official docs.

## Settings and Profile

Use the footer section of the sidebar to:

- Access **Settings** for account preferences and integrations
- View your **Profile** with your engagement level and Pro status (if applicable)
- Sign in if you're not yet logged in
