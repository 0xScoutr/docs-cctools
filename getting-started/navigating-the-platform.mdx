## The Overview dashboard

The Overview is the first thing you see when you land on CCTools, and it's designed to give you an instant read on what's happening across the entire Canton ecosystem. Here's what's on the page:

- **CC token price chart** — live price with a configurable time range so you can see how CC has moved recently.
- **Trending ecosystem projects** — the Canton projects gaining the most traction right now, based on community activity and upvotes.
- **Top leaderboard users** — a snapshot of the highest-ranked community members by XP, updated in real time.
- **Your portfolio summary** — if you're signed in and have wallets connected, your total net worth and recent changes appear directly on the Overview. Sign in to activate this.
- **Recently updated projects** — ecosystem projects with fresh information, new features, or recent milestones.
- **Recent earn listings** — the latest quests and opportunities available in the Learn & Earn section so you never miss a chance to earn XP.

<Tip>
  Bookmark the Overview page as your daily Canton check-in. Between the price chart, trending projects, and earn listings, it takes about 30 seconds to stay up to date with the network.
</Tip>

## Getting the most out of CCTools

Once your account is set up, these three actions will unlock the best of what CCTools has to offer:

**1. Add your wallets to Portfolio.**
Connecting your Canton wallet address is the single most impactful thing you can do. Your portfolio comes to life with live balances, reward estimates, and P&L data — and you'll earn XP just for connecting. Go to **Portfolio → My Wallets → Connect Canton wallet** to get started.

**2. Complete your first Learn & Earn quest.**
Head to the **Learn & Earn** section and pick a quest. Quests are short, practical, and rewarding — you'll earn XP and progress toward badges and leaderboard rankings from your very first one. Daily check-ins also keep your streak alive, which multiplies your XP earnings over time.

**3. Explore the Ecosystem.**
The **Ecosystem** directory has 470+ Canton projects spanning DeFi, RWA, infrastructure, wallets, and more. Browse by category, sort by score or newest, and upvote the projects you find interesting (requires Level 4). It's the best way to understand what's being built on Canton and to discover opportunities you didn't know existed.
