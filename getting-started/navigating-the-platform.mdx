## The top bar

The top bar holds five sections. Four open a menu, and **Portfolio** opens directly:

- **Ecosystem** - Directory, Map, New this month, News, Categories, A to Z and Compare, plus **Submit a project**
- **Markets** - DeFi, Tokenized assets, Tokens, Stablecoins, Bridges, Wallets and custody, and Get CC
- **Network** - Daily record, Fees and rounds, Super validators, Validators, Upgrades, Featured apps, Governance and Rewards calculator
- **Portfolio** - your wallets in one view
- **Library** - Guides, Learn and What's new, and under **Developers**: API, MCP server, Methodology and Build on Canton

To the right sit **Search CCTools**, **Campaigns** with the number of active campaigns, the notifications bell once you are signed in, and your account menu, or **Sign in**. Below 1024 px wide, the sections, Campaigns and your account move into the **Menu** button, which also holds the theme. On a wide screen the theme is in **Settings > Plan and preferences**.

## Search

Press **/** or **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on Mac), or click **Search CCTools**. Search finds pages, directory projects by name, campaigns by title or partner, and published guides. Paste a whole Canton party id to open it in **Party lookup**.

Documentation is not indexed there: use the search on this docs site instead.

## Your account menu

Once you are signed in, the account menu at the right of the top bar opens **Profile**, **Portfolio**, **API keys**, **Settings**, your plan (**Upgrade to Pro**, or **Pro plan** when you have it) and **Send feedback**.

## The footer

The footer repeats the main pages in four columns, **Explore**, **You**, **Developers** and **More**, with Party lookup, Pro, Campaigns, News, the [status page](https://status.cctools.network/), Contact, Privacy and Terms.

## Ask Synchro on this site

The **Ask Synchro** button at the bottom right of this docs site opens the CCTools docs assistant. It answers from the CCTools documentation; for anything it cannot settle, check the pages themselves.
