## The top bar

The top bar holds six sections. Each opens its main page, and its pages sit in the tab row under the top bar:

- **Ecosystem** - Directory, Map, New this month, News, Categories, A to Z and Compare, plus **Submit a project**
- **Markets** - Overview, then **Prices** (Tokens, Launchpad, Pairs and Venues) and **Sectors** (DeFi, Tokenized assets, Stablecoins, Bridges, and Wallets and custody)
- **Trade** - Swap, Bridge and Get CC
- **Network** - Network overview, Fees and rounds, Burned and minted, Super validators, Validators, Upgrades, Featured apps, Governance, Dev Fund and Calculator
- **Portfolio** - your wallets in one view
- **Library** - Guides, Learn and What's new, and under **Developers**: Data, API, MCP server, Methodology and Build on Canton

To the right sit **Search CCTools**, **Campaigns** with the number of active campaigns, the notifications bell once you are signed in, and your account menu, or **Sign in**. Below 1024 px wide, the sections, Campaigns and your account move into the **Menu** button, which also holds the theme. On a wide screen the theme is in your account menu and in the footer, and in **Settings > Plan and preferences**.

## Search

Press **/** or **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on Mac), or click **Search CCTools**. Search finds pages, directory projects by name, campaigns by title or partner, and published guides. Paste a whole Canton party id to open it in **Party lookup**.

Documentation is not indexed there: use the search on this docs site instead.

## Your account menu

Once you are signed in, the account menu at the right of the top bar opens **Profile**, **Leaderboard**, **Alerts**, **Portfolio**, **API keys**, **Settings**, your plan (**Upgrade to Pro**, or **Pro plan** when you have it) and **Send feedback**, then the theme and **Sign out**.

## The footer

The footer repeats the main pages in five columns, **Explore**, **Network**, **You**, **Developers** and **More**, with Party lookup, Pro, Campaigns, the Leaderboard, News, Synchro, the [status page](https://status.cctools.network/), Contact, Privacy and Terms.

## Synchro

Synchro is the CCTools assistant. The **Ask Synchro** button at the bottom right of every page of cctools.network and of this docs site opens it, and [cctools.network/synchro](https://cctools.network/synchro) is its full page. A block's menu has **Ask Synchro** too, with a question about that block.

It answers from what CCTools keeps: the daily record and network data, governance, the project directory, markets and token prices, campaigns, news, CIPs, the Canton developer reference and this documentation, and it lists its sources under each answer. Asked from a docs page, it starts from that page. Synchro is AI and can be wrong; for anything it cannot settle, check the pages themselves.

Synchro answers signed-in members: 3 answers a day, 10 once you are [Verified](/account/verified) and 100 with [Pro](/account/pro-subscription), counted again from 00:00 UTC. With Pro, your chats are saved to your account, to reopen and go on.
