If you have knowledge worth sharing — a Canton workflow you've figured out, a DeFi strategy you've tested, an ecosystem project you want to explain — the Academy is the right place for it. Publishing a post puts your insight in front of the entire CCTools community and earns you XP and recognition on your public profile.

## How to write and publish a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to CCTools">
    You need a CCTools account to submit a post. If you don't have one yet, sign up at [cctools.network](https://cctools.network). Signing in is also required to save drafts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the post writer">
    Go to [cctools.network/academy](https://cctools.network/academy) and click **Write a post**. This takes you to the submission form at `/academy/submit-post`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a format and fill in the details">
    Select your post type at the top — **Article**, **Guide**, or **Tutorial** — and the form will adapt to that format. Fill in:
    - **Title** (a slug is auto-generated from it)
    - **Cover image URL** (optional but recommended)
    - **Tags** — choose tags that match your content so readers can find it through filters
    - **Reading time** or **estimated time** — calculated automatically as you write
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your content">
    Use the built-in editor to write your post. The editor supports inline formatting: **bold** (`**text**`), links (`[label](url)`), and standard paragraphs separated by blank lines. An always-visible formatting help panel shows you exactly what syntax is supported. Note that raw HTML is stripped for safety.

    You don't have to finish in one session — drafts are saved automatically so you can step away and return.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    When you're happy with your post, click **Submit**. Your post enters the moderation queue for review by the CCTools team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Content guidelines

Posts that perform well and pass review tend to share a few qualities. When writing, aim to:

- **Stay relevant** — Write about Canton Network, DeFi on Canton, ecosystem projects, or CCTools platform features. Off-topic content will be declined.
- **Be accurate** — Double-check Canton-specific claims before publishing. The review process checks for factual accuracy, especially on technical topics.
- **Be original** — Don't copy content from other sources without proper credit. Paraphrased or summarised content must attribute the original clearly.
- **Be useful** — Write for a real audience. Posts that answer genuine questions or walk through real workflows earn the most upvotes.

## After publishing

Once your post is approved by the CCTools team, it goes live in the Academy feed immediately. From that point:

- Your post appears in the main Academy feed and is discoverable through type and tag filters
- Community members can upvote it — higher upvote counts push it toward the top of the Most Upvoted sort
- The post is credited to your public profile and counts toward your post total
- You earn XP for the published post, which counts toward your platform level

If the review team requests changes, you'll receive feedback and can edit your draft and resubmit. Submissions that are out of scope or too low-effort may be declined outright.

<Note>
  Posts are moderated by the CCTools team before going live. Content that violates [community rules](/community/community-rules) — including spam, inaccurate claims, or off-topic submissions — will be removed or declined.
</Note>
