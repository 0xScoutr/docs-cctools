## Community Submissions Are Not Open Yet

Academy does not accept community posts today. The **+ Write a post** button on the [Academy page](https://cctools.network/academy) opens a **Coming soon** placeholder, not a working editor, and nothing has ever been published through it.

Everything on Academy right now is written by the CCTools editorial team. The structured-content editor is still being refined, and community submissions open only once it ships. This page will be updated with the live flow when that happens.

If you already have a topic in mind, send it through **Feedback** and it goes into the queue for the first wave of community posts.

## What the Writer Will Ask For

The rest of this page is a preview of the format, not a form you can fill in today.

You pick whether you are writing an **Article** or a **Guide**, and the rest of the form adapts to the format you choose.

Common fields:

- **Title** and **slug** (auto-generated from the title)
- **Cover image URL**
- **Tags** (used for filtering and related-post matching)
- **Reading time** (or estimated time for guides - auto-calculated as you write)

Article-specific fields: kicker, reading focus, intro, sections (with optional callouts / pull-quotes / inline images), key points, takeaways, FAQ, resources, CTA.

Guide-specific fields: goal, prerequisites, what you'll build, numbered steps (each step with optional code / image / tip), troubleshooting, takeaways, resources, CTA.

The editor is block-based: you build the post section by section instead of writing one long text field. Unsafe HTML is stripped before anything is saved, so only a small set of formatting tags survives.

## How Review Will Work

Community posts will not go live on their own. Each submission enters a **review queue**, where the CCTools team checks for:

- Factual accuracy (especially Canton-specific claims)
- Originality (no copy-paste content without credit)
- Formatting sanity (images load, links work, structure follows the template)
- Relevance to the Canton Network ecosystem

Planned outcomes:

- **Approved** - Post goes live on Academy and is credited to your profile
- **Requested changes** - You get feedback and can edit and resubmit
- **Rejected** - Out-of-scope or low-effort submissions.

The first submissions will be walked through by hand to set the quality bar. Once a post of yours is approved and published, it earns XP, counts toward the post count on your public profile, and can be shared freely.
