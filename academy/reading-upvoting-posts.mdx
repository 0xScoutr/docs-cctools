The Academy feed is open to everyone. You don't need an account to read any published post — just visit [cctools.network/academy](https://cctools.network/academy) and start browsing. When you find posts worth surfacing to the rest of the community, upvoting takes one click.

## How to browse the Academy

The Academy landing page lists all published posts in reverse-chronological order by default. To find what you're looking for:

1. Visit [cctools.network/academy](https://cctools.network/academy)
2. Use the **Type** filter to narrow by format: **Article**, **Guide**, or **Tutorial**
3. Use the **Tag** filter to filter by topic — DeFi, governance, RWA, ecosystem projects, platform features, and more
4. Toggle the sort order between **Most Upvoted** and **Newest** depending on whether you want community-endorsed content or the latest additions

You can combine type and tag filters at the same time to drill down further.

<Tip>
  Sort by **Most Upvoted** to find the community's highest-rated guides — these are the posts other Canton users found most useful.
</Tip>

## What each post shows

In the feed, each post card displays:

- **Title** and a short excerpt so you know what you're clicking into
- **Author** name and level, linking to their public profile
- **Type** badge — Article, Guide, or Tutorial
- **Tags** relevant to the post's topics
- **Upvote count** showing how much the community values it
- **Reading time** or estimated completion time for guides

Click any card to open the full post. On the post page you'll also find related posts by the same author, posts sharing the same tags, and any ecosystem projects referenced in the content.

## Upvoting posts

Upvoting helps quality content rise to the top of the feed so other users can find it more easily. To upvote a post:

- Click the **upvote button** on any post card or on the post's own page
- You must be **signed in** to cast an upvote
- Upvoting requires **Level 4** on CCTools — keep engaging with the platform to reach it

A few things to know about how upvotes work:

- You have **one Academy upvote per UTC day**, shared across all posts — not per post
- The counter resets at **midnight UTC**; a small timer on the upvote button shows when your next slot opens
- You can **remove your upvote** on the same day to free up your slot and vote on a different post instead

This limit keeps upvotes a meaningful signal of genuine quality rather than a meaningless click count.

## Sharing posts

Every post has a share panel with one-click options for X, Telegram, and copy-to-clipboard. Post URLs are public and shareable — anyone can read them whether or not they have a CCTools account.
