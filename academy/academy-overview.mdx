## The Guides shelf

**Guides**, in the **Library** menu, is the shelf of reading on CCTools, at [cctools.network/academy](https://cctools.network/academy). It holds articles on how the Canton network works and on projects in its ecosystem, with a search and topics to narrow the list.

Guides are explainers in plain language with numbered sources, and many show figures read from the CCTools daily record when the page loads, so those figures do not go stale. Guides join the shelf as they are published; today it holds the articles. An article that a guide rewrites keeps its place and its address until that guide is published.

**Learn** is the interactive half of the Library: short lessons in order, each with a quiz. See [Learn](/earn/learn-paths).

## Reading an article

Each article opens on its own page, and anyone can read it without signing in. The page shows:

- **Facts in this article**, when it has them: each fact is read from its source and dated, and its number points to that source at the end of the article
- **On this page**, the contents, kept in view beside the text on wide screens
- **Where to look next**, which can point to the matching Learn lessons
- **About this article**, which says who wrote it, with links to the Methodology and to **Suggest a correction**
- **Share** and **Cite**

## Writing for the Library

The Library does not take community posts. If you have a topic in mind or spot an error, tell us through the article's **Suggest a correction**, which opens [Feedback](https://cctools.network/feedback) and needs level 8, or through the [Contact](https://cctools.network/contact) page at any level.
