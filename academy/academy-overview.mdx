The CCTools Academy is your go-to destination for community knowledge about the Canton Network ecosystem. Whether you're just getting started or looking to deepen your understanding, the Academy brings together posts written by real users — practitioners, developers, and DeFi participants — who share what they know.

## What you'll find

Every post in the Academy is written either by the CCTools editorial team or by a community author who has gone through the review process. Content spans the full breadth of the Canton ecosystem:

- **Articles** — Long-form editorial reads covering Canton Network concepts, market dynamics, and ecosystem developments
- **Guides** — Step-by-step, hands-on tutorials that walk you through a specific task, from setting up your portfolio to participating in DeFi pools
- **Tutorials** — Focused walkthroughs on platform features, ecosystem projects, and Canton tooling

Each post shows the author's name, level, and profile link — so you know who wrote it and can explore more from the same contributor.

## Two ways to use the Academy

You can engage with the Academy as a reader or as a contributor:

- **Reading** — Browse and read all posts without signing in. No account is required to access any published content.
- **Upvoting and writing** — Upvoting posts and publishing your own content both require a CCTools account. Upvoting is available to Level 4 users and above.

## How to navigate the Academy

Visit [cctools.network/academy](https://cctools.network/academy) to see the full feed of published posts. From there:

- Use the **Type** filter to show only articles, guides, or tutorials
- Use the **Tag** filter to narrow posts by topic — such as DeFi, governance, RWA, ecosystem projects, or platform features
- Switch between **Most Upvoted** and **Newest** to sort by community endorsement or recency

Individual post pages also surface related content automatically: other posts by the same author, posts sharing the same tags, and ecosystem projects referenced in the post.
