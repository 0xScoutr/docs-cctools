XP — experience points — is the core currency of CCTools' gamification layer. Every meaningful action you take on the platform earns you XP, which accumulates on your profile to determine your level, fuel your leaderboard rank, and unlock recognition across the community. The more you learn, explore, and engage, the more your XP grows.

## Earning XP

XP flows from several different sources across CCTools. The table below covers the main ways to build your total:

| Action | XP earned |
|---|---|
| Complete a Learn & Earn quest | 6 XP |
| Finish all quests in a learn path (completion bonus) | +8 XP (38 XP total per path) |
| Daily check-in streak | Bonus XP per consecutive day |
| Streak milestone (7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 100, 180, 365 days) | Escalating bonus XP + badge |
| Confirmed referral (friend completes all tasks + reaches Level 4) | Bonus XP per referral |
| Upvoting ecosystem projects | XP per upvote (daily limit applies) |
| Exploring the ecosystem | XP for viewing projects (resets daily) |
| Completing partner campaigns | XP varies by campaign |
| One-time actions (e.g. connecting a wallet, following on X) | Fixed XP, awarded once |

**Pro subscribers** receive an XP multiplier on all actions, so every activity earns proportionally more.

<Note>
  Streak XP and upvote XP reset daily at midnight UTC. Missing a day resets your streak back to zero, so consistency is the fastest path to streak bonuses.
</Note>

## Your level

Your cumulative XP determines your level, which goes from 1 up to 50. Each level bracket carries a title that reflects your depth of engagement with the Canton ecosystem:

| Levels | Title |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Newcomer |
| 6–10 | Explorer |
| 11–15 | Analyst |
| 16–20 | Strategist |
| 21–25 | Operator |
| 26–30 | Architect |
| 31–35 | Validator |
| 36–40 | Governor |
| 41–45 | Syndicate |
| 46–50 | Canton OG |

**Level 4 is a key milestone.** Reaching it unlocks upvoting, your active referral code, and badge eligibility. The XP required to advance increases progressively with each level, so early levels feel fast while higher tiers reward long-term commitment.

## Checking your XP

You can find your current XP total in two places:

- **Your profile** — your XP and level are shown prominently in your profile header, visible to anyone who visits your [public profile](/account/public-profile).
- **The leaderboard** — your rank entry shows your XP alongside your streak, referral count, and top badges. Sign in to see your own position highlighted in the rankings.

## XP and the leaderboard

Your XP total is the default sort order for the [CCTools leaderboard](/earn/leaderboard). The leaderboard also supports sorting by referral count and streak length, giving you multiple ways to compete and compare. Time filters (All-time, This Month, This Week) let you focus on recent momentum rather than just historical totals.

<Tip>
  The fastest way to accumulate XP early on is to complete a full Markets learn path — each one is worth 38 XP and takes only a few minutes per quest. Start with [Learn & Earn paths](/earn/learn-paths) to get going.
</Tip>
