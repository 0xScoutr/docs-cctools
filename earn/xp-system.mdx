## Earning XP

XP (Experience Points) is the core engagement metric on CCTools. You earn XP by:

- **Daily check-in** - Log in every day to earn streak XP with milestone bonuses
- **Upvoting projects** - Earn XP for voting on ecosystem projects (daily limit applies)
- **Viewing projects** - Earn XP for exploring the ecosystem
- **Following @cantontools on X** - One-time XP reward
- **Adding a wallet** - XP for connecting your portfolio
- **Referring friends** - XP when your referral completes all tasks and reaches Level 4
- **Completing campaigns** - XP varies by campaign

## Daily Resets & Streaks

Some XP actions reset daily at midnight UTC:

- **Daily check-in** - Earns base XP + streak bonus
- **Upvotes** - Limited number of upvotes per day
- **Project views** - View XP resets daily

**Streak milestones** reward bonus XP and exclusive badges at key intervals (7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 100, 180, and 365 days). The longer your streak, the bigger the rewards.

Missing a day resets your streak, so consistency is key!

## Levels

Your total XP determines your level (max Level 50). Each level has a title:

- **Level 1-4** - Newcomer
- **Level 5-9** - Explorer
- **Level 10-14** - Analyst
- **Level 15-19** - Strategist
- **Level 20-24** - Operator
- **Level 25-29** - Architect
- **Level 30-34** - Validator
- **Level 35-39** - Governor
- **Level 40-44** - Syndicate
- **Level 45-50** - Canton OG

**Level 4** is the key milestone: it unlocks upvoting, referrals, and badge eligibility. XP required per level increases progressively.
