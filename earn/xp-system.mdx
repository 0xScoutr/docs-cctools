## What XP is

XP opens access and exclusive activities on CCTools. It is not a prize and it is not money. Your total XP sets your level, from 1 to 50.

**How XP works**, at [cctools.network/xp](https://cctools.network/xp), lists every source of XP with the amounts the code pays, and what each level opens.

## Where you see it

- **Profile > Overview** - the **Level** figure shows your level, your XP and how much XP is left to the next level
- **Today** and **This week** on your Profile - your daily quests and weekly challenges, what each one pays, and the one thing to do next
- **Profile > Activity > Check-in and XP** - your latest XP events, your check-ins over the last 12 weeks, your longest streak, your completed campaigns and your earlier badges
- **Learn** - each lesson shows the XP it gives, and each path shows what completing it earns
- **Campaigns > Ranking** - your place in the season's ranking

## What earns XP

- **Daily check-in** - once a UTC day, from your Profile
- **Streak milestones** - each time a streak reaches a milestone
- **Today and this week** - the daily quests and weekly challenges on your Profile
- **Learn** - each right answer, once, and each finished path
- **Campaign tasks** - each task you finish in a campaign, and completing the campaign, as the campaign sets
- **Actions on X** - follow and repost tasks in a campaign
- **Votes and visits** - votes on projects, and visits to projects, campaigns, proposals and comparisons
- **First visits** - the first time you open Learn, Markets, the calculator or your invite link
- **Invites** - for you and a friend you invited, as they take their first steps
- **Connected accounts** - the first time you connect an account or add a wallet
- **Your project, your tool or your edit** - when a moderator accepts it
- **Sending feedback** - once a week
- **Discord help tickets** - a bug, a suggestion or a helpful answer
- **Milestones** - one-time steps: linking accounts and wallets, your first lesson, First steps, and activity on Discord

## Check-ins and streaks

One check-in a day counts, from 00:00 UTC, and it keeps your streak going. A missed day ends the streak. The **Check-in** card shows your current streak, your best one and the days you checked in this month.

With [Pro](/account/pro-subscription) you can recover a missed day from the same card, up to 3 times a month. A recovered day keeps the streak and gives no XP.

## What level 8 opens

These actions open at **level 8**:

- Voting for projects in the directory
- Sending feedback
- Suggesting a tool for **Build on Canton**

Below level 8, a live campaign you joined still lets you vote for its project. Until then, you can write to us on the [Contact](https://cctools.network/contact) page. Some campaigns also ask for a level to join: each campaign page says its rule, and [How winners are chosen](/campaigns/how-winners-are-chosen) explains each kind.

## The ranking

The **Ranking** tab of Campaigns lists the XP earned since Season 1 started, Verified accounts only. It resets each season. It carries no prize. See [The ranking](/campaigns/how-campaigns-work#the-ranking).

## Badges

**Profile > Activity > Check-in and XP** holds two lists:

- **Completed campaigns** - the campaigns you completed, each with its day
- **Earlier badges** - the badges you earned before campaign badges, each with its day. They stay on your profile

CCTools no longer gives badges for levels, streaks, Learn paths or Pro. The one-time milestones still pay their XP. Your public page does not draw your earlier badges, but its public data carries them while the page is public.
