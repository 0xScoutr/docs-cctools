## What XP is

XP counts what you do on CCTools, and your total XP sets your level, from 1 to 50. XP is never reset, and no CCTools page ranks members against each other.

## Where you see it

- **Profile > Overview** - the **Level** figure shows your level, your XP and how much XP is left to the next level
- **Profile > Activity > Check-in and XP** - your latest XP events, your check-ins over the last 12 weeks, your longest streak and your badges
- **Learn** - each lesson shows the XP it gives, and each path shows what completing it earns

Campaign pages show no XP. The tasks you complete there still add to it, and they appear among your XP events.

## What earns XP

Your XP events show what added up to your total. They include:

- **Daily check-in** - one a day, from the **Check-in** card on your Profile
- **Learn** - each quiz question you answer right, once, and each path you complete
- **Campaign tasks** - the tasks you complete in a campaign
- **The directory** - opening project pages and voting for projects
- **Badges** - a badge can carry XP of its own

## Check-ins and streaks

One check-in a day counts, from 00:00 UTC, and it keeps your streak going. A missed day ends the streak. The **Check-in** card shows your current streak, your best one and the days you checked in this month.

With [Pro](/account/pro-subscription) you can recover a missed day from the same card, up to 3 times a month. A recovered day keeps the streak and gives no XP.

## What level 8 opens

These actions open at **level 8**:

- Voting for projects in the directory
- Marking interest in a campaign
- Sending feedback
- Suggesting a tool for **Build on Canton**

Below level 8, a live campaign you joined still lets you vote for its project and mark interest in it. Until then, you can write to us on the [Contact](https://cctools.network/contact) page. Some campaigns also ask for a level to join: each campaign page says its rule, and [How winners are chosen](/campaigns/how-winners-are-chosen) explains each kind.

## Badges

The badges you earn are listed, each with the day you earned it, in the **Badges** block of **Profile > Activity > Check-in and XP**. They come from levels and streak milestones, from the Learn paths and campaigns that list one, and from Pro, which has three of its own: Pro Member, Whale Tracker and Portfolio Master. Your public page does not draw your badges, but its public data carries them while the page is public.
