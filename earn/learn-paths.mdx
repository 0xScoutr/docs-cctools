Learn & Earn turns Canton Network education into a rewarding experience. Instead of reading passive documentation, you work through curated sequences of short lessons — each capped with a quiz that tests your understanding and deposits XP directly into your account. The more you learn, the more you earn, and the more confidently you can navigate the Canton ecosystem.

Each path is structured around a specific topic, so you can go deep on the areas that matter most to you without wading through unrelated content.

## How learning paths work

A learn path is made up of individual **quests** — focused lessons with a quiz at the end. Here's what to expect:

- Each path typically contains **5–6 quests**, worth **6 XP each**
- Completing all quests in a path earns a **completion bonus**, bringing the total to **38 XP per path**
- Quests are completed in order — each one unlocks the next
- Your progress is saved automatically, so you can pause and pick up where you left off
- You must pass the quiz to receive the XP for each quest

To start a path, go to the **Learn** section in the sidebar, browse the available paths, and click any path to open its overview and quest list.

<Note>
  Your overall completion percentage, quests completed, and total quests available are all tracked on the Learn page so you always know how far you've come.
</Note>

## Progress tracking

The **Learn** page gives you a complete view of where you stand across all paths:

- **Overall completion %** — how much of the total Learn & Earn content you've finished
- **Quests completed / total** — a running count across every path
- **Per-path progress** — each path shows its own completion bar so you can see at a glance which ones are done, in progress, or untouched
- **Difficulty filter** — filter paths and quests by difficulty level to find content that matches where you are
- **Progress filter** — surface only the paths you haven't started yet, or jump straight to ones you're partway through

## Difficulties

Each quest is tagged with a difficulty level — beginner, intermediate, or advanced. If you're new to Canton, start with beginner quests to build your foundation before moving to more technical content. Difficulty labels are shown on each quest card in the Learn page.

<Tip>
  If you're new to Canton Network, start with **Markets: Get CC** — it's designed for first-timers and covers everything you need to know about acquiring and using Canton Coin. You'll walk away with 38 XP and a solid foundation to explore the rest of the ecosystem.
</Tip>
