## What Learn is

**Learn**, in the **Library** menu at [cctools.network/learn](https://cctools.network/learn), teaches the Canton network in short lessons, each with a quiz. Lessons are grouped in paths, and the paths in three groups: **Foundations**, **Markets** and **Partner paths**. The page also suggests an order for the core paths.

## How a path works

1. **Read the lesson.** Every lesson is open to read, signed in or not. Its quiz opens once the lesson's reading time has passed.
2. **Pass the quiz.** Pass it and the next lesson in the path opens. A question you missed opens again after the wait shown on the lesson.
3. **Complete the path.** A lesson is complete when every question has been answered right, and a path when every lesson is.

Sign in to take the quizzes, keep your place in each path, and earn the XP and badges each path lists. Your paths, done and in progress, are in **Profile > Activity > Learn**.

## What a path earns

Each question you answer right earns its XP once. Completing a path earns its XP and, when it lists one, its badge: the path's page shows both under **On completion**. See [XP, levels and badges](/earn/xp-system) for where they show.

Some campaigns ask you to complete a Learn path as one of their tasks.
