## Badge Types

Badges are collectible achievements that showcase your engagement. There are several categories:

**Adopter Badges** (limited supply, all require Level 4 with organic XP):
- **OG Adopter** - First 500 users (fully claimed)
- **Early Adopter** - First 2,000 users
- **Pioneer** - First 10,000 users
- **Day One** - Accounts created before May 2026

**Level-Up Badges** (earned automatically at milestones):
- Level 5 - First Steps
- Level 10 - Rising Star
- Level 20 - Power User
- Level 35 - Whale Status
- Level 50 - Canton OG

**Streak Badges** (earned for consecutive daily check-ins):
- 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 100, 180, and 365 day streaks

**Activity Badges** (earned through platform engagement):
- **X Follower** - Follow @cantontools on X
- **Eco Explorer** - View multiple different projects
- **Deep Diver** - Visit all major sections of the platform
- **Comparator** - Use the compare feature multiple times
- **Opp Hunter** - Show interest in multiple opportunities
- **Opp Scout** - Suggest an opportunity
- **Verified Scout** - Get multiple opportunity suggestions approved

**Referral Badges** - Awarded at referral milestones as you invite more users.

## How badges appear on your profile

Your three rarest badges are featured automatically at the top of your profile. The rest appear below in the All Badges grid. There is no manual showcase to arrange, and no difference between free and Pro.

Badges are displayed with their rarity color on your profile and leaderboard entry.
