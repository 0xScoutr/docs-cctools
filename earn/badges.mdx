Badges are achievement markers you earn through activity on CCTools. Each badge tells a story about how you've engaged with the Canton ecosystem — whether that's showing up every day for a year, introducing friends to the network, or finishing every quest in a learning path. Badges appear publicly on your [profile](/account/public-profile) and on your leaderboard entry, giving your engagement a visible identity beyond just a number.

## How to earn badges

Badges are grouped into categories based on the type of activity they reward.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Adopter badges" icon="star">
    These limited-supply badges reward the earliest members of the CCTools community. All adopter badges require **Level 4** with organically earned XP.

    | Badge | Condition |
    |---|---|
    | OG Adopter | First 500 users (fully claimed) |
    | Early Adopter | First 2,000 users |
    | Pioneer | First 10,000 users |
    | Day One | Account created before May 2026 |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Quest and path completion badges" icon="graduation-cap">
    Finishing Learn & Earn paths can award dedicated completion badges. These recognise that you've worked through a full topic area — not just dipped in for the XP. Watch for completion badge eligibility on each path's overview page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Streak badges" icon="flame">
    Streak badges are awarded automatically when you hit consecutive daily check-in milestones. The longer you maintain your streak, the rarer the badge you unlock.

    Streak milestones: **7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 100, 180, and 365 days**

    Each milestone also grants escalating bonus XP on top of the badge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Level-up badges" icon="trending-up">
    You earn these automatically when you reach key XP levels on your journey from Newcomer to Canton OG.

    | Badge | Level |
    |---|---|
    | First Steps | Level 5 |
    | Rising Star | Level 10 |
    | Power User | Level 20 |
    | Whale Status | Level 35 |
    | Canton OG | Level 50 |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Referral badges" icon="user-plus">
    Referral badges are awarded as you reach cumulative referral milestones — the more confirmed referrals you have, the higher the tier you unlock. See the [referral program](/earn/referral-program) for details on what counts as a confirmed referral.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Activity badges" icon="zap">
    These badges reward specific platform behaviours and one-time actions:

    | Badge | How to earn |
    |---|---|
    | X Follower | Follow @cantontools on X |
    | Eco Explorer | View multiple different ecosystem projects |
    | Deep Diver | Visit all major sections of the platform |
    | Comparator | Use the compare feature multiple times |
    | Opp Hunter | Show interest in multiple Earn opportunities |
    | Opp Scout | Suggest an opportunity |
    | Verified Scout | Get multiple opportunity suggestions approved |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Rarity

Every badge has a rarity tier, displayed as a colour on your profile and leaderboard entry. Rarity reflects how few users have earned that badge — early adopter badges are among the rarest, while activity badges are more broadly accessible. Rarer badges signal a higher level of commitment and expertise in the Canton ecosystem, and they stand out visually when others browse the leaderboard.

## Showcasing badges on your profile

You can choose which badges to display prominently on your profile. Free users can showcase up to **3 badges**, and Pro users can showcase up to **6**. The rest of your earned badges are still recorded and visible in your full badge collection.

Badges are shown publicly if you have **Show activity stats** enabled in your Privacy settings. To control this, visit [Account → Public Profile](/account/public-profile).

<Info>
  New badges are added as CCTools launches new learning paths, campaigns, and ecosystem milestones. Check the platform announcements so you don't miss a limited-time opportunity to earn a rare badge.
</Info>
