The CCTools referral program rewards you for growing the community. Every time a friend joins through your unique referral code and completes their onboarding, you earn XP — and your referral count climbs the leaderboard for everyone to see. It's one of the most impactful ways to accumulate XP while genuinely helping people discover the Canton Network.

## How to refer friends

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reach Level 4">
    Your referral code only becomes active once you reach **Level 4**. Complete quests, maintain a streak, and explore the ecosystem to hit the milestone quickly. See the [XP system](/earn/xp-system) for the fastest paths to Level 4.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your referral code">
    Sign in to CCTools and open your profile or the leaderboard section. Your unique referral code is displayed there in the format `CC-XXXXXX`. Copy the code or grab the pre-formatted referral link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Send your referral link to friends directly, post it on social media, or drop it in community channels like Discord. Anyone who signs up using your code is linked to your referral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    The referral starts in **pending** status. It becomes confirmed once your friend completes all five required onboarding tasks and reaches Level 4 themselves. When that happens, you receive your referral XP.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your friend needs to do

Signing up with your code is just the first step. For the referral to be **confirmed** — and for you to receive the XP bonus — the person you referred must complete all five onboarding tasks:

1. Follow **@cantontools** on X
2. Maintain a **2-day daily streak**
3. Add a **wallet** to their portfolio
4. **Upvote 3** ecosystem projects
5. **Show interest in 3** Earn opportunities

Each task also earns your friend their own XP, so it's a genuine head-start for them too.

<Note>
  Only new sign-ups who complete all five tasks and reach Level 4 count as confirmed referrals. Existing users who click your link do not count, and self-referrals are not permitted. CCTools has anti-abuse systems in place to ensure fairness.
</Note>

## Referral leaderboard

The [leaderboard](/earn/leaderboard) has a dedicated **Referral** sort, ranking users by their total confirmed referral count. Rankings are updated every 10 minutes, so your count climbs quickly after a friend completes their tasks. Top referrers gain prominent community recognition and may qualify for special campaigns or partner rewards that target high-referral users.

## Tracking your referrals

Your confirmed referral count appears in two places:

- **Your public profile** — visible to anyone who visits your profile page
- **The leaderboard** — shown on your rank entry when the Referral sort is active

You can check pending vs. confirmed referrals from your profile dashboard to see how close your friends are to completing their tasks.

<Tip>
  Sharing your referral link in the CCTools Discord is a great way to reach active Canton community members who are already interested in the ecosystem — and therefore more likely to complete all five tasks quickly.
</Tip>
