CCTools is more than a Canton Network tracker — it's a rewarding experience for every interaction you have with the ecosystem. As you explore, learn, and engage, you accumulate XP (experience points) that build your level, sharpen your leaderboard rank, and unlock badges that mark your milestones. Whether you prefer diving into learning paths, growing the community through referrals, or simply showing up every day, there's a way to earn that fits how you use CCTools.

Here's a quick look at all the ways you can earn on the platform.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Complete Quests" icon="graduation-cap" href="/earn/learn-paths">
    Work through guided Learn & Earn paths covering Canton Network topics like tokens, DeFi, RWA, and more. Each quest you complete earns you **6 XP**, and finishing an entire path rewards a bonus — totalling **38 XP per path**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Refer Friends" icon="user-plus" href="/earn/referral-program">
    Share your unique referral link and earn bonus XP for every friend who signs up and completes the onboarding tasks. Your referral count is tracked publicly on the leaderboard.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Build a Daily Streak" icon="flame" href="/earn/xp-system">
    Log in and engage with CCTools every day to build a consecutive-day streak. Longer streaks earn bonus XP and unlock exclusive streak badges at key milestones like 7, 30, and 100 days.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community Campaigns" icon="megaphone" href="/campaigns/how-campaigns-work">
    Take part in partner campaigns from projects across the Canton ecosystem. Campaigns award XP, badges, and sometimes exclusive rewards for completing a set of sponsored tasks.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where to find earning opportunities

All active opportunities — campaigns, Learn & Earn paths, and partner quests — are listed on the **Earn** page. You can filter by status (Active, Upcoming, Ending Soon, or Permanent) to find what's available right now. Verified opportunities are marked with a badge, and sponsored items are highlighted at the top.

To track your progress, visit your [public profile](/account/public-profile), where your XP total, level, badges, and streak are all displayed. The [leaderboard](/earn/leaderboard) shows how you rank against other CCTools users in real time.

<Tip>
  New to CCTools? Start with a Learn & Earn path to earn your first XP quickly. The **Markets: Get CC** path is designed for newcomers and covers the basics of Canton Coin in a handful of short quizzes.
</Tip>
