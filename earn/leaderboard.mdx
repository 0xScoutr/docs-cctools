The CCTools leaderboard is the platform's public ranking system, showing who is most engaged, most knowledgeable, and most active across the Canton ecosystem. Rankings are refreshed every 10 minutes, so your position moves as you earn XP, confirm referrals, and maintain your streak. Whether you're chasing the top spot or just curious where you stand, the leaderboard gives you a live view of the community's energy.

## Leaderboard tabs

The leaderboard is divided into four tabs, each covering a different slice of the Canton ecosystem:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Users" icon="user">
    Individual CCTools users ranked by their activity. This is the main competitive leaderboard — your XP, streak, and referral totals all feed into your position here.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Validators" icon="shield-check">
    Canton Network validators ranked by their on-chain activity and engagement. A useful reference for understanding who is most active in securing the network.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Featured Apps" icon="layout-grid">
    Canton ecosystem apps that are featured on CCTools, ranked by community engagement metrics. A good way to discover which projects are gaining the most traction.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ecosystem" icon="globe">
    A broader ranking of Canton ecosystem projects, giving you a wider view of relative activity and growth across the network.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Sort options

Within the **Users** tab, you can sort rankings three ways:

| Sort | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **XP** *(default)* | Total experience points earned on CCTools |
| **Referral count** | Number of confirmed referrals |
| **Streak length** | Current consecutive daily check-in streak |

Switching between sort options lets you see who leads in each dimension — the top XP earner might not be the same person with the longest streak or most referrals.

## Time filters

Every leaderboard view supports three time filters:

- **All-time** — cumulative totals since each account was created
- **This Month** — XP and activity earned in the current calendar month
- **This Week** — XP and activity earned in the last seven days

Time filters let you discover who has been most active recently, not just who joined earliest. If you're a newer user, **This Week** or **This Month** gives you a much more competitive playing field.

## Your ranking

When you're signed in, your own position is highlighted in the leaderboard so you can see exactly where you stand without scrolling. Your entry displays:

- Your display name and avatar
- Your current level and level title
- Your total XP (or referral count / streak, depending on sort)
- Your top 5 rarest badges
- Your confirmed referral count

To improve your rank, focus on completing [Learn & Earn quests](/earn/learn-paths) for steady XP, inviting friends through the [referral program](/earn/referral-program), and showing up daily to protect and extend your [streak](/earn/xp-system).

<Tip>
  Switch to the **This Week** filter to see who's most active right now. It's a great way to spot engaged community members to connect with — head to the [CCTools Discord](/community/discord) and say hello.
</Tip>
