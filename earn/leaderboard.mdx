## How Rankings Work

The leaderboard ranks users by total XP earned. Positions are not live to the second: the all-time boards are served from a short cache, so new XP shows up a couple of minutes later. The weekly and monthly boards work differently, they read a pre-computed snapshot that is rebuilt once a week (on Monday) and once a month (on the 1st), and the weekly board covers the week that has just closed rather than the one in progress.

The leaderboard displays:

- Your current rank
- Display name and avatar
- Level and level title
- Total XP
- Current streak
- Your 3 rarest badges
- Confirmed referral count

## Boards and Sorts

The page has four boards:

- **User** - individual members
- **Validators** - validator rewards ranking
- **Featured Apps** - app rewards ranking
- **Ecosystem** - combined ecosystem engagement

Inside the **User** board you choose how to sort:

- **XP** - total XP earned
- **Referral** - confirmed referrals (all-time only)
- **Streak** - current daily check-in streak (all-time only)

The **XP** sort adds a period filter with three options: **All-time**, **This Month** and **This Week**. Referral and Streak have no period filter, they are always all-time.

## Does XP Ever Reset?

No. XP and badges are permanent, and there is no seasonal reset planned. CCTools is built as a reputation platform, so wiping the board would punish the members who built the longest history.

If you want a shorter window, the **XP** sort already gives you **This Month** and **This Week**, which rank recent activity without touching anyone's all-time total.
