## How Rankings Work

The leaderboard ranks users by total XP earned. Your position is updated in real-time.

The leaderboard displays:

- Your current rank
- Display name and avatar
- Level and level title
- Total XP
- Current streak
- Top 5 rarest badges
- Confirmed referral count

There are multiple leaderboard views:

- **Standard** - All-time total XP
- **Weekly** - XP earned in the last 7 days
- **Bracket** - XP earned in the last 30 days

## Seasons (Coming Soon)

In the future, CCTools will introduce seasonal leaderboards that reset periodically. This gives everyone a fresh start and rewards consistent engagement over time.

Season rewards may include exclusive badges, early access features, and more.
