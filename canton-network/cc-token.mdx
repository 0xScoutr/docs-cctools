## How Canton Coin works

**Canton Coin (CC)** is the native currency of Canton Network.

- **Issuance**: CC is issued every minting round on a schedule that steps down: 40 billion a year for the first six months, 20 billion to year 1.5, 10 billion to year 5, 5 billion to year 10, then 2.5 billion a year with no end. CC has no maximum supply.
- **Who receives it**: each round's issuance goes to featured apps, super validators, validators (by the CC they burn for traffic) and the Development Fund (CIP-0082). A reward is minted only when its coupon is claimed in time; unclaimed rewards are not minted.
- **Burn**: CC is burned when validators buy synchronizer traffic, the fee Canton Network charges for submitting transactions
