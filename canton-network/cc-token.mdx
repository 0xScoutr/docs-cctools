## Token Economics

The **CC token** is the native currency of Canton Network.

- **Ten-year issuance cap**: 100 billion CC. Canton has no fixed max supply; CC is emitted continuously on a halving schedule, and this cap is what the network stats page measures circulating supply and FDV against.
- **Issuance**: CC is minted every mining round as rewards to Super Validators, validators and featured apps, and 5% of each mint goes to the Development Fund (CIP-0082)
- **Burn**: CC is burned when validators buy synchronizer traffic, the fee Canton Network charges for submitting transactions
- **Halving**: Minting rewards are reduced periodically following a halving schedule

## Where to Track CC

CCTools provides real-time CC token data on the **Network Stats** page:

- Current price and 24h change
- Market cap and circulating supply
- Historical price charts
- CC burned and featured app rewards
- Halving countdown
