## Validators Page

The **Validators** page lists validators on the Canton Network ranked by the rewards they have minted, grouped by the ecosystem project they are linked to when CCTools knows the link. For each validator you can see:

- Validator name and identity
- Role (Super Validator or regular Validator)
- Performance metrics and rewards data

## Super Validators

Super Validators operate the Global Synchronizer, the shared infrastructure that orders and confirms Canton Network transactions, and they govern it:

- Voting on Canton Improvement Proposals (CIPs), which cover protocol changes, tokenomics, standards, and Super Validator membership and weights
- Voting on-ledger on network configuration, such as synchronizer traffic fees and the CC/USD conversion rate used in each mining round

CIPs and their status are public at [github.com/canton-foundation/cips](https://github.com/canton-foundation/cips).

## Governance

CCTools tracks all governance activity on the **Governance** page:

- Active and past proposals
- Voting status and results
- Super Validator participation
- Approval rate and execution status
- Network parameter changes

This gives the community full transparency into how the network is governed.
