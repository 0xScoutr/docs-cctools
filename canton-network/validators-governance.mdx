## Validators

**Validators**, at [cctools.network/validators](https://cctools.network/validators), starts with the latest day of the daily record: **Validator licenses**, **Validator rewards** and the **Reward per CC of coupon**, beside the number of **Super validators** from the governance state, read every hour, and a chart of validator licenses by day. A license says nothing about whether its validator was active.

The **Validator rewards ranking** lists every licensed validator party by its validator rewards over the last 30 complete UTC days, or over its lifetime, from Loop Data, measured once a day. You can search it and filter it to super validators or the others, and to parties linked to a directory project or not. Each row shows its rank, its project when the directory links one, its rewards in CC and its share of the period. The ranking downloads as CSV, and each party opens a page of its own.

## Super validators

**Super validators**, at [cctools.network/super-validators](https://cctools.network/super-validators), shows how many there are, the votes a proposal needs, the total reward weight and the day's super validator rewards, before each member's split to its beneficiaries. Each member is listed with its reward weight, its share of the weight, its share of the day's super validator rewards at that weight, its latest price vote and the round it joined. **Votes on open proposals** shows each member's vote on each open proposal.

Each super validator casts one vote, whatever its weight, and each round's super validator rewards are split by reward weight.

## Featured apps

**Featured apps**, at [cctools.network/apps](https://cctools.network/apps), counts the featured app rights at the end of the day and their app rewards, how many were featured by a governance vote and how many are linked to a directory project. It charts the rights by day and lists the grant and removal votes of the last 30 days. Rights are granted per provider party, so an app run from several parties holds several.

## Governance

**Governance**, at [cctools.network/governance](https://cctools.network/governance), follows the proposals the super validators vote on: the votes in progress, the proposal list as Loop Data lists it (**Open**, **Executed**, **Rejected** or **Expired**) with a search by requester, app or reason, the super validators, the reference price and its votes beside the market price, the voting threshold, and the outcomes. The approval rate counts executed proposals out of decided ones.

The proposals and the requesters' summaries come from Loop Data; each vote, the super validators, their weights and their price votes come from Canton Scan. CIPs and their status are public at [github.com/canton-foundation/cips](https://github.com/canton-foundation/cips).

## Upgrades

**Upgrades**, at [cctools.network/network/upgrades](https://cctools.network/network/upgrades), shows the next MainNet upgrade (what it requires, its protocol version and its on-chain schedule), how many active validators are ready, the active validators by version and the next upgrades on the calendar. **Check your validator** takes your validator party ID.

## Dev Fund

**Dev Fund**, at [cctools.network/dev-fund](https://cctools.network/dev-fund), follows the Canton Development Fund: the share of every round's issuance that goes into it, the grants approved and committed, what has been paid, the milestones paid, the RFPs on the roadmap, the milestone votes closing next and the latest payments. Each proposal and each RFP has a page of its own, and the grants, milestones and proposals download as CSV.

**On the ledger** sets what left the fund each UTC day, read from Scan, beside the payments the Foundation's notices give. It lists the latest allocations found on the ledger, each with its update ID, its grant and milestone, and the allocations by grant team, linked to the directory.

Every block names its source and when it was read, and the [Methodology](https://cctools.network/methodology) page explains how each figure is collected.
