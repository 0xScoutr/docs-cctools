## Three Calculators

CCTools provides three rewards calculators to help you estimate potential earnings:

- **App Builder Calculator** - Estimate rewards for building applications on Canton Network based on app coupon issuance rates
- **Validator Calculator** - Calculate potential validator rewards based on delegation amount and validator coupon rates
- **CC Holder Calculator** - Estimate holding rewards based on your CC token balance

Access the calculator from the sidebar under **Calculator** or from the **Rewards** page.

## How Calculations Work

The calculators use real-time network data including:

- Current issuance rates per round (Super Validator, Validator, App coupons)
- Number of halvings completed
- Current CC price

Results are estimates based on current network conditions and may vary as the network parameters change over time.
