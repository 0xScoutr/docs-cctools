## Four Calculators

CCTools provides four calculators, selected from the tabs at the top of the page:

- **$CC Token** - Convert between CC and fiat in either direction at the live price, with an advanced mode for break-even targets and price scenarios
- **Featured App** - Estimate your monthly CC rewards as a Canton Featured App based on your user count and transaction volume
- **Validator** - Model your node economics: node count, uptime, setup and server cost, against the live validator coupon rate
- **Investor** - Project your CC portfolio value at different price targets. Canton has no passive staking, so returns come from price appreciation

Access the calculator from the sidebar under **Network > Calculator**.

## How Calculations Work

The calculators use real-time network data including:

- Current issuance rates per round (Super Validator, Validator, App coupons)
- Number of halvings completed
- Current CC price

Results are estimates based on current network conditions and may vary as the network parameters change over time.
