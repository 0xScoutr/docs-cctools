The CCTools Calculator lets you estimate potential returns from Canton Network participation — whether you're running a validator, building an app, or holding CC. Rather than relying on generic yield figures, the Calculator pulls real Canton Network issuance parameters and applies them to your inputs, giving you a grounded projection of what your involvement could earn over time.

You can reach the Calculator from the home page under **More Tools**, or directly at [cctools.network/calculator](https://cctools.network/calculator).

<Info>
  The Calculator uses real Canton Network parameters — current round issuance rates, halving stage, and validator tier data — and is updated automatically as the network evolves.
</Info>

## Who the calculator is for

The Calculator covers three distinct participation models, each with its own set of inputs and outputs:

**1. Validators**

If you're running or considering running a Canton validator node, the Validator calculator lets you model CC rewards based on:

- Your stake size and validator tier (Super Validator vs. regular Validator)
- Current per-round issuance rates for your tier
- Number of halvings completed to date
- Projected network growth and round frequency

This is particularly useful if you're evaluating the economics of becoming a validator, or if you want to understand how your expected rewards will change as the halving schedule progresses.

**2. App builders**

If you're building an application on Canton Network, the App Builder calculator estimates the CC you can earn through:

- App coupon issuance — Canton Network rewards app builders for driving transaction volume through their applications
- Transaction fee flows generated by your app's user activity
- Reward program participation based on your app's category and engagement level

Use this calculator to build a revenue model for your Canton app before or during development.

**3. CC holders**

If you hold CC and are considering staking delegation (assigning your stake to a validator in exchange for a share of their rewards), the CC Holder calculator projects potential returns based on:

- Your CC balance
- The delegation terms offered by validators on the network
- Current issuance rates and halving stage

This helps you compare different validators' delegation offers and understand what a given balance could earn over a chosen time horizon.

## How to use the calculator

1. Go to [cctools.network/calculator](https://cctools.network/calculator) — it's linked from the home page under **More Tools**
2. Select your participation type: **Validator**, **App Builder**, or **CC Holder**
3. Enter your parameters in the input fields (stake size, CC balance, expected transaction volume, etc.)
4. Review the projected returns broken down by round, day, month, and year
5. Adjust your inputs to model different scenarios — for example, comparing returns at the current halving stage versus the next

The Calculator displays results in both CC and USD equivalents, using the current CC price pulled from the network in real time.

## Important notes

Canton Network is a live, evolving system. The Calculator gives you a useful baseline, but actual rewards depend on several factors that can shift over time:

- **Network activity** — total transaction volume affects how much CC is burned versus minted, influencing the network's overall token economics
- **CC halving schedule** — each halving reduces per-round issuance, which directly lowers projected validator and app builder rewards
- **Validator performance** — rewards are tied to uptime and participation in governance rounds; underperforming validators earn less
- **Delegation competition** — as more CC holders stake, the per-holder share of validator rewards may change
- **Network parameter changes** — the GSF and Super Validators can adjust network configuration through governance proposals

<Warning>
  Calculator outputs are estimates only and do not constitute financial advice. Actual returns may be higher or lower than projected depending on network conditions, your operational performance, and other factors outside CCTools' control. Always do your own research before making investment or infrastructure decisions.
</Warning>
