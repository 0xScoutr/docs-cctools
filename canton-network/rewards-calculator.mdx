## Three estimates

The **Rewards calculator**, in the **Network** menu at [cctools.network/calculator](https://cctools.network/calculator), estimates what the network's reward rules give, for one of three roles picked at the top of the page:

- **Featured app** - for your app, type the marked transactions a day, the marker weight per transaction, the traffic per transaction in bytes and your hosting cost, and choose how many markers all other apps create. You get the estimated app rewards (the reward per marked transaction, the rate per CC of coupon, your share of the day's markers and the net a day after traffic and hosting), and **Fee vs reward** for one marked transaction: the network fee, the marker weight, the app reward and the weight to fee ratio the FA Overage Policy watches. If your own node buys the traffic, tick the box and the estimate adds the validator reward on it.
- **Validator** - for your node, type the traffic it buys in MB a day and your hosting cost, and choose how much traffic all other nodes buy. **What your node gets back** shows the reward back on the CC it burns for traffic, the net cost a MB and the liveness reward.
- **CC holder** - for any amount of CC, **What holding gets from the protocol** shows its value, its share of the supply and how the supply moved. The protocol pays holders nothing: new CC goes to featured apps, validators and super validators for the work they do on the network, so the protocol reward for holding is 0 CC. The page also explains the holding fee, which is never taken from a balance, and the locks some roles require, which pay nothing for holding.

## Prices and inputs

Each estimate runs at a price you pick: the **reference price**, the CC price the super validators vote and the network converts at; the **market price**, traded across markets; or **your price**, a scenario you type.

The inputs come from the network itself: the traffic price, the marker value and the reward rules from the hourly read of Canton Scan, and issuance, coupons and rewards from the latest day of the [daily record](/network/network-stats), shown under **Inputs from the record**. When an input cannot be read, or is too old, the calculator gives no estimate rather than guess.

App rewards are estimated from activity markers, while the latest read of the network names them as its minting method. If the minting version changes, the calculator says so and gives no app reward figure until it models the new method.

## What it leaves out

Each role ends with **What this estimate leaves out**: the rate moves every round, featured status takes a vote of the super validators and a lock under CIP-0116, rewards arrive as coupons that have to be minted in time, and more. The results are estimates, not a forecast and not advice. The page's link keeps your inputs, so you can share a scenario.
