Canton Network is an enterprise-grade blockchain built by [Digital Asset](https://www.digitalasset.com/) — the company behind the Daml smart contract language. Unlike general-purpose public blockchains, Canton was designed from the ground up for institutions and users who need strong privacy guarantees, compliance-friendly architecture, and the ability to transact across applications atomically. CCTools is built entirely around Canton Network, giving you purpose-built tools to track, earn, govern, and explore this ecosystem.

## Built for institutions and users

Most blockchains offer a simple trade-off: either everything is public (Ethereum), or the network is closed (permissioned enterprise chains). Canton takes a different approach with its **sub-transaction privacy model** — only the parties directly involved in a transaction can see its details, while the overall network still benefits from shared state and composability.

This design makes Canton uniquely attractive to regulated financial institutions. Major global banks and asset managers run Canton validators not just as infrastructure operators, but as active participants in a shared financial network. For end users, this means you interact with a blockchain that has institutional-grade security and uptime behind it — while still being able to use DEXes, wallets, and DeFi apps just as you would on any other network.

Key properties of Canton Network:

- **Sub-transaction privacy** — transaction data is only visible to the parties involved
- **Atomic cross-app transactions** — applications can transact with each other in a single atomic step
- **Daml smart contracts** — all Canton apps are powered by Daml, a purpose-built smart contract language with built-in privacy semantics
- **Institutional validator set** — major financial institutions run the infrastructure

## Daml smart contracts

Every application on Canton Network — from DEXes to wallets to real-world asset platforms — is built using **Daml** (Digital Asset Modeling Language). Daml is a functional smart contract language that encodes privacy natively: contracts specify exactly which parties are signatories, observers, and controllers, which means privacy rules are enforced at the language level rather than bolted on afterward.

For you as a user, this means Canton apps have predictable, auditable behavior. If you're an app builder or developer, Daml's composability lets your contracts interact directly with other Canton contracts — enabling complex multi-party workflows without going through a centralized intermediary. CCTools tracks all Daml-powered projects in the Ecosystem directory.

## The Canton ecosystem

More than **470 projects** are building on Canton Network, spanning virtually every vertical in digital finance:

- **DEXes and trading** — Cantex, OneSwap, and others provide on-chain spot trading for CC and other Canton assets
- **Institutional platforms** — Temple Digital Group, Fairmint, and similar projects bring tokenized securities and real-world assets (RWA) to Canton
- **Wallets** — Bron, Send, and other wallets let everyday users hold and transfer CC and other Canton tokens
- **Infrastructure and bridges** — cross-chain bridges connecting Canton to Base, Ethereum, and Solana are in development
- **DeFi and stablecoins** — lending protocols, stablecoin issuers, and liquidity providers are expanding the Canton DeFi stack

You can explore the full directory on the [Ecosystem](/ecosystem/browsing-projects) page — filter by category, status, or role, upvote projects you're following, and track new entrants to the network.

## Why CCTools is Canton-specific

CCTools was built by the community, for the community. Every feature on the platform is tailored to Canton Network:

- **Portfolio tracking** is Canton-native — it reads directly from Canton party data, not a generic multi-chain indexer
- **Governance monitoring** tracks Super Validator votes, price consensus rounds, and on-chain proposals specific to Canton's governance model
- **Network Stats** show Canton-specific metrics like the burn/mint ratio, CC halving countdown, and per-validator uptime
- **Ecosystem directory** covers Canton projects exclusively, with CCTools scores based on Canton-specific activity signals
- **Rewards Calculator** models CC rewards using the actual Canton Network issuance parameters — not generic yield estimates

Because CCTools is single-network focused, every data point you see is directly relevant to your Canton activity. There's no noise from other chains.
