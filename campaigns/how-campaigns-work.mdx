## What are campaigns?

Partners run campaigns on CCTools. Each campaign page states its tasks, its reward and who can claim a code or win a draw. A reward can be an access code, a link, a place in a draw or a prize the partner delivers.

## The Campaigns page

Open **Campaigns** from the top bar, where it shows how many are active, or from the footer: [cctools.network/earn](https://cctools.network/earn). The page has three tabs: **Active**, **Ended** and **Ranking**. In **Active** and **Ended** you can narrow the list by kind (**Access code**, **On-chain**, **Learn path**, **Social tasks** and **Partner offer**) or search by name or partner. Each row shows the campaign's rule in short, such as **Verified to win** or **Level 8+**.

Code campaigns show how many codes are left:

- **Plenty left** - more than half the codes
- **Some left** - a fifth to a half
- **Few left** - under a fifth
- **Sold out** - none left

## Joining a campaign

1. Open the campaign and read its tasks and its rule. A rule checked when you join comes first, in a **Before you start** card
2. Click **Join campaign** (**Sign in to join** if you are signed out)
3. Complete the tasks, in any order. Once you have joined, the top of the page counts the required tasks that are done, and **Continue** takes you to the next one. Optional tasks carry an **Optional** chip
4. **Complete your entry**, the last row of the list, finishes it. When every required task is done and you meet the rule, it reads **Entry complete**. If the rule asks for Verified and you are not Verified yet, use **Get Verified** (**See how to get Verified** on a draw marked **account check**), then press **Complete**

What happens next depends on the campaign:

- a **code or link** campaign hands out its reward the moment your last required task is verified, while codes last
- a **draw** enters you once every required task is done, and its winners are drawn after the campaign ends. A **Verified to win** rule is checked when the draw runs

Some campaigns have a participant limit, so join early to secure your spot. Your campaigns, with their state and the one thing left to do, are in **Profile > Activity > Campaigns**. See [How winners are chosen](/campaigns/how-winners-are-chosen) for the rules and the draw.

## Completing a campaign

A campaign is completed when every required task is done and you meet its rule. Completing asks for no wallet signature. When the campaign sets completion XP, completing pays it once, on top of the XP of each task. Your completed campaigns are listed under **Completed campaigns** in **Profile > Activity > Check-in and XP**, and **Campaigns completed** on your Profile counts them. Campaigns that ended before this rule stay as they were.

## Claiming the campaign badge

The badge of a campaign you completed is yours once you sign for it with your wallet. Under **Complete your entry**, the campaign page shows **Claim your badge**, and **Claim** in **Completed campaigns** on your Profile leads there.

1. Press **Sign with your wallet**
2. Pick a wallet linked to your account that can sign, and approve one message in it

The signature moves nothing: no transaction and no fee. There is no deadline, even after the campaign ends, and your XP does not wait for it: it counted when you completed. With no wallet linked, **Add a wallet to claim** lets you add one and brings you back. If your wallet cannot sign yet, the campaign stays completed, and you can claim from another wallet whenever you like. On a phone, if the signature does not come through, open CCTools on a computer to sign.

A claimed badge shows **Signed** in **Completed campaigns**, with its day and its season, and your public page lists it under **Campaign badges** with the campaign and the day, never the wallet.

## Watching a campaign

**Watch**, the eye beside **Share** on a campaign page, tells you when the campaign goes live, is about to end, ends and when its winners are out. It pays no XP and shows no count, and only you can see what you watch. Press **Watching** to stop, or use **Campaigns you watch** on [Alerts](/account/alerts). The eye goes away once there is nothing left to tell you.

## The ranking

The **Ranking** tab, at [cctools.network/earn?tab=ranking](https://cctools.network/earn?tab=ranking), holds the Season 1 ranking: XP earned since the season started, Verified accounts only. The ranking resets each season and carries no prize.

- **The rows** - the top 100, each with its level and its XP. Your own row is marked, and it is added below them when you are lower
- **Your place** - your number, how many are on the ranking, who stands just above and just below you, and the XP it takes to pass the next member
- **Move up** - the ways to earn XP from here, each with what it pays
- **Earlier seasons** - the final rows of a season that ended

The ranking is counted every hour. Milestones, linked accounts, invites and XP given by staff do not count. It reads CCTools' account check, not a wallet signature (see [Getting Verified](/account/verified)). Private pages and banned or flagged accounts are not listed.

A campaign page has its own **Ranking** card under the tasks when its tasks pay XP: the XP from that campaign's tasks, Verified accounts only. With the same XP, who finished first ranks higher. It carries no prize, and in a campaign with a draw it does not change your chance in the draw.

## Task types

- **Follow** - follow an account on X
- **Like + RT** - like and repost a post on X
- **Post** - publish your own post on X that meets the campaign's requirements
- **Upvote** - vote for the partner's project in the directory
- **Visit** - visit a page or website
- **Join now** - join the partner's Discord server or Telegram group
- **Sign up** - subscribe to the partner's email list
- **Star** - star a GitHub repository
- **Verify** - complete a Learn path, or do something real on Canton (a swap, a trade, a paid call) and have it checked against the partner's endpoint with your party id
- **Submit** - send the text the campaign asks for
- **Complete** - another task the partner defines

Tasks that check an account need it connected in **Settings > Connected accounts**. Voting for a project opens at level 8, but a live campaign you joined lets you vote for its own project at any level.
