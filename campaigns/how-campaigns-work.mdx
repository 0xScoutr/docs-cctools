## What are campaigns?

Partners run campaigns on CCTools. Each campaign page states its tasks, its reward and who can claim a code or win a draw. A reward can be an access code, a link, a place in a draw or a prize the partner delivers, and some campaigns also award a campaign badge.

## The Campaigns page

Open **Campaigns** from the top bar, where it shows how many are active, or from the footer: [cctools.network/earn](https://cctools.network/earn). The page has two tabs, **Active** and **Ended**. You can narrow the list by kind (**Access code**, **On-chain**, **Learn path**, **Social tasks** and **Partner offer**) or search by name or partner. Each row shows the campaign's rule in short, such as **Verified to win** or **Level 8+**.

Code campaigns show how many codes are left:

- **Plenty left** - more than half the codes
- **Some left** - a fifth to a half
- **Few left** - under a fifth
- **Sold out** - none left

## Joining a campaign

1. Open the campaign and read its **Before you start** card, task zero: the rule that decides who can take part, and whether it is checked when you join or when the draw runs
2. Click **Join campaign** (**Sign in to join** if you are signed out)
3. Complete the tasks, in any order. Once you have joined, **Your progress** shows how many required tasks are done, and optional tasks carry an **Optional** chip
4. What happens next depends on the campaign:
   - a **code or link** campaign hands out its reward the moment your last required task is verified, while codes last
   - a **draw** enters you once every required task is done, and its winners are drawn after the campaign ends

Some campaigns have a participant limit, so join early to secure your spot. Your campaigns, with their state and the one thing left to do, are in **Profile > Activity > Campaigns**. See [How winners are chosen](/campaigns/how-winners-are-chosen) for the rules and the draw.

## Task types

- **Follow** - follow an account on X
- **Like + RT** - like and repost a post on X
- **Post** - publish your own post on X that meets the campaign's requirements
- **Upvote** - vote for the partner's project in the directory
- **Mark interest** - mark interest in the campaign
- **Visit** - visit a page or website
- **Join now** - join the partner's Discord server or Telegram group
- **Sign up** - subscribe to the partner's email list
- **Star** - star a GitHub repository
- **Verify** - complete a Learn path, or do something real on Canton (a swap, a trade, a paid call) and have it checked against the partner's endpoint with your party id
- **Submit** - send the text the campaign asks for
- **Complete** - another task the partner defines

Tasks that check an account need it connected in **Settings > Connected accounts**. Voting for a project and marking interest open at level 8, but a live campaign you joined lets you do both for its own project at any level.
