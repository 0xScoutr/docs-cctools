CCTools Campaigns are time-limited reward programs run in partnership with Canton ecosystem projects. Each campaign gives you a structured way to engage with a project — through on-chain actions, community tasks, or both — and earn real rewards in return. Campaigns are designed to reward genuine participation, not luck.

## What campaigns involve

Every campaign is built around a set of tasks that reflect how the partner project wants users to engage. Depending on the campaign, tasks may include:

- **Bridging assets** — moving tokens between chains (for example, Base → Canton or Canton → another network)
- **Using a DEX** — swapping tokens or providing liquidity on a Canton exchange
- **Upvoting or rating projects** — casting verified votes on CCTools ecosystem listings
- **Sharing on social media** — posting about a project or campaign via connected social accounts
- **Holding a minimum balance** — maintaining a token balance in your Canton wallet by a snapshot date
- **Providing liquidity** — depositing into a pool for a sustained period to qualify for pool-based rewards

Campaign requirements are always listed on the campaign page before you commit to any action.

## How to join a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find active campaigns">
    Check the **Earn** section or the CCTools home page for campaigns that are currently open. Each listing shows the reward pool, task summary, and deadline at a glance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the requirements">
    Open the campaign detail page and review every task. Pay attention to minimum amounts (for bridges or swaps), eligibility rules (wallet age, KYC status), and the deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the required tasks">
    Carry out each task with the wallet you want rewards sent to. On-chain tasks are tracked automatically by wallet address — make sure you use the same wallet throughout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your rewards">
    After the campaign closes, rewards are distributed based on verified on-chain activity or confirmed task completion. You don't need to submit a claim form for most campaigns — rewards are sent directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reward types

Campaigns offer a variety of reward structures depending on what the partner project chooses to offer:

| Reward type | Description |
|---|---|
| **CC (Canton Coin)** | Native Canton Network token distributed to qualifying participants |
| **XP** | CCTools experience points that contribute to your profile rank and unlock platform perks |
| **Project tokens** | Tokens native to the partner project, distributed to campaign winners or top performers |
| **Whitelist spots** | Early access or allowlist positions for upcoming project launches or token sales |

Some campaigns combine multiple reward types — for example, top performers might receive both CC and project tokens.

## Campaign duration

Every campaign has a published start date and end date. Tasks completed outside this window are not eligible for rewards. Check the campaign page for the exact deadline, and aim to complete tasks well before the close date to avoid last-minute indexer delays affecting your verification.

<Warning>
  On-chain tasks must be completed and confirmed on-chain before the campaign deadline. Pending transactions at the time of campaign close may not be counted.
</Warning>

## Real campaign examples

**OneSwap Asset Relayer Campaign** — Users bridge assets from Base to Canton (or Canton to Base) using OneSwap's Asset Relayer. Bridged volume is verified on-chain, and participants who meet the minimum bridge amount earn CC rewards. This campaign rewards hands-on use of Canton's cross-chain infrastructure.

**Temple Leaderboard Rewards** — Temple runs a rolling leaderboard that ranks active market makers by verified on-chain settlement volume. The top performers each period earn CC proportional to their contribution, making it a competitive campaign for more active traders and liquidity providers.
