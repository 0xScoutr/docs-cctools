CCTools offers sponsored campaign placements for Canton ecosystem projects that want to drive user engagement, on-chain activity, or community growth. A sponsored campaign puts your project in front of an audience that is already active on Canton Network and looking for ways to earn and participate — making it one of the most targeted ways to acquire genuine on-chain users.

<Note>
  Sponsored campaigns require a Canton Network project. The CCTools team reviews all applications for relevance and quality before a campaign goes live.
</Note>

## What a sponsored campaign includes

When you run a sponsored campaign on CCTools, you get:

- **Featured placement** on the CCTools Earn and Campaigns pages — your campaign is shown prominently to logged-in users
- **Promotion to 20,000+ active users** — CCTools reaches a large base of Canton Network participants who regularly engage with campaigns
- **On-chain verification infrastructure** — CCTools handles task tracking and wallet-level verification so you don't need to build it yourself
- **Results tracking** — view participation numbers, task completion rates, and on-chain activity metrics during and after the campaign

Your campaign runs for a defined period, with rules and reward structures you define. CCTools handles the distribution mechanics and user experience.

## Types of campaigns

Sponsored campaigns can be structured around a variety of engagement goals:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Trading Volume" icon="chart-line">
    Reward users who generate swap or settlement volume on your platform over the campaign window. Leaderboard or threshold-based structures both work.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bridge Campaigns" icon="bridge">
    Incentivize users to move assets to or from Canton Network through your bridge. Verified by bridge transaction on-chain.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Referral Campaigns" icon="users">
    Grow your user base by rewarding participants who bring new wallets into your ecosystem. Tracked by referral link and on-chain first activity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quest Completion" icon="list-check">
    Combine multiple tasks — social follows, on-chain actions, upvotes — into a structured quest. Users earn rewards for completing the full set.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Custom structures are also possible. If your campaign idea doesn't fit neatly into one of these categories, describe your goals when you apply and the CCTools team will work with you.

## How to apply

Getting a sponsored campaign on CCTools is straightforward:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reach out to the CCTools team">
    Submit your interest via the [feedback form]("https://cctools.network/feedback") or join the CCTools Discord and message the team directly. Include a brief description of your project and what you want the campaign to achieve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your campaign goals">
    Tell the team what action you want users to take, what rewards you're offering, your target audience, and your preferred campaign window. The more detail you provide, the faster the review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approval">
    The CCTools team reviews applications for Canton Network relevance, project quality, and reward legitimacy. You'll hear back with feedback or an approval within a few business days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Campaign setup and launch">
    Once approved, the team sets up the campaign page, configures on-chain verification, and schedules the featured placement. You'll have a chance to review everything before launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The most successful sponsored campaigns combine an on-chain action (bridging, swapping, providing liquidity) with a meaningful reward. Users on CCTools are sophisticated — they respond to campaigns that require real engagement and offer fair compensation.
</Tip>
