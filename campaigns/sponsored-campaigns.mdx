## Types of Sponsorship

CCTools offers several ways for projects to gain visibility:

- **Banner** - Prominent placement at the top of the platform, visible across all pages
- **Sponsored Ecosystem Listing** - Your project appears highlighted in the Ecosystem directory with a "Sponsored" badge
- **Sponsored Earn Listing** - Your opportunity appears highlighted in the Earn section
- **Campaign with Tasks** - A fully custom campaign with tasks, badges, and rewards for users

All sponsored content is clearly labeled so users know what is organic and what is promoted.

## For Projects

If you're a project team looking for visibility in the Canton ecosystem, visit **cctools.network/contact** and select "Sponsored Listing" or "Partnership" as the subject.

Include:

- Your project name and description
- Which type of sponsorship interests you
- Desired duration
- Links (website, Twitter, docs)

We'll get back to you to discuss details and pricing.
