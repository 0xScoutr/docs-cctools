## Types of sponsorship

CCTools offers several ways for projects to reach its readers:

- **Banner** - a labelled placement at the top of some pages
- **Sponsored directory listing** - your project is listed first in the Ecosystem directory, with a **Sponsored** label. See [Sponsored listings](/ecosystem/sponsored-listings)
- **Sponsored campaign listing** - your listing is highlighted on the Campaigns page
- **Campaign with tasks** - a campaign with its own tasks and rewards for members

All sponsored content is clearly labelled, so readers know what is organic and what is promoted.

## For projects

If you are a project team, open the [Contact](https://cctools.network/contact) page and choose **Partnership**.

Include:

- Your project's name and what it does
- Which type of placement you have in mind
- How long you want it to run
- Your links (website, X, docs)

We'll get back to you to discuss details and pricing.
