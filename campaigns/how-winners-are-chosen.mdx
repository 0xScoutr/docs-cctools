## Three ways to give out rewards

When a campaign has a limited number of rewards (for example, a fixed number of codes or prizes from the partner), CCTools gives them out in one of **three ways**, chosen with the partner:

- **Draw** - Everyone who completes all the required tasks enters a random draw. Winners are picked at random after the campaign ends. Doing the tasks earlier gives you no advantage: every valid entry has the same odds.
- **First come, first served** - The reward goes to the first members to finish all the required tasks, in order, until none are left. Here speed matters: finishing early secures your spot.
- **Ranked winners** - Prizes are attached to positions: first place gets one prize, second place another, and so on. Winners are ordered by the criterion the campaign states up front (for example, the quality of a post you submitted), so it is neither a random draw nor a race. A single campaign can have ranked winners and a draw at the same time.

The [leaderboard](/campaigns/how-campaigns-work#the-leaderboard) is something else: it carries no prize and chooses no winner.

How the prize reaches you also varies. Some campaigns hand out a **redemption code** you claim on the campaign page, and some are **external**, meaning your name goes on the published winners list and the partner delivers the prize off-platform (an on-chain payment, for instance).

The campaign page tells you which one applies: a draw lists its prizes and how many winners it has in its **Prizes** card, ranked prizes show their positions, and a code campaign shows how many codes are left.

## Who can take part and win

Each campaign page states its rule and when it is checked. A rule checked when you join comes first, in a **Before you start** card. A rule checked when the draw runs is in **Complete your entry**, the last row of the tasks. Campaign rows show the same rule in short:

- **Level N+** - joining asks for level N or higher on CCTools, checked when you join
- **Level N+ or Verified** - either one lets you join
- **Verified to join** - joining asks for a Verified account
- **Verified to win** - anyone can join and finish the tasks, but only Verified accounts can win, checked when the draw runs
- **Verified to win: account check** - only accounts Verified by CCTools' account check can win this draw. A signed wallet alone does not pass it
- **Highest check to win** - only accounts that meet CCTools' highest check when the draw runs can win

A campaign with no rule lets any signed-in account join. A draw can also split its prizes into tiers by level: your level when you join sets your tier, and inside a tier every valid entry has the same odds. See [Getting Verified](/account/verified) for what Verified needs.

Past those rules, selection is random (draw), by finish order (first come, first served) or by position on the campaign's stated criterion (ranked winners). It is never by how much XP you have.

## How the draw works

The draw happens **after the campaign deadline passes**, never before. The process is:

1. We take everyone who completed all the required tasks and meets the campaign's rule
2. Winners are drawn **at random** from them
3. For higher-value prizes, basic integrity checks are applied first: for example, filtering out clusters of accounts sharing one IP address or sets of tasks all completed within the same few seconds (a sign of automation rather than a real person)

The goal is simple: real participants get a fair, random chance, and obvious abuse does not crowd them out.

## Claiming your reward and the 7-day window

Once winners are selected, they are notified through **two channels**: a public announcement (for example, on X) and a direct message where applicable. If a reward needs information from you (such as a party ID for an on-chain prize), you'll be asked for it.

Winners have a **7-day claim window** from the announcement to respond and claim. This window is **final and applies equally to everyone**. If a winner does not respond within 7 days, the prize is handled as the campaign's terms say.

Why the firm deadline? Winners who respond on time deserve certainty, and one window for everyone is the only fair way to treat people the same. If you completed a campaign you hope to win, check back within the week.

A prize you won and have not claimed yet is the first row of **Your record** on your Profile. The claim itself stays on the campaign page.
