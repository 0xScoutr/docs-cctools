## Contact Channels

**Contact form**: The best way to reach us is the contact form at **cctools.network/contact**. Choose what it is about (**Partnership**, **Press**, **Support**, **Data and API**, **List a project** or **Other**) and describe your issue.

**X**: @cantontools (DMs open for quick questions)

**Community**: Join our Telegram for community-powered support

When contacting support, include:
- Your CCTools email/username
- Description of the issue
- Screenshots or screen recordings
- Browser and device info
