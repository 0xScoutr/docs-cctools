The CCTools team is available through several channels. Discord is the fastest for real-time help; the feedback form is best for detailed bug reports or issues that need a paper trail. Choose the channel that fits your situation.

## Support channels

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Discord" icon="discord" href="https://discord.gg/Pm2YpafyxP">
    **Fastest response.** Join the CCTools Discord for real-time help from the team and community. The team monitors Discord daily and typically responds within hours during active periods. Best for urgent issues, quick questions, and checking on known outages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Feedback Form" icon="envelope" href="https://cctools.network/feedback">
    **For detailed reports.** Submit a report at [cctools.network/feedback](https://cctools.network/feedback) with full context — screenshots, wallet addresses, and steps to reproduce. Best for bug reports, data issues, and anything that needs investigation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Before contacting support

Checking these resources first can save you time — many issues are documented or already being tracked:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the FAQ">
    Visit the [FAQ at /community/faq](/community/faq) for answers to the most common questions about accounts, rewards, and platform features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the troubleshooting guides">
    If you're having a login problem, see [Login Issues](/troubleshooting/login-issues). For portfolio or balance problems, see [Portfolio Issues](/troubleshooting/portfolio-issues).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Discord #status channel">
    Before reporting an issue, check **#status** in the CCTools Discord. Known outages, indexer delays, and scheduled maintenance are posted there first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to include in your report

The more context you provide, the faster the team can investigate. When reaching out — via Discord or the feedback form — try to include:

| Detail | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| **Your username or registered email** | Identifies your account |
| **The page or feature affected** | Narrows down where the issue is occurring |
| **A description of the issue** | What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened |
| **Steps to reproduce** | Helps the team replicate the issue on their end |
| **A screenshot** | Visual context is often the fastest way to diagnose display or data issues |
| **Your wallet address** | Required for any portfolio, balance, or reward issue |

<Tip>
  Discord is the fastest way to get help. The community is active and the team monitors it daily — you'll often get an answer from another experienced user before the team even sees it.
</Tip>
