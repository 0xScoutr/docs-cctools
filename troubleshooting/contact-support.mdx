## Contact Channels

**Contact Form**: The best way to reach us is through the contact form at **cctools.network/contact**. Select a subject (Sponsored Listing, Partnership, General Inquiry, etc.) and describe your issue.

**Twitter/X**: @cantontools (DMs open for quick questions)

**Community**: Join our Telegram for community-powered support

When contacting support, include:
- Your CCTools email/username
- Description of the issue
- Screenshots or screen recordings
- Browser and device info
