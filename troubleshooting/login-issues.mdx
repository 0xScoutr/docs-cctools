## Ways to sign in

Click **Sign in** at the top right. You can sign in with your email and password, or with **Google**, **X** or **Discord** under **Sign in with**. Use the way you created the account with, or one you connected later in **Settings > Connected accounts**. If the page asks for a check, complete it, then sign in.

## Common messages

**"Invalid email or password."**
- Check the email and the password
- Forgot it? Use **Forgot password?** beside the password field, or **Reset your password** in the message: enter your email and we send a reset link. A link that is invalid or expired asks you to request a new one

**"Please confirm your email before signing in."**
- Open the confirmation link in your inbox, and check spam
- No message? Use **Send a new link** on the sign-in page

**"This account has been temporarily locked due to too many failed login attempts."**
- Check your email: the message we sent unlocks the account

**"Too many login attempts" or "Too many attempts"**
- Wait a while, then try again. Attempts are counted for each network and for each email

**A Google, X or Discord sign-in that did not finish**
- "Login was cancelled", "The login link expired or was already used" or "Login failed": try again, or use another way to sign in

## Two-factor sign-in

After your password, **Two-factor authentication** asks for a 6-digit code, from your authenticator app or sent to your email. When both are on, you can switch with **Use an email code instead** or **Use your authenticator app**.

- With the app, use the code it shows now: it changes every 30 seconds
- With email, use the newest code, since a new one replaces the one before it. **Send a new code** sends another after a short wait
- Lost your authenticator app? If email codes are on too, use an email code. If not, write to us (see below)

## Page loads but nothing happens

- Clear your browser cache and cookies for cctools.network
- Try a different browser
- Disable browser extensions that might interfere

## Still can't sign in?

Write to us at [cctools.network/contact](https://cctools.network/contact): choose **Support**, then **Account or sign-in**, and include:

- the email you sign in with
- the message you see
- your browser and device
- a screenshot, if you can
