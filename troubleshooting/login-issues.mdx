Most login issues on CCTools can be resolved in a few steps. The most common cause is a mismatch between the sign-in method you're using now and the one you used when you created your account — for example, trying email/password when you originally signed up with Google. Work through the accordion below to find your situation.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password">
    On the CCTools login page, click **Forgot Password** below the password field. Enter the email address associated with your account and submit the form. A password reset link will be sent to that address.

    If you don't receive the email within a few minutes:
    - Check your spam or junk folder
    - Add `noreply@cctools.network` to your contacts and try again
    - Make sure you're using the email address you registered with — not an alias

    If you originally signed up with a social login (Google, X, Discord), you may not have a password set. Try signing in with that social account instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving the verification email">
    Verification emails can sometimes be delayed or filtered by email providers.

    1. **Check spam and junk folders** — verification emails from `noreply@cctools.network` are occasionally caught by spam filters
    2. **Add the sender to your contacts** — add `noreply@cctools.network` and request a new verification email
    3. **Wait a few minutes** — delivery can take up to 5 minutes depending on your provider
    4. **Try a different email provider** — if you're using a work or school email, it may be blocking the domain

    If none of these work, contact support from the [Contact Support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My social login isn't working">
    If signing in with Google, X, or Discord is failing, try the following:

    - **Switch to email/password login** — if you set a password when registering, this will work independently of your social account
    - **Check your social account connection** — once logged in via email, go to **Settings → Profile → Connections** and verify that your social account is linked correctly
    - **Re-authorize the connection** — disconnect and reconnect the social account in Settings if the authorization token has expired
    - **Clear your browser cache and cookies** — stale auth state can cause social login flows to fail

    If your social account's email address has changed since you created your CCTools account, the social login may not match. Contact support to merge accounts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm locked out after too many failed attempts">
    After several failed login attempts, CCTools temporarily locks the account to protect against brute-force attacks.

    **Wait 15 minutes**, then try again with your correct credentials. The lockout clears automatically.

    If the lockout persists beyond 15 minutes, or if you didn't trigger it yourself, contact support immediately — this could indicate an unauthorized access attempt. See [Contact Support](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My 2FA code isn't working">
    Two-factor authentication codes are time-sensitive (TOTP codes expire every 30 seconds). Common causes:

    - **Device clock out of sync** — make sure your device's date and time are set to automatic/network time. Even a 30-second drift can invalidate TOTP codes
    - **Wrong authenticator app entry** — if you have multiple CCTools entries in your authenticator, try each one
    - **Email 2FA delay** — if you're using email-based 2FA, check your spam folder and wait up to 2 minutes for delivery

    **If you've lost access to your authenticator app:** Contact CCTools support via Discord or the [feedback form]("https://cctools.network/feedback"). You'll need to verify your identity before 2FA can be reset.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find my account">
    If the login page says your account doesn't exist:

    1. **Try all sign-in methods** — the account may have been created with a different method (Google vs. email, for example). Try each option on the login page
    2. **Check for typos in your email** — make sure you're entering the exact email address you registered with, including any dots or plus signs
    3. **Check if you have multiple email addresses** — try other email addresses you might have used at signup

    If you're certain you created an account but can't find it, reach out to support with any details you remember about how you signed up.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still stuck?

If none of the steps above resolve your issue, the CCTools support team can help. See [Contact Support](/troubleshooting/contact-support) for all available channels.
